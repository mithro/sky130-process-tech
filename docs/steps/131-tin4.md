## What this step is

`TIN4` deposits the liner of the via-2 plug: a thin titanium nitride
film sputtered over the whole wafer — across the
{ref}`NCAPOX4 <step-128>` cap oxide and down into every 0.20 µm via
opened at {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` — coating the oxide sidewalls and
the titanium–tungsten floor. The liner is described here as
{term}`IMP` TiN (ionised-metal-plasma physical vapour deposition, in
which the sputtered titanium is ionised and drawn into the hole by a
wafer bias[^rossnagel-1998]). SkyWater lists "Imp TiN" among its AMAT
PVD films,[^skw-01] and ionised PVD was the 200 mm-era way to coat the
floor of a hole this deep (inference). {ref}`TIN2 <step-109>` and
{ref}`TIN3 <step-120>` describe the same liner below. The film is
the nucleation surface for the tungsten of {ref}`WDEP4 <step-132>`,
the barrier that keeps WF₆ and its fluorine off the metal-2 cap and
off any aluminium an unlanded via has exposed, and the adhesion layer
for the plug; it is removed from the field, with the tungsten, at
{ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>`. Its thickness is not public; a 200 mm,
130 nm-era via liner is typically a few tens of nanometres on the
field and much less on the via floor (industry-typical
value[^txt-05][^rossnagel-1998]).

The hole is the deepest of the metal-level plug holes so far: 0.20 µm
wide and 0.42 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04] an {term}`aspect ratio`
of about 2.1:1, against 1.8:1 at via 1 and roughly 2:1 at the metal-1
contact. The local-interconnect contact filled at
{ref}`WDEP <step-099>` is far steeper — a 0.08 µm bottom under about
0.5 µm of dielectric, about 6:1.[^pdk-03] The via-2 floor is the top
of metal 2 — on the public description of the S8 process, 300 Å of
TiW over Al–0.5%Cu[^cyp-qtp-113005] — so, as at
{ref}`TIN3 <step-120>`, this is a TiN-on-TiW interface. The
PDK's `via2` is 3 410 mΩ,[^pdk-08] the liner and its interfaces
included.

## Step category

`TIN4` is a {ref}`Thin-film deposition <category-deposition>` step of
the *PVD* class; {ref}`TIN2 <step-109>` sets out the physics of
ionised PVD at length and {ref}`TIN3 <step-120>` what changes when the
liner lands on an aluminium-alloy level. Within SKY130 it is the
fourth IMP TiN liner, after {ref}`TI/TIN1 <step-097>`,
{ref}`TIN2 <step-109>` and {ref}`TIN3 <step-120>`;
{ref}`TIN5 <step-146>` follows at via 3. What
is specific to this instance is the aspect ratio: at 2.1:1 the
bottom coverage of a line-of-sight sputter would be poor, and the
directional flux of IMP — or of the collimated sputtering SkyWater
also lists[^skw-01] — is what makes a continuous film at the
floor-to-sidewall corner possible; and, on our reading, the metal
that will cover the finished plug, the thick metal 3 of
{ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>`, makes any plug recess or seam less
forgiving, since a 0.845 µm[^pdk-04] sputtered film is not what fills
a hole.

## Why this step exists

A tungsten via cannot be grown directly into the hole
{ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` leaves:

* **Nucleation and adhesion.** CVD tungsten nucleates poorly and
  adheres badly on oxide; the liner gives the WF₆ process a surface
  to start on.[^srinivas-1992] Ireland reviews the liner-plus-plug
  process for high-aspect-ratio holes,[^ireland-1997] and Wang et al.
  the optimisation of a Ti/TiN liner for a 0.35 µm tungsten
  plug.[^wang-1996-liner]
* **Barrier against the precursor.** WF₆ and the HF it releases attack
  the titanium of a Ti:W cap and the bare Al–Cu sidewall of an
  unlanded via; a continuous TiN film on floor and sidewalls keeps the
  fluorine off both — Wittmer's barrier role for refractory
  nitrides[^wittmer-1985] — and prevents the "volcano" defects of an
  unprotected via (industry experience[^txt-05]).
* **Coverage at the bottom of a 2:1 hole.** Ordinary sputtering
  delivers little metal to the floor of a hole above about 2:1; IMP
  makes the flux directional.[^rossnagel-1993][^rossnagel-1998] Hamaguchi
  and Rossnagel modelled the liner conformality it
  gives,[^hamaguchi-1996] and Mao and Hopwood built a deposition model
  for ionised PVD of TiN specifically.[^mao-2004] Applied Materials'
  patent on alternating IMP and conventional sputtering shows how
  sidewall and bottom coverage were traded.[^pat-imp-amat]
* **Via resistance and reliability.** The liner is in series with
  every `via2`,[^pdk-08] and the W/TiN/TiW interface is where a
  tungsten-plug via fails under electromigration.[^kwok-1990][^hu-1993]
  Bui et al. found the cap beneath the plug controls the via's
  lifetime,[^bui-1994] and Kawasaki and Hu modelled tungsten-plug via
  lifetimes.[^kawasaki-1996]

Without `TIN4` the tungsten of {ref}`WDEP4 <step-132>` would not stick
to the oxide, its precursor would attack the metal-2 cap, and the vias
would be voided, resistive or open.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic IMP TiN via-liner deposition for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's recipe is not public); the sequence is that
of {ref}`TIN3 <step-120>`.

1. **Cluster tool.** A multi-chamber PVD platform under one vacuum
   with degas, sputter pre-clean and IMP chambers; SkyWater lists
   "Sputter etch, degas" beside its PVD films,[^skw-01] and Applied
   Materials' Endura is the 200 mm-era example.[^amat-endura]
2. **Degas.** A vacuum bake to drive out the water the open vias have
   absorbed since the {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` clean — the first
   defence against the poisoned via, whose origin in dielectric
   outgassing Kobayakawa et al. and Romero et al.
   documented.[^kobayakawa-1991][^romero-1991]
3. **Sputter pre-clean.** A low-energy argon (or Ar/H₂) plasma etch
   removes the fluoride and oxide residue from the TiW floor, with
   care not to sputter oxide from the via edge onto the floor or to
   thin the 300 Å cap[^cyp-qtp-113005] (industry practice[^txt-05]);
   Boumerzoug et al. report a plasma treatment that removes the oxide
   on a TiN cap and lowers via resistance.[^boumerzoug-1997] In a
   2.1:1 hole the re-deposition of sputtered material onto the lower
   sidewall is more of a concern than at via 1 (inference from the
   geometry).
4. **IMP TiN deposition.** Reactive sputtering of a titanium target in
   Ar/N₂[^berg-2005] in an IMP chamber whose RF coil sustains a dense
   secondary plasma[^hopwood-1992] that ionises the titanium; a wafer
   bias draws the ions down the hole at tens of mTorr.[^rossnagel-1998]
   Collimated sputtering[^rossnagel-1991] is the older route, and
   SkyWater lists "Collimated Ti" beside "Imp TiN".[^skw-01]
5. **Thickness.** Not public; of the order of 10–30 nm on the field
   for a 130 nm-era via liner (industry-typical value[^txt-05]). The
   0.20 µm via[^pdk-periph] leaves more room for the film than the
   0.15 µm via 1, and a 0.42 µm depth[^pdk-04] needs a longer, more
   directional deposition to reach the floor.
6. **Optional treatment.** Some flows follow the liner with a short
   N₂/H₂ plasma or a rapid thermal step to densify the TiN; whether
   SKY130 does is not public.
7. **Metrology.** Sheet resistance by {term}`four-point probe` on
   monitors; thickness by X-ray fluorescence or reflectometry; stress
   by wafer bow; bottom and sidewall coverage by cross-section SEM
   during development; via-2 chain resistance later at {term}`e-test`.

## Resources required

* **Titanium sputter target** (high-purity Ti; SkyWater's filings name
  Honeywell Electronic Materials (2021 S-1 and fiscal 2023 10-K) and JX
  Metals (fiscal 2023 10-K) as sputter-target suppliers[^sec-01][^sec-02]).
* **Argon** (sputter and pre-clean) and **nitrogen** (reactive
  gas);[^berg-2005] possibly **hydrogen** in the pre-clean.
* **Chamber consumables** — shields, coil, clamp or electrostatic
  chuck parts — and **target changes** on a kilowatt-hour schedule.
* **Monitor wafers** for sheet resistance and particles.

## Open questions

* The liner thickness, whether the film is TiN alone or TiN over a
  thin Ti, the bias and pressure of the IMP process, and whether a
  post-deposition treatment is used are not public.
* Whether the via-2 liner uses the same recipe as the via-1 liner or
  a longer, more directional one for the 2.1:1 hole is not public.
* The PVD platform model is inferred from "AMAT PVD Metal" and the
  film list;[^skw-01] no public source names an Endura at SkyWater.
* The liner's share of the 3.4 Ω `via2`[^pdk-08] is not separated in
  any public source.

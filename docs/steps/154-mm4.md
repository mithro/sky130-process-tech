## What this step is

`MM4` is the metal-4 lithography. A resist is coated, exposed and
developed so that resist remains wherever the layout draws `met4` (GDS
71:20, "Metal 4"[^pdk-06]) and is cleared everywhere else; the exposed
metal is removed at {ref}`MM4E <step-155>`. As at
{ref}`MM3 <step-139>`, the resist is printed over a partly built
device: the metal-4 stack of {ref}`WTIAL4 <step-149>` has stayed blanket
while the second {term}`MiM capacitor` was built on it — the dielectric of
{ref}`CAPILD2 <step-150>`, the plate film of {ref}`CAPTIW2 <step-151>` and the
plate pattern of {ref}`CAP2M <step-152>` and {ref}`CAP2ME <step-153>`.
Every `cap2m` plate must end up inside a metal-4 shape, since metal 4 is
that capacitor's bottom electrode; in the PDK's stacked `cap_mim`
cross-section the same metal-4 pattern also carries a separate
"M4 (plate 1)" shape that links "M3 (plate 1)" to "M5 (plate 1)",
beside the "M4 (plate 2)" shape under `CAP2M`.[^pdk-07]

The PDK's mask table lists "Metal 4, MM4" once, flagged as used in
SKY130,[^pdk-05] and the minimum-CD table gives `MM4CD` and `MM4CDSP` of
0.3 µm, with a separate "Metal 4-Cu" entry for a copper
variant.[^pdk-03] The periphery rules give "Min width of met4" 0.300 µm
(m4.1) and "Min spacing between two met4" 0.300 µm (m4.2), 0.400 µm to
or from "huge_met4" features (m4.5a, m4.5b), a minimum area of
0.240 µm² (m4.4a), a via-3 enclosure of 0.065 µm (m4.3), a minimum
oxide {term}`pattern density` of 0.7 (m4.pd.1) checked in 700 µm windows
stepped by 70 µm, and a `cmm4 waffleDrop` fill check; the via-4 openings
that will land on metal 4 must be enclosed by it by 0.190 µm
(via4.4).[^pdk-periph] Rules flagged "CU" — m4.7, m4.11–m4.16, among
them a 10 µm maximum width, slotting, a 0.77 maximum density and a
separate 0.060 µm via-3 enclosure (m4.15) — apply,
on our reading of the flag, to the copper variant rather than to the
aluminium flow described here (inference).[^pdk-periph] The metal being
patterned is 0.845 µm on the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] with a sheet
resistance of 47 mΩ/sq (limits 38–56).[^pdk-07][^pdk-08]

One further use of this mask is public. The {term}`metal-fuse <metal fuse>` rules say that
the "SP8P*/SKY130P* (PLM) CADflow" uses "MM4 for Metal Fuse", and give
fuses a width of 0.800 µm and a length of 7.200 µm (mf.1, mf.2), with
spacings from the fuse centre to the surrounding fuse metal and to
metal 1, local interconnect, poly, tap, diffusion and n-well
(mf.4, mf.6–mf.11).[^pdk-periph] The fuse links are thus
printed by this mask along with the wiring (inference from the note).

## Step category

`MM4` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *line-and-space* type printed on metal, like
{ref}`MM1 <step-113>`, {ref}`MM2 <step-124>` and {ref}`MM3 <step-139>`,
which set out the imaging and reflectivity arguments. It is not a
critical layer in the resolution sense: at 0.30 µm on a 248 nm tool of
{term}`NA` 0.70 (ASML's PAS 5500/750E[^asml-750e]) the process
factor {term}`k₁ <k1>` is {math}`k_1 = 0.30 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.85`, and on an i-line
tool of NA 0.6 {math}`k_1 \approx 0.49` (our arithmetic), above the
production boundary the category page quotes.[^wiki-litho] SkyWater
lists "ASML I-line" and "ASML DUV" steppers and scanners;[^skw-01]
which prints metal 4 is not public, and either is plausible for a
0.3 µm line (inference; ASML describes older exposure tools that
"migrate to the lithography of choice for less critical
layers"[^asml-30]).

What is specific to this instance is the substrate, and it is the same
kind as at {ref}`MM3 <step-139>`: capped aluminium — the cap being TiW
or TiN, which the public record does not settle
({ref}`overview-metal-cap`) — under a thin
capacitor dielectric across most of the wafer, and capacitor plates of the
order of 0.1 µm high over the capacitors (our reading at
{ref}`CAPTIW2 <step-151>`) — two thin-film stacks with different
reflectivities (Brunner's {term}`swing-curve <swing curve>` analysis[^brunner-1991]).

## Why this step exists

Metal 4 is the second coarse-pitch routing level, the bottom plate of
every `cap2m` capacitor and, on the PDK's fuse note, the fuse level.
The mask fixes:

* **Line width and resistance.** At 0.3 µm and 47 mΩ/sq[^pdk-07] metal 4
  matches metal 3; Bohr's argument that interconnect limits
  performance[^bohr-1995] and Stamper, Fuselier and Tian's account of
  wiring RC delay[^stamper-1998] are why an aluminium back end puts its
  thick, wide levels on top. The space sets the gap
  {ref}`NILD6 <step-156>` must fill.
* **The bottom plate of the second capacitor.** Each `cap2m` plate
  printed at {ref}`CAP2M <step-152>` must lie inside a metal-4 shape;
  the PDK publishes no `cap2m` enclosure rule,[^pdk-periph] so the value
  is not known. In the Newport Fab patent the bottom plate is likewise
  patterned from the interconnect metal after the top plate is
  defined,[^pat-mim-newportfab] and Ng et al. review MiM integration in
  Al–Cu and copper back ends.[^ng-2005]
* **Overlay to two earlier layers.** Metal 4 must enclose the via-3
  plugs by 0.065 µm (m4.3)[^pdk-periph] and the `cap2m` plates by an
  unpublished margin; it is printed after a non-wiring mask, so whether
  `MM4` aligns to via-3 targets under the metal, to the `cap2m` pattern
  or to both decides which enclosure carries the larger error
  (inference; Levinson's book has a chapter on overlay[^levinson-2005]).
* **A landing pad for the large via 4.** The via-4 openings are 0.800 µm
  squares (via4.1) that metal 4 must enclose by 0.190 µm
  (via4.4),[^pdk-periph] so every via-4 landing needs a metal-4 pad at
  least 1.18 µm wide (our arithmetic) — four times the minimum line.
* **Reflectivity control on a mixed substrate.** The refractory cap is
  a partial anti-reflective layer, the role Rocke and Schneegans
  documented for a titanium-nitride cap and which a Ti:W cap plays in
  the same way (inference),[^rocke-1988] but the capacitor films change the
  reflectance locally; an organic {term}`BARC` levels the optical
  conditions and part-planarises the plate steps (industry
  practice;[^brunner-1991][^mack-2007] SKY130's scheme is not public).
* **Fill for the polish.** The `cmm4` waffle-drop fill and 0.7 minimum
  oxide density[^pdk-periph] exist for {ref}`CMPM4 <step-157>`; the
  assumptions table gives "Oxide Bias for MM4" ({term}`oxide bias`) 1.15, the same as MM3 and
  against 0.6 for MM1 and MM2.[^pdk-03]

Without `MM4` metal 4 would remain a sheet shorting every via-3 plug,
every second-level capacitor and every fuse together.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic metal-line lithography sequence for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public); {ref}`MM1 <step-113>` and
{ref}`MM3 <step-139>` describe the shared steps.

1. **Surface preparation.** The wafer comes from the
   {ref}`CAP2ME <step-153>` strip and clean; dehydration bake, and,
   as at {ref}`MM1 <step-113>`, no {term}`HMDS` prime: the surface is
   part refractory plate and part dielectric, and the step pages read
   the adhesion promoter as unnecessary over the metal (inference),
   then an organic {term}`BARC` whose thickness is chosen from the swing-curve
   minimum over the mixed surface.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive resist — chemically amplified for
   248 nm[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] or DNQ/novolac for i-line[^dammel-1993]
   — thick enough to survive the etch of the 0.845 µm stack;[^pdk-04]
   aluminium etches in Cl₂/BCl₃ only a few times faster than resist
   (industry-typical[^nojiri-2015]). The PDK's 1.14 µm "Photoresist
   thickness"[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule assumption.
3. **Exposure.** KrF or i-line with conventional or mild off-axis
   illumination; at {math}`k_1` 0.5–0.85 no phase shifting is needed and
   {term}`OPC`, if used, is modest (inference from the
   geometry[^wong-2001]). The `cmm4` fill[^pdk-periph] is on
   the reticle.
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB`, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH develop,
   rinse and dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To marks under the opaque metal — the via-3 level —
   read through the topography of the plugs in the sputtered film (our
   reading of industry practice; Levinson's book has a chapter on
   overlay[^levinson-2005]; SKY130's alignment tree is not public);
   whether the `cap2m` plate edges, which stand ~0.1 µm proud of the
   surface on this reference's reading, are also used as overlay
   targets is not public (inference that they could be).
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Line {term}`CD` by {term}`CD-SEM`;
   {term}`overlay` on the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class;[^skw-01] after-develop
   inspection for bridging and for resist defects over the plates and
   fuses; dose and focus tracked by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999]

  ACT/Lithius,[^tel-act] DNS, Sokudo).

## Resources required

* **Positive photoresist** ({ref}`lithography materials <material-lithography-materials>`; 248 nm chemically amplified or i-line
  DNQ/novolac); SkyWater's 2021 S-1 names Dow, JSR and Tokyo Ohka Kogyo as
  photoresist suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **Organic BARC**, **2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH developer** (the 2021 S-1
  names "Air Products & Chemicals, Inc., Moses Lake" for
  developer[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse solvents, {ref}`DI water <material-ultrapure-water>` and
  {ref}`nitrogen <material-process-gases>`.[^txt-02]

  with its `cmm4` fill and the fuse shapes.[^pdk-06][^pdk-periph]
* **Exposure-tool consumables** (KrF laser gas or mercury lamps).

## Open questions

* Whether `MM4` is exposed on an i-line or a DUV tool, with what resist,
  BARC and reticle type, is not public.
* The alignment tree — to via 3, to `cap2m`, or both — and the `cap2m`
  enclosure rules are not public; the PDK has no `cap2m` rule
  set.[^pdk-periph]
* That the CU-flagged metal-4 rules do not apply to the aluminium flow is
  our reading of the flag.[^pdk-periph]
* Whether fuses are drawn in metal 4 in SKY130 designs as published, and
  whether they need a separate opening later, is not public beyond the
  fuse note.[^pdk-periph]
* Whether the metal under the resist is 0.845 µm (the stack
  diagram[^pdk-04]) or 0.8 µm (the antenna table's P-flow
  entry[^pdk-03]) is not resolved; see {ref}`WTIAL4 <step-149>`.

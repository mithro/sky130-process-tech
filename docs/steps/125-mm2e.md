## What this step is

`MM2E` is the metal-2 etch. Through the resist lines of
{ref}`MM2 <step-124>` a chlorine plasma removes the whole
Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack of {ref}`TIAL12 <step-123>` — on the public
description of the S8 process, 300 Å of TiW over 3 200 Å of Al–0.5%Cu
over 100 Å of Ti[^cyp-qtp-113005] — stopping on the
{ref}`NCAPOX3 <step-117>` cap oxide and on the tops of the via-1
tungsten plugs of {ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>`, so that what remains is
the `met2` wiring (GDS 69:20[^pdk-06]) at 0.140 µm minimum width and
space.[^pdk-periph] Because chlorine-etched aluminium corrodes in air,
the etch is followed, in the same tool or immediately after, by a
passivation step, a resist strip and a wet clean; the step list used
in this reference shows no separate strip after `MM2E`, so on our
reading those belong to this step, as at {ref}`MM1E <step-114>`.

The etch is subtractive, and its geometry is metal 1's: 0.14 µm
spaces 0.36 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04] an {term}`aspect ratio`
above 2.5:1 for {ref}`NILD4 <step-126>` to fill. What differs is the
floor. Under metal 1 the etch stopped on a contact-level oxide with
0.17 µm plugs to 0.3 µm-deep contacts; here it stops on the via-1 cap
oxide, only 0.27 µm above the metal-1 lines,[^pdk-04] with 0.15 µm
tungsten plugs[^pdk-periph] whose tops the polish may have left
slightly recessed. An over-etch that digs into that oxide thins the
metal-1-to-metal-2 spacing; one that attacks a plug top exposed by a
misaligned line (the 0.055 µm enclosure of m2.4[^pdk-periph]) erodes
the via. SkyWater lists two metal etchers by name, "Lam 9600, Al,
TiW, TiN, Pt" and "Lam 2300 Versys, Al, TiW, TiN, Nb, Pt",[^skw-01]
whose material lists match the stack.

## Step category

`MM2E` is an {ref}`Etch <category-etch>` step of the *metal,
chlorine-chemistry* class — the category page's "Aluminium–copper
with Ti/TiN or Ti:W caps" entry — and the second of the five metal
etches. {ref}`MM1E <step-114>` sets out the chemistry at length:
aluminium etches spontaneously in chlorine, so {term}`anisotropy`
comes entirely from a sidewall passivation film, and the etch
products leave the lines liable to corrode.[^hess-1982][^nojiri-2015]
What is specific to metal 2 is that it is the first metal etch whose
floor contains tungsten *vias* rather than contacts. On the PDK's
definition — the ratio of interconnect area exposed to the plasma etch
to the gate area connected to it, taken layer by layer — its antenna
is the metal-2 area exposed to this etch that connects, through the
via, metal 1 and the contact below, to a gate, which is what the PDK's
antenna rules for metal 2 bound.[^pdk-11]

## Why this step exists

The etch turns the resist image into wires; its details decide:

* **Line width and profile.** The etched width is what the PDK's
  0.140 µm[^pdk-periph] describes; the "Min process bias 3s tolerance"
  of 0.032 µm in the assumptions table[^pdk-03] is the public trace of
  the litho-plus-etch bias budget. A vertical profile gives
  {ref}`NILD4 <step-126>` its best chance; AT&T's patent on tapered
  aluminium etching with CHF₃ and Cl₂ shows the alternative that
  older, non-planarised flows preferred.[^pat-taper-att]
* **Complete clearing through three films.** W etches in
  chlorine[^fischl-1987] and TiW in chlorine–fluorine
  mixtures,[^liu-2007-tiw] Al–Cu in
  Cl₂/BCl₃,[^schaible-1978][^hess-1982] but the copper chlorides are
  involatile and are removed only by ion bombardment, leaving
  copper-rich residues that seed corrosion.[^lee-1981] Abraham,
  Gabriel and Zheng compared etch chemistries for a TiN anti-reflective
  cap on a high-density metal etcher and the microloading between
  dense and isolated lines that results[^abraham-1997] — the
  metal-2 pattern, with its long parallel buses and isolated
  connections, is where microloading shows.
* **Corrosion.** Chlorine left in the sidewall polymer reacts with
  moisture to form HCl, which attacks aluminium galvanically at the
  copper precipitates;[^lee-1981] Wai and Ling describe how a
  production line prevents it through the post-etch and polymer-clean
  sequence.[^wai-2017]
* **Charging.** A long metal-2 line connected through a via, a
  metal-1 line and a contact to a gate collects current from the
  plasma while it is being etched; Fang and McVittie described the
  thin-oxide damage,[^fang-1992] and Hwang and Giapis the "notching"
  at the foot of lines from pattern-dependent charging.[^hwang-1997]
  The PDK's antenna rules[^pdk-11] address this kind of charging.

Without `MM2E` metal 2 would be a continuous sheet shorting every
via-1 plug together.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic aluminium-stack etch for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab
(SKY130's recipe is not public); the step-by-step chemistry is at
{ref}`MM1E <step-114>`.

1. **Chamber.** A high-density plasma metal etcher — inductively or
   transformer-coupled, with separate bias, a cooled electrostatic
   chuck and helium backside cooling — with integrated passivation
   and strip modules. Lam's TCP source[^pat-tcp-lam] and Applied
   Materials' DPS reactor[^pat-dps-amat] are the two 200 mm-era
   designs; Lam's 10-K describes its 9600/2300 metal etch
   lines.[^lam-10k]
2. **BARC and cap open.** The organic {term}`BARC` (if used) is opened
   in an O₂/N₂ or Cl₂-containing step, then the TiW cap is broken
   through with a chlorine/fluorine mixture.[^liu-2007-tiw]
3. **Main etch.** Cl₂/BCl₃ with N₂ (or CHF₃ or CH₄) at a few to some
   tens of mTorr; chlorine etches aluminium as AlCl₃, BCl₃ scavenges
   water and oxygen and breaks the native Al₂O₃, and nitrogen or a
   carbon source builds the sidewall film that makes the profile
   vertical.[^schaible-1978][^hess-1982][^chen-1989] Cooperberg,
   Vahedi and Gottscho's profile simulation shows how these knobs set
   the sidewall.[^cooperberg-2002] The 0.36 µm stack clears in well
   under a minute at the hundreds of nanometres per minute of the
   class (industry-typical[^txt-02]).
4. **Endpoint.** Optical emission on the atomic aluminium line near
   396 nm (or the AlCl band near 261 nm), which drops as the
   aluminium clears;[^bawolek-1987] the emission is strong on a metal
   level because the open area is large.
5. **Over-etch.** A timed step with reduced power and a more
   passivating chemistry to remove the Ti underlayer and stringers,
   with {term}`selectivity` to the cap oxide and to the tungsten plug
   tops; this is where notching and micro-trenching from charging
   appear.[^hwang-1997] On a via level the over-etch is bounded by
   the 0.27 µm of oxide[^pdk-04] beneath (inference).
6. **Passivation.** Without an air break, an H₂O-vapour or O₂/CF₄
   plasma that converts residual AlCl₃ on the sidewalls to oxide and
   fluoride and removes chlorine from the resist.[^nojiri-2015][^txt-02][^wai-2017]
7. **Strip and clean.** Oxygen or O₂/N₂ downstream plasma
   {term}`ash` — SkyWater lists Gasonics, Iridia and Mattson tools
   with N₂/O₂ and CF₄/H₂ options[^skw-01] — then a solvent or
   semi-aqueous polymer clean (EKC265/270-type solvents are on
   SkyWater's wet-bench list[^skw-01]) within a time limit of the
   etch, followed by DI rinse and dry.
8. **Metrology.** Line CD by {term}`CD-SEM` after etch; profile by
   cross-section SEM on monitors; metal-2 sheet resistance and
   serpentine/comb structures at {term}`e-test` (the PDK's
   125 mΩ/sq[^pdk-08]); via-chain resistance, now that the vias are
   capped by metal on both sides; corrosion inspection after a
   queue-time delay.

## Machines typically used

* **High-density metal etcher**, 200 mm: Lam TCP 9600 / 2300
  Versys,[^lam-10k] Applied Materials Centura DPS Metal,[^pat-dps-amat]
  TEL Unity ({ref}`category-etch`).
* **Integrated passivation and strip modules**; **downstream asher**
  (Gasonics, Mattson); **solvent wet bench**.
* **CD-SEM**, **cross-section SEM**, **e-test**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Lam 9600 and Lam 2300 Versys.** SkyWater lists "Lam 9600, Al,
  TiW, TiN, Pt" and "Lam 2300 Versys, Al, TiW, TiN, Nb, Pt" as its
  metal etchers.[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for the tools and for
  the material set; which runs metal 2 is not public (inference:
  the two tight-pitch levels, metal 1 and metal 2, would share the
  newer 2300 platform).
* **Strip — Gasonics PEP, Iridia, Mattson Aspen2; clean — Akrion Gamma
  wet bench and batch rotational tools with "EKS265, EKC270
  solvents".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence; assignment to
  this step is an inference.

## Resources required

* **Cl₂ and BCl₃**, **N₂** (and possibly CHF₃ or CH₄) for the etch;
  **CF₄ or SF₆** for the cap breakthrough; **Ar** and **He** (backside
  cooling) — industry practice;[^nojiri-2015] SkyWater names Cl₂ and
  CF₄ on its etchers.[^skw-01]
* **H₂O vapour or O₂/CF₄** for passivation; **O₂/N₂** and **H₂/N₂**
  for the ash.[^skw-01]
* **Amine or semi-aqueous polymer-removal solvent** (EKC265/270
  class[^skw-01]) and DI water.
* **Chamber consumables** — liners, coil windows, electrostatic chuck
  parts; **monitor wafers** with the metal stack; **chlorine
  abatement** on the exhaust.

  queue-time rules of `MM2E` are not public.

  consume, and how a plug top exposed by misalignment is protected,
  are not public.
* Which of the two listed Lam metal etchers runs metal 2 is
  inferred.[^skw-01]
* The step list used in this reference has no separate strip step
  after `MM2E`; this page treats the resist strip and polymer
  clean as part of the etch step.

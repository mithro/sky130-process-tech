## What this step is

`PDM` is the last lithography step of the flow. A resist is coated
over the passivation nitride of {ref}`NTSD <step-167>`, exposed through
the pad {term}`reticle` and developed, so that openings stand over
every {term}`bond pad`, probe pad and test pad; the {ref}`PDME <step-169>` etch then cuts
the nitride and the oxide beneath it down to the metal-5 pad metal.
The PDK is explicit about the layer: `pad` (GDS 76:20) is "Passivation
cut (opening over pads)", its mask layer `cpdm` (37:0) is "Pad mask",
and a `padCenter` marker (81:20) and an `areaid.fe` identifier ("Pads in
padframe identifier") accompany it.[^pdk-06] The periphery rule table's
function line for the layer is "Opens the passivation".[^pdk-periph]
The process stack diagram draws the opening as a "glass cut" through
the "TOPNIT" nitride and "TOPOX" oxide over `metal5`, with both films
continuing over the edge of the metal around it.[^pdk-04]

The mask table lists two variants, "Pad (scribe protect)" and "Pad
(scribe unprotect)", both with the acronym `PDM`, and flags only the
first as used in SKY130.[^pdk-05] The minimum-CD table gives "Pad
(scribe protect)" a `PDMCD` of 2 µm and a `PDMCDSP` of
1.27 µm;[^pdk-03] the rules give a minimum spacing of `pad` to `pad` of
1.270 µm (pad.2) and a "Max area of hugePad NOT top_metal" of
30000 µm² (pad.3).[^pdk-periph] The PDK
does not explain the two variants; we read "scribe protect" as the
version in which the passivation is left over the
{term}`scribe lanes <scribe line>` except
where pads are drawn (inference from the names and the flag).

**A real pad.** SkyWater's I/O library shows what the layer looks like
on a product pad. In the `sky130_fd_io__top_gpiov2` GPIO cell (80 µm ×
200 µm in its LEF abstract) the `pad` opening is a single octagon — a
60 µm × 70 µm rectangle with 4.95 µm chamfered corners — over a
similarly chamfered 65.4 µm × 75.4 µm metal-5 pad, so the metal extends
2.7 µm beyond the opening on every side, including the chamfers, and
0.8 µm via-4 squares connect the pad to metal 4 below (our reading of
the published GDS and LEF).[^pdk-io-gpiov2] The pad openings are
therefore large features; the 2 µm minimum CD[^pdk-03] and the 1.270 µm
`pad` spacing (pad.2[^pdk-periph]) matter for small openings such as
test-structure pads in the frame (inference).

## Step category

`PDM` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *non-critical, large-feature* type. At 2 µm features on
1.27 µm spaces[^pdk-03] the process factor {term}`k₁ <k1>` on an i-line
tool of {term}`NA` 0.6 is
{math}`k_1 = 1.27 \times 0.6 / 0.365 \approx 2.1` (our arithmetic), well
above any resolution limit, and the ITRS 2001 lithography chapter
assigns such layers to the cheaper exposure tools;[^itrs-03] we infer an
i-line exposure with a DNQ/novolak resist. What is specific to this
instance is the substrate: a transparent nitride-over-oxide stack of
the order of 0.6–1 µm (the PDK's 0.09 µm TOPOX and 0.54 µm TOPNIT,[^pdk-04]
or the 1000 Å oxide and 7000–9000 Å nitride of Cypress reports for other
processes at the fab[^cyp-qtp-123907][^cyp-qtp-113005]) over highly
reflective aluminium, on the 1.26 µm-tall metal-5 topography.[^pdk-04]
It is also the only mask printed after the passivation nitride, so any rework
strips resist from the finished, sealed die.

## Why this step exists

* **Access to the circuit.** Bond wires, probe needles and bumps must
  touch metal; the passivation must be opened over the pads and nowhere
  else. The opening's size and its enclosure by the pad metal (2.7 µm
  per side in the GPIO cell[^pdk-io-gpiov2]) decide how much of the pad
  is usable for bonding and how far the passivation edge sits from the
  metal edge, where it protects the pad's sides from corrosion (industry
  practice;[^txt-05] Comizzoli et al. review corrosion of exposed
  metallisation[^comizzoli-1986]).
* **Probe marks and pad damage.** The opening must be large enough for
  probe needles and a wire bond to land without touching the nitride
  edge; Hunter et al. used wire bonding to study how wafer probing
  cracks the oxide under aluminium pads.[^hunter-2012] SkyWater lists
  parametric and sort testers,[^skw-01] whose needles land in these
  openings at {ref}`HPETEST <step-171>` and in later sort (inference).
* **Test pads in the scribe.** The PDK's e-test modules are placed
  outside the dice ("Die must not overlap areaid.mt"),[^pdk-periph] and a
  TSMC patent describes the usual arrangement of "testlines in the
  scribe line area between adjacent wafer dies" contacted through probe
  pads;[^pat-testline-tsmc] their pads, too, are opened by this mask
  (inference).
* **Other openings.** The PDK places a "Metal fuse target" layer on the
  same GDS layer number (76:44) as `pad`;[^pdk-06] a Vanguard patent
  shows how "bonding pad via openings and the final fuse access opening
  can be accomplished by a single mask".[^pat-fuse-vanguard] Whether the
  SKY130 pad mask also opens fuse windows is not public.

Without `PDM` the finished circuit would be sealed under nitride with no
way to connect to it.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic pad-opening lithography step for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab with an aluminium top metal (SKY130's is not public):

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake and {term}`HMDS` prime on
   the nitride.
2. **Resist coat.** A positive i-line DNQ/novolak
   resist[^dammel-1993][^reichmanis-1989] thick enough to cover the
   metal-5 steps and to survive an etch through the nitride and oxide
   (inference; the thickness is not public), with
   thickness chosen against the {term}`swing curve` of a transparent
   nitride/oxide stack over aluminium (Brunner's analysis[^brunner-1991];
   Mack treats coating over topography[^mack-2007]).
3. **Exposure.** i-line {term}`stepper`, conventional illumination, a binary
   reticle generated from `pad` (mask layer `cpdm` 37:0[^pdk-06]).
4. **Post-exposure bake, develop, hard bake.** 0.26 N TMAH develop,
   rinse and dry; a hard bake to stiffen the resist for the
   etch.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the metal-5 pattern; the enclosure of the opening
   by the pad metal (2.7 µm in the GPIO cell[^pdk-io-gpiov2]) is a loose
   {term}`overlay` budget (Levinson treats overlay budgets[^levinson-2005]).
6. **Inspection.** Opening size and resist residue in the openings
   (scum left in a pad becomes an unetched film on the bond surface);
   overlay on the KLA class SkyWater lists.[^skw-01]

## Machines typically used

* **i-line stepper**, 200 mm: ASML PAS 5500 i-line
  models,[^asml-30][^chiphistory-pas5500] Nikon NSR-i, Canon FPA
  ({ref}`category-lithography`).
* **Coat/develop track** (TEL ACT/Lithius,[^tel-act] DNS, Sokudo).
* **Optical inspection**, **overlay tool**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **ASML I-line stepper / I-line scanner.** SkyWater lists both.[^skw-01]
  Strength: **strong** for existence; the assignment of `PDM` to i-line
  is an **inference** from the 2 µm / 1.27 µm rules.[^pdk-03]
* **Tracks — DNS 80B, Sokudo RF3, TEL ProZ Lithius.**[^skw-01] Strength:
  strong for existence.
* **Overlay — KLA 5200/5300/Archer.**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for
  existence.

## Resources required

* **i-line positive photoresist** (DNQ/novolak); SkyWater's S-1 names
  Dow, JSR and Tokyo Ohka Kogyo as photoresist suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **HMDS**, **0.26 N TMAH developer**, edge-bead remover, rinse
  solvents, DI water and nitrogen.[^txt-02]
* **The PDM reticle** ("Pad (scribe protect)"[^pdk-05]), generated from
  the drawn `pad` layer (76:20).[^pdk-06]
* **Mercury-lamp consumables** for the i-line tool.

## Open questions

* What "scribe protect" and "scribe unprotect" mean in the mask
  table[^pdk-05] is not public; our reading is an inference.
* The resist, its thickness and the exposure tool are not public.
* Whether the pad mask also opens laser-fuse windows over the metal-4
  fuses (the `target` layer shares GDS layer 76 with `pad`[^pdk-06]) is
  not public.
* The PDK's periphery rules give no minimum enclosure of `pad` by
  metal 5 (the only pad enclosure rule we found is m4.16, "Min enclosure
  of pad by met4", 0.850 µm, flagged "CU"[^pdk-periph]); the 2.7 µm of the
  GPIO cell[^pdk-io-gpiov2] is one layout, not a rule.

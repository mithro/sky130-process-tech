## What this step is

`MM3E` is the metal-3 etch, and with it the step that turns the
blanket {term}`MiM <MiM capacitor>` sandwich into finished capacitors. Through the resist of
{ref}`MM3 <step-139>` a chlorine plasma removes everything down to the
via-2 level: first whatever remains of the {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>`
dielectric outside the capacitor plates (on our reading of
{ref}`CAPME <step-138>`, most of its thickness), then the metal-3
stack of {ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>` — a TiW cap, a thick Al–Cu layer
and a thin refractory bottom layer — stopping on the
{ref}`NCAPOX4 <step-128>` cap oxide and on the tops of the via-2
tungsten plugs of {ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>`. What remains is the `met3`
wiring (GDS 70:20[^pdk-06]) at 0.300 µm minimum width and space (m3.1,
m3.2[^pdk-periph]); under each `capm` plate, protected by the resist,
the stack is left whole, so that every capacitor now has its own
bottom plate — the "M3 (plate 1)" of the PDK's `cap_mim`
cross-section.[^pdk-07] As at the lower metal etches, a passivation
step, resist strip and wet clean follow without delay; this reference
treats them as part of this step, as at {ref}`MM2E <step-125>`.

**How thick is the metal?** The public record gives several numbers and
does not reconcile them. The PDK's process stack diagram labels
`met3` 0.845 µm,[^pdk-04] and its antenna-rule table uses 0.85 µm for
"S8T* other than S8TM*" and 0.8 µm for the S8P, S8Q and SP8T
flows.[^pdk-03] The Cypress qualification report for the S8TNV-5R
technology at Bloomington gives "Metal 3: 150A Ti / 7200A Al-0.5%Cu /
300A TiW", which sums to 7 650 Å (0.765 µm), in a process whose
"Number of Metal Layers" is "3 Metal";[^cyp-qtp-113005] and the 2014
report on the S8DI metal-stack change, also a three-metal process,
gives "Metal 3: 500A TiW/21,250A Al 0.5% Cu/300A TiW", 2.2 µm.[^cyp-qtp-123907]
In both Cypress flows metal 3 is the *top* metal, whereas in the
five-metal SKY130 flow it is an intermediate level. A third, older
Bloomington report, for the 0.18 µm "Triple Metal" R7FT-3R derivative,
gives "Metal 3: 150Å Ti / 8,000Å Al / 300Å TiW" — a sum of exactly
0.845 µm[^cyp-qtp-014807] — which shows that an 8 000 Å Al metal 3 of
the PDK's thickness has existed in the same fab; whether the SKY130
flow uses such a stack is not stated anywhere public. We quote the
numbers without resolving them and use the PDK's 0.845 µm for
geometry below (see *Open questions*). The PDK's electrical data do
not decide it: `RSM3` is 47 mΩ/sq (limits 38–56),[^pdk-07][^pdk-08]
which for 0.72–0.80 µm of Al–Cu implies a resistivity of 3.4–3.8 µΩ·cm,
within the range expected for sputtered Al–0.5%Cu (our arithmetic;
typical industry value[^txt-02]).

## Step category

`MM3E` is an {ref}`Etch <category-etch>` step of the *metal,
chlorine-chemistry* class — the category page's "Aluminium–copper
with Ti/TiN or Ti:W caps" entry — and the third of the five metal
etches. {ref}`MM1E <step-114>` sets out the chemistry at length:
aluminium etches spontaneously in chlorine, so {term}`anisotropy`
comes from a sidewall passivation film, and the etch products leave
the lines liable to corrode.[^hess-1982][^nojiri-2015] Three things
are specific to this instance. The stack is more than twice as thick
as at metals 1 and 2 (0.845 µm against 0.36 µm[^pdk-04]), so the
etch is longer, the resist budget tighter and the copper residue
heavier. The spaces are wider but deeper — 0.30 µm by 0.845 µm, an
{term}`aspect ratio` of about 2.8:1 (our arithmetic from the PDK
values[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04]). And the top of the stack is not bare
TiW but, outside the plates, TiW under the thin capacitor dielectric
(for an oxynitride, as assumed at {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>`, a film
which a chlorine chemistry etches slowly and which must be broken
through first), which makes this one of the two metal etches in the
flow that begin with a dielectric (with {ref}`MM4E <step-155>`).

## Why this step exists

The etch makes the wiring and, in the same pattern, completes the
capacitors:

* **Line width and profile in a thick film.** The etched width is
  what the PDK's 0.300 µm[^pdk-periph] describes, and the "Min process
  bias 3s tolerance" of 0.032 µm[^pdk-03] is a public trace of the
  litho-plus-etch bias budget; over 0.845 µm of height a small
  sidewall angle becomes a large width difference between the top
  and bottom of the line. A vertical profile gives
  {ref}`NILD5 <step-141>` its best chance of filling a 2.8:1 gap;
  Cooperberg, Vahedi and Gottscho show how the Cl₂/BCl₃ balance and
  the passivant set the sidewall,[^cooperberg-2002] and AT&T's patent
  on tapered aluminium etching shows the opposite choice older flows
  made.[^pat-taper-att]
* **Breaking through the capacitor dielectric.** Wherever the
  dielectric of {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>` remains on the metal, a
  chlorine plasma that etches aluminium readily makes little
  progress on it; a fluorine-containing breakthrough step, of the
  kind used to open a TiW cap (Liu and Kuo etched TiW in
  CF₄/Cl₂[^liu-2007-tiw]) or a TiN cap,[^abraham-1997]
  removes it and the cap together. Any dielectric left in a space
  masks the metal beneath and leaves a stringer that shorts adjacent
  lines (inference from the stack; the Newport Fab patent patterns
  its bottom-plate metal only after removing the dielectric outside
  the capacitor[^pat-mim-newportfab]).
* **Complete clearing of a thick Al–Cu film.** W etches in
  chlorine[^fischl-1987] and TiW in chlorine–fluorine
  mixtures,[^liu-2007-tiw] Al–Cu in
  Cl₂/BCl₃,[^schaible-1978][^hess-1982] but copper chlorides are
  involatile and are removed only by ion bombardment, leaving
  copper-rich residues that seed corrosion;[^lee-1981] a thicker film
  holds more copper. Abraham, Gabriel and Zheng describe the
  {term}`microloading <loading effect>` between dense and isolated features on a high-density
  metal etcher,[^abraham-1997] which grows with etch depth.
* **Corrosion.** Chlorine in the sidewall polymer and the resist
  forms HCl with moisture and attacks the aluminium at copper
  precipitates;[^lee-1981] Wai and Ling describe the post-etch and
  polymer-clean sequence that prevents it.[^wai-2017]
* **Charging — now of a capacitor as well as of gates.** A long
  metal-3 line connected through vias and lower metals to a gate
  collects plasma current while it is being isolated
  ({term}`plasma charging`); Fang and
  McVittie described the thin-oxide damage[^fang-1992] and Hwang and
  Giapis the notching at the line foot.[^hwang-1997] Here a MiM
  capacitor's bottom plate is also being cut free under a top plate
  that is connected to nothing until via 3 is made, so the thin
  dielectric can be charged across (inference from the geometry;
  Wang, Ackaert et al. showed how plasma-charging damage of floating
  MiM capacitors depends on the antenna areas connected to each
  plate[^wang-2004-mim]). The PDK's antenna rules[^pdk-11] address
  this kind of charging.

Without `MM3E` metal 3 would remain a sheet shorting every via-2 plug
and every capacitor together.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic thick aluminium-stack etch for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's recipe is not public); the step-by-step
chemistry is at {ref}`MM1E <step-114>`.

1. **Chamber.** A high-density plasma metal etcher —
   transformer- or inductively coupled, with independent bias, a
   cooled {term}`electrostatic chuck` and helium backside cooling — with
   integrated passivation and strip modules. Lam's {term}`TCP`
   source[^pat-tcp-lam] and Applied Materials' DPS reactor[^pat-dps-amat]
   are the two 200 mm-era designs; Lam's 10-K describes its 9600/2300
   metal etch lines.[^lam-10k]
2. **BARC open and dielectric breakthrough.** The organic
   {term}`BARC` of {ref}`MM3 <step-139>` is opened in an O₂/N₂ or
   Cl₂-containing step; the capacitor dielectric and the TiW cap are then
   broken through with a fluorine-bearing mixture (CF₄ or CHF₃ with
   Cl₂), as for the cap alone at the lower levels
   (industry practice;[^liu-2007-tiw][^abraham-1997] fluorocarbon
   etching of silicon oxide and nitride films is set out by Oehrlein
   et al.[^oehrlein-1994b]).
3. **Main etch.** Cl₂/BCl₃ with N₂ (or CHF₃) at a few to tens of
   mTorr; chlorine removes aluminium as AlCl₃, BCl₃ scavenges oxygen
   and breaks the native oxide, and the nitrogen or carbon species
   build the sidewall film.[^schaible-1978][^hess-1982][^chen-1989]
   At the hundreds of nanometres per minute of the class
   (industry-typical[^txt-02]) the 0.845 µm stack[^pdk-04] clears in
   one to two minutes — two to three times longer than at metals 1
   and 2, with a correspondingly thicker resist
   ({ref}`MM3 <step-139>`).
4. **Endpoint.** Optical emission on the atomic aluminium line near
   396 nm or the AlCl band, which falls as the aluminium
   clears;[^bawolek-1987] the open area of a metal level is large, so
   the signal is strong.
5. **Over-etch.** A timed, lower-power, more passivating step to
   remove the bottom Ti (or TiW) and stringers at plug edges and in
   the narrowest spaces, with {term}`selectivity` to the
   {ref}`NCAPOX4 <step-128>` oxide and the tungsten plug tops;
   {term}`notching` and micro-trenching from charging appear
   here.[^hwang-1997] The over-etch is bounded by the 0.42 µm of via-2
   oxide beneath[^pdk-04] (inference).
6. **Passivation.** Without an air break, an H₂O-vapour or O₂/CF₄
   plasma that converts residual chlorides on the sidewalls and
   strips chlorine from the resist.[^nojiri-2015][^wai-2017]
7. **Strip and clean.** Downstream O₂/N₂ {term}`ash` — SkyWater lists
   GaSonics, Iridia and Mattson strippers with N₂/O₂, CF₄ and H₂/N₂
   options[^skw-01] — then a solvent or semi-aqueous polymer clean
   (the "EKS265, EKC270 solvents" on SkyWater's wet-bench
   list[^skw-01]) within a queue-time limit, which must also be benign
   to the exposed plate edges and the dielectric under them
   (inference); DI rinse and dry.
8. **Metrology.** Line CD by {term}`CD-SEM`; profile by
   cross-section SEM on monitors; metal-3 sheet resistance and
   comb/serpentine structures at {term}`e-test` against the PDK's
   47 mΩ/sq;[^pdk-07] capacitance, leakage and breakdown of the now
   separate MiM capacitors against `CMIMA` and `CMIMP`;[^pdk-07]
   corrosion inspection after a delay.

## Machines typically used

* **High-density metal etcher**, 200 mm: Lam TCP 9600 / 2300
  Versys,[^lam-10k] Applied Materials Centura DPS Metal,[^pat-dps-amat]
  TEL Unity ({ref}`category-etch`).
* **Integrated passivation and strip modules**; **downstream asher**;
  **solvent wet bench**.
* **CD-SEM**, **cross-section SEM**, **e-test** for lines and
  capacitors.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Lam 9600 or Lam 2300 Versys.** SkyWater lists "Lam 9600, Al, TiW,
  TiN, Pt" and "Lam 2300 Versys, Al, TiW, TiN, Nb, Pt" as its metal
  etchers.[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for the tools and the
  material set; which runs metal 3 is not public (inference: the
  coarser, thicker level is a natural load for the older 9600).
* **Strip — GaSonics PEP, Iridia, Mattson Aspen II; clean — batch
  rotational tools with "EKS265, EKC270 solvents".**[^skw-01]
  Strength: strong for existence; assignment is an inference.

## Resources required

* **Cl₂ and BCl₃**, **N₂** (and possibly CHF₃) for the main etch;
  **CF₄ or CHF₃** for the dielectric and cap breakthrough; **Ar**;
  **He** for backside cooling (industry practice;[^nojiri-2015]
  SkyWater lists no gases for its metal etchers, but names Cl₂, CF₄
  and CHF₃ on its other etchers[^skw-01]).
* **H₂O vapour or O₂/CF₄** for passivation; **O₂/N₂** for the ash.[^skw-01]
* **Amine or semi-aqueous polymer-removal solvent** and DI water.[^skw-01]
* **Chamber consumables**, replaced more often for a thick metal;
  **monitor wafers** with the full metal-3 stack; **chlorine
  abatement**.

## Open questions

* **Metal-3 thickness.** The Cypress S8TNV report's metal 3 sums to
  0.765 µm[^cyp-qtp-113005] and the PDK's stack diagram labels
  0.845 µm;[^pdk-04] the antenna table's 0.8 and 0.85 µm[^pdk-03] sit
  between, and a 0.18 µm Fab 4 derivative's 150 Å Ti / 8 000 Å Al /
  300 Å TiW metal 3 sums to exactly 0.845 µm.[^cyp-qtp-014807] Whether
  the SKY130 metal-3 Al–Cu is 8 000 Å rather than the S8TNV report's
  7 200 Å, whether the PDK value was carried over from another flow,
  or whether it includes another film, is not public; we have not
  resolved it.
* How much of the {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>` dielectric remains outside
  the plates, and how the breakthrough step is set, depend on
  {ref}`CAPME <step-138>` and are not public.
* The chemistry, endpoint, over-etch, passivation and queue-time
  rules of `MM3E`, and which of the two Lam etchers runs it, are not
  public.
* Whether the SKY130 metal-3 bottom layer is Ti (as in the S8TNV
  report[^cyp-qtp-113005]) or TiW (as in the S8DI report[^cyp-qtp-123907])
  is discussed, unresolved, at {ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>`.
* This page treats the resist strip and polymer clean as part of the
  etch.

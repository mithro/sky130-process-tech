## What this step is

`TIAL12` deposits the metal-2 film stack. Onto the polished cap oxide
and tungsten via plugs left by {ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>` a sputtering
cluster tool lays down — in one vacuum sequence, on our reading — the
same kind of sandwich as at metal 1: a thin refractory bottom layer,
the aluminium–copper alloy that carries the current, and a thin
refractory cap. The stack is blanket; it becomes wiring only
when {ref}`MM2 <step-124>` and {ref}`MM2E <step-125>` pattern and etch
it into the `met2` layer (GDS 69:20, "Metal 2").[^pdk-06]

The composition has the same public source as metal 1. The Cypress
qualification report for a 64 K nvSRAM family on "S8TNV-5R"
technology at the Bloomington fab, design rule "0.13 µm", gives
"Metal 2: 100A Ti / 3200A Al -0.5%Cu / 300A TiW" — identical to its
metal 1 — and a thicker "Metal 3: 150A Ti / 7200A Al -0.5%Cu / 300A
TiW".[^cyp-qtp-113005] The three films sum to 3 600 Å, the 0.36 µm
the PDK's stack diagram labels for `met2`,[^pdk-04] and close to the
0.35 µm "Metal 2 thickness for antenna ratio calculations" of its
assumptions table;[^pdk-03] the extraction tables give metal 2 the
same 125 mΩ/sq as metal 1.[^pdk-08] Cypress's 2014 report on the
"Metal Stack Change from Ti/AlCu/TiW to Ti/TiN/AlCu/Ti/TiN in CMI
Fab 4" gives, for its S8DI technology, the new metal 2 as "150A
Ti/250A TiN/3200A Al 0.5% Cu/90A Ti/500A TiN", again identical to its
metal 1,[^cyp-qtp-123907] and its notification announces the move of
the fab's 130 nm SONOS back end from "Titanium Tungsten (TiW) based
metal stack" to a "Titanium Nitride (TiN) based metal
stack".[^cyp-pin145273] Metal 2 is one of the levels that change
reaches on every reading of its scope. As at {ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>`,
this page describes the Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack because the PDK's 0.36 µm
matches its 3 600 Å sum exactly and not the 4 190 Å of the TiN version
(our arithmetic), and marks that as an inference; SkyWater's PVD film
list ("TiW", "ESC TiN", "Aluminum both pure and Cu doped", "Collimated
Ti"[^skw-01]) covers both and settles nothing. The evidence on both
sides is set out under {ref}`overview-metal-cap`.

**On the bottom titanium.** The step list used in this reference
labels this step "Al Cu 2/TiW deposition", as it does the upper levels
({ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>`, {ref}`WTIAL4 <step-149>` and
{ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`), and does not explain the label; this page's
title and the steps index normalise the spelling to "AlCu" without the
space. The label is not taken here as a description of the stack. We describe a
titanium underlayer as present because the Cypress reports list it
for metal 2 as for metal 1[^cyp-qtp-113005] (inference).

## Step category

`TIAL12` is a {ref}`Thin-film deposition <category-deposition>` step
of the *PVD, multi-layer metal* class; {ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>` sets
out the sputtering of Ti, Al–Cu and Ti:W films and the reasons for
each layer at length, and this page dwells on what is different one
level up. Two things are. The surface is now a via level rather than
a contact level: the tungsten plugs the stack lands on are 0.15 µm
squares[^pdk-periph] whose tops the {ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>` polish
may have recessed, and the metal-1 lines beneath are only 0.27 µm
below.[^pdk-04] And the lines that will be cut from this stack are
bounded at *both* ends by tungsten — vias below at
{ref}`WDEP3 <step-121>` and, later, vias above at
{ref}`WDEP4 <step-132>` — which changes their electromigration
behaviour in a way the metal-1 page only anticipates.

## Why this step exists

Metal 2 is the second general wiring level, and in a standard-cell
design the first that runs the length of a block; the flow needs it,
and it needs each film for the reasons given at
{ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>`. The via-level additions:

* **Al–Cu between tungsten studs.** Tungsten blocks aluminium
  diffusion, so each metal-2 segment between two vias is a closed
  system under electromigration: aluminium piles up at the anode end
  and depletes at the cathode end until the stress gradient balances
  the electron wind, the mechanism Blech and Herring described and
  Blech quantified as a critical length below which a segment does
  not fail.[^blech-1976][^blech-herring-1976][^blech-1998] Filippi,
  Biery and Wood showed this "short-length effect" in aluminium
  metallisation with tungsten barriers,[^filippi-1993] and Kwok et al.
  and Hu, Small and Ho studied the two-level Al–Cu/W structures in
  which the stud is the flux-divergence point.[^kwok-1990][^hu-1993]
  The half per cent of copper still matters: it slows the aluminium
  diffusion that the stud cannot stop,[^ames-1970] and Nix and Arzt
  describe void nucleation and growth in such lines.[^nix-1992]
* **Ti underlayer.** It wets the plug tops and the oxide, gives the
  Al–Cu its (111) texture,[^kamoshida-1997][^knorr-1996] and reacts to
  a TiAl₃ shunt that keeps a segment conducting after a void opens
  (industry practice; Domenicucci et al. measured Ti–AlCu–Ti
  lifetimes over two-level tungsten stud structures[^domenicucci-1996]).
  On a via level it also
  bridges any recess in the plug tops; a thin collimated or ionised
  film is the usual choice.[^rossnagel-1991][^rossnagel-1998]
* **The cap.** Whichever refractory film it is
  ({ref}`overview-metal-cap`), as at metal 1 it is the anti-reflective
  and hard surface for the {ref}`MM2 <step-124>` exposure and etch — Chen,
  Chen and Tsou describe the equivalent role of a sputtered TiN cap
  in back-end lithography[^chen-1991-tin] — a hillock and corrosion
  barrier,[^rocke-1988][^ghate-1978][^olowolafe-1985] and the landing
  pad the {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` via etch stops on. May, and Martin
  and McPherson, characterised the electromigration of vias in
  Ti:W/Al–Cu multilayer metallisation,[^may-1991][^martin-1989] and
  Armstrong the TiW + Al/Cu system as a whole;[^armstrong-1991]
  Georgiou, Baker and Eshraghi related sputtered-TiW deposition
  conditions to its barrier properties,[^georgiou-1991] and
  Hartsough measured the resistivity of bias-sputtered TiW
  films,[^hartsough-1979] which is why a 300 Å cap adds little to the
  stack's conductance.
* **Wiring delay.** Stamper, Fuselier and Tian set out the RC-delay
  issues of aluminium wiring at the sub-0.25 µm generation;[^stamper-1998]
  a 0.36 µm Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack at 125 mΩ/sq[^pdk-04][^pdk-08] on a
  0.28 µm pitch[^pdk-periph] is the compromise this level strikes.

Without `TIAL12` there is no metal 2; the via-1 plugs would end in
air.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic Ti/Al–Cu/TiW deposition for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era
fab (SKY130's recipe is not public beyond the layer thicknesses in the
Cypress reports[^cyp-qtp-113005]); the film-by-film account is at
{ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>`.

1. **Cluster tool.** A multi-chamber PVD platform — SkyWater's "AMAT
   PVD Metal" with "Sputter etch, degas"[^skw-01] — so that degas,
   pre-clean and the three depositions happen without an air break;
   the Endura is the 200 mm-era Applied Materials
   platform.[^amat-endura]
2. **Degas and pre-clean.** A vacuum bake to drive moisture from the
   polished cap oxide, then a light argon sputter etch to remove the
   tungsten oxide from the plug tops and any post-CMP residue; too
   aggressive a pre-clean sputters oxide onto the plugs and raises
   via resistance (industry practice[^txt-05]).
3. **Ti, 100 Å.** Titanium in argon, collimated[^rossnagel-1991] or
   ionised[^rossnagel-1998] so that it also reaches into any plug
   recess; thickness per the Cypress report.[^cyp-qtp-113005]
4. **Al–0.5%Cu, 3 200 Å.** Sputtered from an Al–Cu target (the Cypress reports give the film as Al-0.5%Cu) at
   a wafer temperature of roughly 150–300 °C (industry-typical[^txt-02]);
   temperature and pressure set grain size and texture through the
   structure-zone relations.[^thornton-1974][^ohring-2002] The plugs
   are flat, so no reflow step is needed. Thickness per the Cypress
   report.[^cyp-qtp-113005]
5. **Cap, 300 Å.** On the Ti/Al–Cu/TiW reading, TiW sputtered from a
   Ti:W target (10 wt.% Ti[^pat-tiw-hitachi]) in argon; thickness per
   the 2013 Cypress report.[^cyp-qtp-113005] On the 2014 stack the cap
   is 90 Å of titanium and 500 Å of reactively sputtered TiN
   instead.[^cyp-qtp-123907]
6. **Metrology.** Sheet resistance of the stack by
   {term}`four-point probe` (the PDK's 125 mΩ/sq[^pdk-08] is the
   target such a stack reads after the back-end anneals); thickness
   by XRF or profilometry on monitors; reflectivity; stress by wafer
   bow; particles; via-chain resistance at {term}`e-test` once the
   level is patterned.

## Open questions

* No public source describes the SKY130 metal-2 stack directly; we
  read it as Ti/Al–Cu/TiW on the strength of the 2013 Cypress
  report.[^cyp-qtp-113005]
* Whether SkyWater's SKY130 flow uses the Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack or the
  Ti/TiN/AlCu/Ti/TiN stack qualified in 2013–2014[^cyp-qtp-123907] is
  not public; the PDK's 0.36 µm favours the former, which this page
  follows, while the 2014 change and its
  notification[^cyp-pin145273] are evidence for the latter at this
  level. See {ref}`overview-metal-cap`.
* Deposition temperatures, pressures and powers, and whether the Ti
  is collimated or ionised, are not public.

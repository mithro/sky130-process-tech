## What this step is

`PWI` is a high-energy P-type implant into every region left open by
the P-well block mask {ref}`PWBM <step-026>`. Boron is the only
practical choice for a deep p-type layer. That is, into all the
silicon that is not N-well and not one of the special 20 V regions. It
forms the body of the
NMOS transistors: a *{term}`retrograde P-well <retrograde well>`* whose doping peaks below the
surface.

The PDK's process assumptions give the outcome:

| Quantity[^pdk-03] | Value |
|---|---:|
| "p-well peak concentration" | 4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ |
| "p-well peak coordinate" | 0.42 µm |
| characteristic length "y.char" | 0.13 µm |
| P-well vertical dimension | 0.75 µm |
| "min. p-well width to guarantee 90 % peak concentr." | 0.33 µm |

A second boron implant, {ref}`PWI2 <step-028>`,
follows through the same resist; the resist is stripped at
{ref}`PWIS <step-029>`.

Because SKY130 is a bulk process on a lightly doped p-type substrate (8
× 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ background[^pdk-03]; "Bulk"[^skw-02]), the P-well and the
substrate are the same polarity. The well is nonetheless essential
because the substrate alone is far too lightly doped for a 130 nm NMOS.
Inside a deep-N-well tub ({ref}`DNI <step-008>`) the same implant forms
the *isolated* P-well that the PDK's 1.8 V NMOS cross-section shows
"inside of a deep n-well".[^pdk-07]

## Step category

`PWI` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step of the
*well* class: boron at a few hundred keV, dose of order 10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻²,
from a high-energy or high-end medium-current implanter
(industry-typical values).[^txt-01] Boron's light mass gives it about
twice the range of phosphorus at the same energy, so the P-well needs
markedly less energy than the N-well of {ref}`NWI <step-018>`.[^txt-01]

## Why this step exists

The P-well does for NMOS what the N-well does for PMOS, and its
profile is engineered for the same reasons:

* **{term}`Punch-through <punch-through>` and short-channel control.** A boron peak 0.4 µm
  down[^pdk-03] keeps the drain depletion region from reaching the
  source under the 0.15 µm gates while the surface doping is set
  separately by the channel implants ({ref}`LVTNI <step-015>`,
  {ref}`NCHI <step-045>`). ITRS 2001's rule that "the retrograde well
  profile must be less than 0.5 times the drain extension
  depth"[^itrs-01] is the design constraint.
* **Isolation.** Doping under the shallow trenches must be high enough
  that the field does not invert; the PDK's punch-through spacings for
  "n+ - n+" of 0.23 µm and "n+ in pwell to nwell" of 0.15 µm at
  3.3 V[^pdk-03] depend on it.
* **Latch-up.** The P-well is the base of the parasitic NPN of the CMOS
  thyristor;[^wiki-latchup] a heavily doped buried layer lowers its
  resistance and gain[^hook-2003] and allows the 0.23 µm latch-up
  spacing[^pdk-03] on a non-epitaxial wafer.
* **Isolated wells.** Inside the deep N-well, the P-well is the body of
  the isolated 1.8 V NMOS and of the 20 V isolated NMOS whose "p-well
  [is] isolated from the substrate",[^pdk-07] and of the `res_iso_pw`
  P-well resistor.[^pdk-07]

  The published SKY130 {term}`test tile`
  measures an "Isolated Pwell Resistor under Field" and one "under N+
  Active" (25/25 µm, one square), "Pwell to Pwell Space" inside the
  deep N-well from 1.40 to 0.84 µm, and "Pwell to Psub Space" from 0.65
  to 1.40 µm.[^raw-data-testtile-pads]

  The device page's e-test table
  gives the P-well sheet resistance `RSPW` as 3 050 Ω/sq (limits
  2 565–3 535).[^pdk-07] Published two-terminal sweeps give 2 924 Ω
  for the one-square resistor under field and 3 776 Ω for the one
  under N⁺ active.

  The five `xpwres` resistors — the pad list's old
  name for `res_iso_pw` — are 2.65 µm wide and 10.7–159 µm long. They
  give about 3 530–3 550 Ω/sq from the slope of resistance against
  length on the drawn width, at the upper limit of that range, and
  each measures 6–11 % more
  than the resistance the pad list gives for it. This is our extraction from
  the published measurements, with no width correction; the files
  record no temperature, date or
  wafer.[^raw-data-passives][^raw-data-testtile-pads]

MeV-implanted retrograde wells avoid a long high-temperature drive-in:
in Stolmeijer's twin-well process "[t]he high-energy ion-implantation
steps eliminate the need for extended processing times at high
temperatures".[^stolmeijer-1986] Borland, Cho and Kim compared diffused
and retrograde twin wells, among other MeV well structures, for
latch-up at 0.35, 0.25 and 0.18 µm design rules.[^borland-1998]

## How it is typically performed

*An industry-generic retrograde P-well implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era
fab.* SKY130's energy and dose are not public; the values are typical
or from public patents.

* **Species.** Boron (¹¹B⁺) from boron trifluoride.[^wiki-implant] BF₂
  is not used for wells because its boron energy fraction is too small;
  bare boron is the only practical choice for a deep p-type layer
  (category page).
* **Energy.** Set by the wanted peak depth. Published retrograde
  P-wells:

  | Source | Boron energy |
  |---|---|
  | IBM[^pat-well-ibm] | 550 (deep), 150, 45 keV |
  | Hynix[^pat-well-hynix] | 350, 150, 80 keV |
  | Hynix triple-well[^pat-dnw-hynix] | "about 180 keV to about 300 keV" |
  | Harris[^pat-twin-harris] | 360/185/55 keV |
  | Fujitsu[^pat-fuj] | 400 keV, at 1.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻² |

  A boron peak at 0.42 µm[^pdk-03]
  corresponds to an energy in the low hundreds of keV on standard range
  curves.[^txt-01] The 150 keV member of the IBM and Hynix chains is a
  close public analogue.
* **Dose.** 10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻² per energy, e.g. 1.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻² at
  350 keV[^pat-well-hynix] or "about 5×10¹² ions/cm² to about 3×10¹³
  ions/cm²";[^pat-dnw-hynix] a peak of 4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³[^pdk-03] is
  consistent with this range.
* **Tilt and twist.** 7° with twist, or 0° to exploit {term}`channelling` for
  depth.[^wiki-implant][^txt-02] Boron is the species most prone to
  channelling tails, so twist and the {term}`screen oxide` matter (category
  page).
* **Wafer handling.** Batch disc on a high-energy tool, or serial on a
  medium-current tool if the energy allows; resist temperature is
  controlled by disc cooling and the hard bake.
* **Anneal.** {ref}`RTAI <step-034>` — the Cypress integration patent,
  which may still be in force, says where that anneal falls, in the
  collapsed note below this list.
* **Monitoring.** {term}`Sheet resistance <sheet resistance>` on monitor wafers after a monitor
  anneal; thermal-wave on product.

:::{dropdown} From a patent shown as in force (US 8,093,128; estimated expiry 2028-10-22) — open to read
A Cypress patent on the {term}`SONOS`/logic integration states that "a
rapid thermal anneal is performed after implanting both the n-well and
p-well".[^pat-03]
:::

## Open questions

* **Energy and dose.** SKY130's P-well energies and doses are not
  public; only the peak concentration, peak depth and vertical extent
  are.[^pdk-03]
* **Implanter class.** Which implanter class runs it depends on the
  deepest energy, which is unknown.
* **Tilt.** Whether the P-well is implanted at 0° (channelled) or
  tilted is not public.
* **Species.** The species (boron) is inferred, not stated publicly.

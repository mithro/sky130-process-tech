## What this step is

`PWI` is a high-energy P-type implant (boron, the only practical choice for a deep p-type layer) into every region left open by
the P-well block mask {ref}`PWBM <step-026>` — that is, into all the
silicon that is not N-well and not one of the special 20 V regions. It
forms the body of the NMOS transistors: a *retrograde P-well* whose
doping peaks below the surface. The PDK's process assumptions give
the outcome: "p-well peak concentration" 4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ at a "p-well
peak coordinate" of 0.42 µm, a characteristic length "y.char" of
0.13 µm, a P-well vertical dimension of 0.75 µm, and a "min. p-well
width to guarantee 90 % peak concentr." of 0.33 µm (PDK-03). A second
boron implant, {ref}`PWI2 <step-028>`, follows through the same
resist; the resist is stripped at {ref}`PWIS <step-029>`.

Because SKY130 is a bulk process on a lightly doped p-type substrate
(8 × 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ background, PDK-03; "Bulk", SKW-02), the P-well and
the substrate are the same polarity; the well is nonetheless
essential because the substrate alone is far too lightly doped for a
130 nm NMOS. Inside a deep-N-well tub ({ref}`DNI <step-008>`) the
same implant forms the *isolated* P-well that the PDK's 1.8 V NMOS
cross-section shows "inside of a deep n-well" (PDK-07).

## Step category

`PWI` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step of the
*well* class: boron at a few hundred keV, dose of order
10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻², from a high-energy or high-end medium-current
implanter (category page). Boron's light mass gives it about twice
the range of phosphorus at the same energy, so the P-well needs
markedly less energy than the N-well of {ref}`NWI <step-018>`
(TXT-01, ch. 8).

## Why this step exists

The P-well does for NMOS what the N-well does for PMOS, and its
profile is engineered for the same reasons:

* **Punch-through and short-channel control.** A boron peak 0.4 µm
  down (PDK-03) keeps the drain depletion region from reaching the
  source under the 0.15 µm gates while the surface doping is set
  separately by the channel implants ({ref}`LVTNI <step-015>`,
  {ref}`NCHI <step-045>`); ITRS 2001's rule that "the retrograde well
  profile must be less than 0.5 times the drain extension depth"
  (ITRS-01) is the design constraint.
* **Isolation.** Doping under the shallow trenches must be high
  enough that the field does not invert; the PDK's punch-through
  spacings for "n+ - n+" of 0.23 µm and "n+ in pwell to nwell" of
  0.15 µm at 3.3 V (PDK-03) depend on it.
* **Latch-up.** The P-well is the base of the parasitic NPN of the
  CMOS thyristor (WIKI-LATCHUP); a heavily doped buried layer lowers
  its resistance and gain (HOOK-2003) and allows the 0.23 µm latch-up
  spacing (PDK-03) on a non-epitaxial wafer.
* **Isolated wells.** Inside the deep N-well, the P-well is the body
  of the isolated 1.8 V NMOS and of the 20 V isolated NMOS whose
  "p-well [is] isolated from the substrate" (PDK-07), and of the
  `res_iso_pw` P-well resistor (PDK-07).

MeV-implanted retrograde wells replaced diffused wells because they
need no long drive-in and leave the surface lightly doped
(MORRIS-2000; BORLAND-1998).

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic retrograde P-well implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era
fab. SKY130's energy and dose are not public; the values are typical
or from public patents.

* **Species.** Boron (¹¹B⁺) from boron trifluoride (WIKI-IMPLANT).
  BF₂ is not used for wells because its boron energy fraction is too
  small; bare boron is the only practical choice for a deep p-type
  layer (category page).
* **Energy.** Set by the wanted peak depth. Published retrograde
  P-wells: boron at 550 keV (deep), 150 keV and 45 keV (PAT-WELL-IBM);
  350 keV, 150 keV and 80 keV (PAT-WELL-HYNIX); "about 180 keV to
  about 300 keV" (PAT-DNW-HYNIX); 360/185/55 keV (PAT-TWIN-HARRIS);
  400 keV at 1.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻² (PAT-FUJ). A boron peak at 0.42 µm
  (PDK-03) corresponds to an energy in the low hundreds of keV on
  standard range curves (TXT-01, ch. 8) — the 150 keV member of the
  IBM and Hynix chains is a close public analogue.
* **Dose.** 10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻² per energy, e.g. 1.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻² at 350 keV
  (PAT-WELL-HYNIX) or "about 5×10¹² ions/cm² to about 3×10¹³
  ions/cm²" (PAT-DNW-HYNIX); a peak of 4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ (PDK-03) is
  consistent with this range.
* **Tilt and twist.** 7° with twist, or 0° to exploit channelling for
  depth (WIKI-IMPLANT; TXT-02). Boron is the species most prone to
  channelling tails, so twist and the screen oxide matter (category
  page).
* **Wafer handling.** Batch disc on a high-energy tool, or serial on
  a medium-current tool if the energy allows; resist temperature is
  controlled by disc cooling and the hard bake.
* **Anneal.** {ref}`RTAI <step-034>` — the Cypress integration patent
  states that "a rapid thermal anneal is performed after implanting
  both the n-well and p-well" (PAT-03).
* **Monitoring.** Sheet resistance on monitor wafers after a monitor
  anneal; thermal-wave on product.

## Machines typically used

* **High-energy batch implanter** (Axcelis GSD/HE, Varian VIISta 3000)
  for energies above the medium-current ceiling (category page).
* **Medium-current implanter** (Axcelis 8250HT "3keV to 750keV",
  AXCELIS-8250; Varian E500) for a P-well in the 100–300 keV band.
* **Four-point probe / thermal-wave** metrology.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Axcelis GSD** — "B11, BF2, P, As, 10-3000kev, 1e11 to 5e15,
  tilt/twist" (SKW-01). Strength: **strong** for the tool; assignment
  is an **inference**.
* **Axcelis 8250** — "B11, BF2, As … 1e11 to 1e14, 0-60 deg tilt"
  (SKW-01): boron is available and, if the deepest P-well energy is
  within the 8250's range, this tool could run the P-well as well.
  Strength: strong for existence, weak for assignment.

## Resources required

* **Boron trifluoride (BF₃)** source gas in sub-atmospheric cylinders
  (WIKI-IMPLANT; category page); fluorinated species raise
  ion-source consumable usage (AXCELIS-GSD-PAGE).
* Support gases, cryopump and beam-line consumables, monitor wafers.
* Gas suppliers named by SkyWater: Air Products, Praxair, Linde,
  Airgas (SEC-01, SEC-02).

## Open questions

* SKY130's P-well energies and doses are not public; only the peak
  concentration, peak depth and vertical extent are (PDK-03).
* Which implanter class runs it depends on the deepest energy, which
  is unknown.
* Whether the P-well is implanted at 0° (channelled) or tilted is not
  public.
* The species (boron) is inferred, not stated publicly.

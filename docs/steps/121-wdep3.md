## What this step is

`WDEP3` fills the via-1 holes with tungsten. A blanket
chemical-vapour-deposited tungsten film is grown over the whole
wafer — on the IMP TiN liner of {ref}`TIN3 <step-120>`, across the
field and into every 0.15 µm via — thick enough that the holes close
from their sidewalls inward and the surface above them is continuous
metal. The film is polished away from the field at
{ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>`, leaving tungsten only in the holes: the
{term}`W plug` that the PDK calls `via`, "Contact from metal 1 to
metal 2".[^pdk-06] The same fill is described at
{ref}`WDEP <step-099>` (local contact) and {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`
(metal contact), and recurs at {ref}`WDEP4 <step-132>` and above.

The holes being filled are, on our reading of {ref}`VIME <step-119>`
and the PDK, 0.15 µm wide and 0.27 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04]
narrowed by the liner — an {term}`aspect ratio` under 2:1, the
easiest fill of the tungsten-plug levels — and their floor is the TiN
liner over the TiW cap of metal 1.[^cyp-qtp-113005] The PDK's "min.
etch and fill capability for via" of 0.18 µm[^pdk-03] is the public
statement that the fill is qualified down to that opening. The
deposited thickness is not public; a blanket fill must exceed half the
largest hole width plus an over-fill margin, so a film of the order of
0.2–0.3 µm is the industry norm for a via of this size
(industry-typical value[^txt-05]). SkyWater describes its capability
as "Lam/Novellus PECVD Tungsten – plug fill – PNL option for high
aspect ratio (up to 10:1)".[^skw-01]

## Step category

`WDEP3` is a {ref}`Thin-film deposition <category-deposition>` step of
the *CVD tungsten* class; the category page and {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`
set out the process — a silane- or diborane-reduced nucleation layer,
then hydrogen reduction of WF₆ at roughly 400–450 °C and tens of
Torr, almost perfectly conformal — and the kinetics behind
it.[^rev-03][^mcconica-1986] Its product is sacrificial everywhere
except in the holes. What is specific to the via levels is that the
surface under the liner is now an aluminium-alloy line: the
deposition temperature and the fluorine chemistry are bounded by what
the metal below and the TiN between can tolerate, and a void or open
seam in the plug is a via that fails under current rather than a
contact that reads high.

## Why this step exists

Tungsten plugs are how an aluminium back end connects its levels
through holes it cannot fill with sputtered metal, and have been
since Kaanta et al.'s tungsten studs of 1987[^kaanta-1987] and IBM's
four-level fully planarised interconnect of 1991.[^uttecht-1991] The
reasons are those of {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`, with via-level twists:

* **Conformality.** Surface-reaction-limited CVD coats the sidewalls
  at the field rate, so the via fills from the outside in; Ireland's
  review of the tungsten-plug process for high-aspect-ratio
  contacts[^ireland-1997] and Saito et al.'s work on the reliability
  of blanket-tungsten filling[^saito-1993] describe the practice.
  Sputtered aluminium could reach the bottom of a 1.8:1 via only with
  heat or force — Gn, Liu and Guo compare tungsten and aluminium
  plugs for sub-micron contacts and vias[^gn-1994] — and the selective
  tungsten via fill of the 1980s[^rang-1988] gave way to the blanket
  fill because selectivity loss was uncontrollable.
* **Nucleation on the liner, not in the via.** Tungsten nucleates on
  the TiN of {ref}`TIN3 <step-120>`,[^srinivas-1992][^mcconica-1988]
  and a thin silane-reduced nucleation layer[^hegde-1997] — or
  Novellus's pulsed variant, the "PNL option" SkyWater
  names[^skw-01][^pat-pnl-novellus] — makes the bulk step start
  uniformly. Where the sidewall outgasses or carries residue the
  nucleation stalls and the via is "poisoned";[^kobayakawa-1991][^pat-via-poison-umc]
  the {ref}`VIME <step-119>` clean and the degas before the liner
  exist to prevent it.
* **Filling without voids.** A re-entrant profile from the etch or
  liner overhang at the via mouth leaves a seam or keyhole that
  {ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>` may open. Applied Materials' early patent
  on CVD tungsten deposition describes the pressure and nitrogen
  additions used to control the film,[^pat-wcvd-amat] and Kim et al.
  the pulsed nucleation that improves fill in narrow
  holes.[^kim-2004]
* **Resistance and reliability.** The 4 500 mΩ of the PDK's
  `via`[^pdk-08] is dominated by the interfaces rather than the
  tungsten; under current the plug does not electromigrate, but the
  aluminium on either side does, and the plug is the flux-divergence
  point where voids form. Kwok et al. and Hu, Small and Ho describe
  the damage in two-level Al–Cu/W structures,[^kwok-1990][^hu-1993]
  Kawasaki and Hu model the lifetime of tungsten-plug contacts and
  vias,[^kawasaki-1996] and Le, Banerjee and McPherson show how it
  depends on via size.[^le-1996] Thermal stress in the tungsten-plug
  system can also void the aluminium at the plug, as Walls and
  Domae et al. report.[^walls-1995][^domae-1998]
* **Stress.** CVD tungsten is strongly tensile — Shioya et al.
  measured it at temperature[^shioya-1987] — and the blanket film is
  kept to the minimum that fills.

Without `WDEP3` the lined vias would be filled by the aluminium of
{ref}`TIAL12 <step-123>`, which cannot reach their bottoms; the vias
would be open or voided.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic blanket tungsten via fill for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's recipe is not public); the chemistry is set
out in full at {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`.

1. **Chamber.** A single-wafer or multi-station warm-wall CVD reactor
   with a heated pedestal at about 400–450 °C, showerhead delivery
   and a remote NF₃ clean (industry-typical values[^txt-01][^wiki-wf6]);
   Novellus's multi-station Altus family[^novellus-history] is the
   reference design for the "Lam/Novellus" tool SkyWater lists.[^skw-01]
   SkyWater's wording "PECVD Tungsten"[^skw-01] we read as a label
   rather than a statement of a plasma-driven deposition (inference).
2. **Nucleation layer.** WF₆ reduced by SiH₄ (or B₂H₆) for a few
   nanometres,[^wiki-wf6][^txt-01] pulsed if the PNL option is
   used;[^pat-pnl-novellus] the layer is fine-grained and resistive and
   is kept thin.[^hegde-1997]
3. **Bulk fill.** Hydrogen reduction, WF₆ + 3H₂ → W + 6HF, at tens of
   Torr with a large H₂ excess,[^rev-03][^mcconica-1986] at several
   hundred nanometres per minute (industry-typical[^txt-01]); the
   liner keeps the HF and WF₆ from the TiW and any exposed aluminium.
4. **Thickness.** To close a 0.15 µm via, more than 0.075 µm on each
   sidewall plus an over-fill; a film of the order of 0.2–0.3 µm on
   the field is typical for this size (industry-typical
   value[^txt-05]). Too thick a film costs polish time at
   {ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>` and adds stress.[^shioya-1987]
5. **Chamber clean and edge.** NF₃ plasma clean between wafers or
   lots; edge-exclusion or bevel removal of tungsten where the liner
   is absent, since it flakes (industry practice[^txt-05]).
6. **Metrology.** Thickness and sheet resistance on monitors by
   {term}`four-point probe`; stress by wafer bow; cross-section SEM
   for seams and voids; particle counts; via-chain resistance and
   electromigration on test structures at {term}`e-test`.

## Machines typically used

* **{ref}`Blanket tungsten CVD reactor <machine-tungsten-cvd>`**, 200 mm: Novellus Altus
  (multi-station),[^novellus-history] Applied Materials Centura WxZ,
  Genus/ULVAC and Tokyo Electron tungsten CVD chambers
  ({ref}`category-deposition`).
* **Remote plasma clean** (NF₃) on the reactor.
* **Four-point probe**, **stress gauge**, **cross-section SEM**,
  **particle inspection**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Lam/Novellus tungsten CVD with PNL.** SkyWater lists
  "Lam/Novellus PECVD Tungsten – plug fill – PNL option for high
  aspect ratio (up to 10:1)".[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for vendor,
  process and the pulsed nucleation option; the tool model (Altus is
  the Novellus tungsten platform[^novellus-history]) is an
  **inference**. Whether the PNL option is used on this via level,
  whose aspect ratio is far below 10:1, is not public.

## Resources required

* **Tungsten hexafluoride** (WF₆)[^wiki-wf6] and **hydrogen**; **silane**
  (or diborane) for the nucleation layer; **argon** and **nitrogen** carrier
  and purge gases. Gas suppliers named in SkyWater's filings: Air Products
  and Praxair (2021 S-1), Linde and Airgas (fiscal 2023
  10-K).[^sec-01][^sec-02]
* **NF₃** for the chamber clean; HF-tolerant exhaust and abatement.
* **Heater, showerhead and pedestal consumables**; **monitor wafers**
  with TiN for thickness and resistance checks.

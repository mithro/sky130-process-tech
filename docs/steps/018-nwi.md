## What this step is

`NWI` is a high-energy N-type implant — phosphorus, we infer on range
grounds — through the resist windows opened at {ref}`NWM <step-017>`. It
forms the main body of the N-well: a region about a micrometre deep
whose doping peaks well below the surface and falls off towards it — a
*retrograde* well. The PDK's process assumptions describe the result:
"n-well peak concentration" 6.0 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³, a "background
concentration" of 8.0 × 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ and a baseline N-well vertical
dimension of 1.1 µm.[^pdk-03] The second implant of the pair,
{ref}`NWI2 <step-019>`, follows immediately through the same resist, and
the P-channel threshold implant {ref}`LVTPI <step-020>` after that.

The implant passes, we infer, through the pad oxide over the active
areas and through the field oxide over the trenches; at these energies
the difference in range is small compared with the well depth, so the
well is essentially continuous under active and field alike.[^txt-01]

## Step category

`NWI` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step of the
*well* class: phosphorus at hundreds of keV to about 1 MeV and a dose of
order 10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻² from a high-energy implanter (industry-typical
values).[^txt-01] It is, we infer, the second MeV-class implant of the
flow after {ref}`DNI <step-008>`, and shallower than it.

## Why this step exists

Every PMOS transistor in SKY130 sits in this well,[^wiki-cmos][^pdk-07]
and the 16 V extended-drain NMOS use lightly doped N-well as their drain
extension[^pdk-hv][^pat-demos-ti] — we infer the same for the 20 V
family, whose HV methodology is not separately documented. Beyond simply
being N-type, the well's *profile* is engineered:

* **Latch-up.** The parasitic thyristor of CMOS runs through "the source
  of the NMOS, the p-substrate, the n-well and the source of the
  PMOS".[^wiki-latchup] A heavily doped buried layer gives "a low
  resistance path and suppress[es] parasitic bipolar gain for latchup
  protection",[^hook-2003] which is how a bulk process without an
  {term}`epitaxial layer`[^skw-02] meets its 0.23 µm latch-up spacing.[^pdk-03]
  The IBM well patent puts it plainly: "The concentration of dopant is
  higher at the bottom of the well and lower at the upper surface of the
  well. This doping profile helps prevent latch up by raising the
  threshold voltage of the parasitic device formed by the
  well".[^pat-well-ibm]
* **{term}`Punch-through <punch-through>` and short-channel control.** A doping peak beneath the
  channel stops the drain depletion region from reaching the source
  without raising the surface doping that sets the threshold; ITRS 2001
  notes that "the retrograde well profile must be less than 0.5 times
  the drain extension depth to improve short channel effects".[^itrs-01]
* **Well-to-well isolation.** The PDK's assumptions list an "n-well -
  n-well" punch-through spacing of 0.835 µm for 3.3 V;[^pdk-03] the
  buried peak sets this. The published SKY130 {term}`test tile` has a
  "High Voltage Nwell to Nwell Isolation" module with spaces of 2.00,
  2.50, 1.50, 1.27 and 1.00 µm,[^raw-data-testtile-pads] and, in other
  modules of the same tile, an "Nwell-Psub diode" and an "n-well
  resistor";[^raw-data-testtile-pads] 1.27 µm is the PDK's
  minimum N-well space, `NWMCDSP`.[^pdk-03]

{term}`Retrograde wells <retrograde well>` by MeV implantation avoid a
long high-temperature drive-in: in Stolmeijer's twin-well process "[t]he
high-energy ion-implantation steps eliminate the need for extended
processing times at high temperatures".[^stolmeijer-1986] Borland, Cho
and Kim compared diffused and retrograde twin wells, among other MeV
well structures, for latch-up at 0.35, 0.25 and 0.18 µm design
rules.[^borland-1998] Without `NWI` there would be no PMOS
body and no isolation ring for the deep N-well.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic retrograde N-well implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era
fab. SKY130's values are not public; the figures are typical or from
public patents.

* **Species.** Phosphorus (³¹P⁺) from phosphine.[^wiki-implant]
  Phosphorus is preferred over arsenic for wells because its range at a
  given energy is much longer (category page). Where the implanter
  cannot reach the energy singly charged, doubly charged P⁺⁺ is used — a
  UMC patent implants "double charge … P++ … with an energy within a range
  from about 380 keV to about 400 keV" for a retrograde
  well.[^pat-umc-dc]
* **Energy.** Set by the wanted peak depth. Published retrograde N-wells
  of the 0.25–0.13 µm era: 500, 275 and 130 keV phosphorus in a Harris
  twin-well flow;[^pat-twin-harris] 700 keV at 2.0 × 10¹³ cm⁻² plus
  120 keV at 2.0 × 10¹² cm⁻² in a Hynix flow;[^pat-well-hynix] 850 keV
  at 5.2 × 10¹³ cm⁻², 550 keV at 1.25 × 10¹² cm⁻² and 50 keV at 5 ×
  10¹¹ cm⁻² at IBM;[^pat-well-ibm] a "middle n-well" at "about 500 keV
  to about 600 keV" and 5 × 10¹²–2 × 10¹³ cm⁻² in a Hynix
  triple-well.[^pat-dnw-hynix] For a 1.1 µm well depth[^pdk-03] the
  deepest `NWI` energy is plausibly in the 500 keV–1 MeV band (inference
  from range tables).[^txt-01]
* **Dose.** 10¹²–10¹³ cm⁻² per energy (the patents above), giving a peak
  of a few 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ — consistent with the PDK's 6 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³.[^pdk-03]
* **Tilt.** Either the conventional 7° with twist to avoid
  {term}`channelling`[^wiki-implant][^txt-02] or 0° to use channelling for
  depth; the well-edge scattering that tilt causes is the origin of the
  {term}`well proximity effect`.[^hook-2003]
* **Wafer handling.** Batch spinning-disc end station; the long implant
  heats the resist, so disc cooling and a hard-baked resist matter
  (category page).
* **Anneal.** At {ref}`RTAI <step-034>`; a Cypress patent on the
  {term}`SONOS`/logic integration that may still be in force says where
  that anneal falls, in the collapsed note below this list.
* **Monitoring.** {term}`Sheet resistance <sheet resistance>` of monitor wafers after a monitor
  anneal; thermal-wave damage on product.

A Cypress patent on the {term}`SONOS`/logic integration states that "a
rapid thermal anneal is performed after implanting both the n-well and

## Machines typically used

* **{ref}`High-energy ion implanter <machine-high-energy-implanter>`**, 200 mm, batch end station: Axcelis
  (Eaton) GSD/HE ("10 stage LINAC with energies up to
  3 MeV"),[^axcelis-gsd-page] Varian VIISta 3000, Genus/AMAT Quantum,
  Nissin EXCEED-HE[^txt-09] (category page).
* A **{ref}`medium-current <machine-medium-current-implanter>`** tool can reach the lower end of the range if
  doubly charged ions are used — a UMC patent implants "double charge …
  P++ … with an energy within a range from about 380 keV to about
  400 keV" for a retrograde-process punch-through implant, without
  naming a tool class;[^pat-umc-dc] the tool-class reading is ours
  (category page).
* **{term}`Four-point probe <four-point probe>`** and **{ref}`thermal-wave <machine-sheet-resistance-metrology>`** metrology.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Axcelis GSD high-current/high-energy implanter.** SkyWater's
  facilities page lists "Axcelis GSD High current/energy B11, BF2, P,
  As, 10-3000kev, 1e11 to 5e15, tilt/twist"[^skw-01] — phosphorus, the
  full energy band and the dose window of a well implant. Strength:
  **strong** for the tool; assignment to `NWI` is an **inference** from
  its being the only listed tool above 750 keV.
* **Axcelis 8250 medium-current**[^skw-01] is listed without phosphorus
  (its species list is "B11, BF2, As") and is limited in energy, so it
  is unlikely for this step. Strength: strong for existence.

## Open questions

* The SKY130 N-well energies and doses are not public; only the
  resulting peak concentration and depth are.[^pdk-03]
* Whether `NWI` is the deep or the shallow member of the pair, and
  whether doubly charged ions are used, is unknown.
* Tilt angle (0° channelled versus 7°) is not public.
* The species (phosphorus rather than arsenic) is inferred from the
  required range, not stated publicly.
* The device page's e-test table gives the N-well sheet resistance
  `RSNW` as 950 Ω/sq (limits 550–1 350).[^pdk-07] The one test-tile
  N-well resistor with published data, "24.505 / 24.87 : 0.99 sq",
  measures 1 674 Ω two-terminal, about 1.69 kΩ per square and above
  that range (our extraction from the published
  measurements).[^raw-data-passives][^raw-data-testtile-pads] A single,
  nearly square two-terminal structure includes its contacts and the
  spreading of current from them, and the PDK does not say how `RSNW`
  is measured, so the published data cannot tell whether the
  difference lies in the structure or in the well.

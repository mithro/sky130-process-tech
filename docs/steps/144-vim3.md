## What this step is

`VIM3` is the via-3 lithography: the mask step that defines the holes
through which metal 4 will reach metal 3 and the top plates of the
first {term}`MiM capacitor`. On the cap oxide of {ref}`NCAPOX5 <step-143>`,
which seals the polished {ref}`NILD5 <step-141>` dielectric, a resist
is coated, exposed and developed so that a square opening stands over
every place the layout draws a `via3` — GDS 70:44, "Contact from
metal 3 to metal 4".[^pdk-06] The holes are etched at
{ref}`VIM3E <step-145>`, lined at {ref}`TIN5 <step-146>`, filled at
{ref}`WDEP5 <step-147>` and polished at {ref}`WCMP5 <step-148>`.
Unlike `VIM2`, the via-3 mask appears only once in the PDK's mask
table, as "Via3-PLM", flagged as used in SKY130;[^pdk-05] the
minimum-CD table gives it 0.2 µm for `VIM3CD` and `VIM3CDSP`.[^pdk-03]

The periphery rules give "Min and max L and W of via3" 0.200 µm
(via3.1), with two square sizes, 0.200 µm and 0.800 µm, allowed inside
`areaid.mt` (via3.1a) — the layer that marks e-test modules[^pdk-06] —
a spacing of 0.200 µm (via3.2), "Only min. square via3s are allowed
except die seal ring" (via3.3), and enclosure by metal 3 of 0.060 µm
(via3.4) and 0.090 µm on one of two adjacent sides (via3.5); metal 4
must enclose the via by 0.065 µm (m4.3).[^pdk-periph] The rule table
states the layer's function as "Via3 connects met3 to met4 in the
SKY130Q*/SKY130P*/SP8Q/SP8P* flow".[^pdk-periph] The extraction tables
put a finished `via3` at 3 410 mΩ, the same as `via2`.[^pdk-08] The
hole is 0.20 µm wide and, to metal 3, 0.39 µm deep — the height the
stack diagram leaves between the top of `met3` and the bottom of
`met4`[^pdk-04] — an {term}`aspect ratio` of about 1.95:1 as drawn.

What makes this via mask different from the two below is that the
same pattern lands on two kinds of floor. The PDK's `cap_mim`
cross-section draws a via from metal 4 landing on "CAPM" and another
beside it on "M3 (plate 1)":[^pdk-07] the top plate of every capacitor is
contacted from metal 4 through via 3, and so is its bottom plate.
Since the plate stands on the capacitor dielectric above the metal-3
cap, a via over a plate is shallower than a via over bare metal 3 —
by the plate and dielectric thicknesses, of the order of 0.1–0.2 µm
on our reading of {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>` and
{ref}`CAPTIW1 <step-136>` (inference).

## Step category

`VIM3` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *contact-hole* type, like {ref}`CTM1 <step-107>`,
{ref}`VIM <step-118>` and {ref}`VIM2 <step-129>`, which set out the
imaging problem of an isolated small square.[^mack-2007] Its geometry
is via 2's: at 0.20 µm on a 0.40 µm pitch, a 248 nm tool of
{term}`NA` 0.70 (ASML's PAS 5500/750E[^asml-750e]) gives
{term}`k₁ <k1>` = {math}`0.20 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.56`, while an i-line
tool at NA 0.63 would give about 0.35 (our arithmetic), too low for
production contact holes ({ref}`category-lithography`). We therefore
infer, as at via 2, that `VIM3` is exposed on the "ASML DUV stepper"
or "ASML DUV scanner" SkyWater lists,[^skw-01] with a chemically
amplified resist ({term}`CAR`) and {term}`OPC`; nothing public states
the tool or resist.

The process-steps sheet does record a mask type for the via-3 plate.
A tab headed "Sheet4" gives a coded mask type for three masks
only,[^steps-sheet] and, as the {ref}`mask index <masks-index>` does,
we read the codes for the via 2 (`VIM2`) and via 3 (`VIM3`) plates as
denoting embedded {term}`attenuated phase-shift masks <attenuated PSM>`
for 248 nm exposure: the codes use the abbreviation a mask maker uses
for this type, "EAPSM" for "embedded attenuated phase-shift masks" at
248 nm and 193 nm.[^photronics-abr] We also read a digit 4 in each
code, less certainly, as the 4× reduction ratio; the tab does not
define its codes, does not say to which runs these types apply, and
names no exposure tool, resist or tone. A mask made for 248 nm fits
the DUV reading above (inference).

What is specific to this instance is the substrate: through 0.39 µm of
oxide the resist sees reflective metal-3 lines and, over the capacitors, TiW plates at a different
depth, so the pattern-dependent reflection discussed at
{ref}`VIM <step-118>` and Brunner's {term}`swing-curve <swing curve>` analysis[^brunner-1991]
now involve two buried reflectors.

## Why this step exists

Every connection from metal 4 down to metal 3, and every connection
to a `capm` top plate, passes through a `via3`; the mask fixes what
the plug inherits:

* **Hole size.** The drawn 0.200 µm is the top opening; the etch of
  {ref}`VIM3E <step-145>` and the liner of {ref}`TIN5 <step-146>` need
  it printed close to size, and the PDK's 3.4 Ω `via3`[^pdk-08] is
  measured on plugs whose bottom this mask sets. Le, Banerjee and
  McPherson showed that the {term}`electromigration` performance of a
  tungsten-plug via depends on its size.[^le-1996]
* **Placement on metal 3.** With 0.060 µm of metal-3 enclosure
  (via3.4) and 0.065 µm of metal-4 enclosure (m4.3),[^pdk-periph] the
  {term}`overlay` of this layer to {ref}`MM3 <step-139>` is looser than
  via 2's 0.040 µm to metal 2 but on a coarser, 0.3 µm
  line;[^pdk-periph] a via that slips off a line lands on oxide beside
  it. Levinson treats how such budgets are allocated.[^levinson-2005]
* **Placement on the capacitor plate.** A via on a `capm` plate must
  land wholly on the plate and keep its distance from the plate edge,
  where the field concentrates; the PDK's `capm` rule set includes an
  enclosure of the via by the plate and via-to-plate spacings, whose
  values are not published (capm.4 and capm.5 name `via2`; capm.8
  names `via`),[^pdk-periph] and the extraction tables likewise give
  the capacitor via2 and m3 terminals.[^pdk-08] On our reading —
  consistent with the cross-section,[^pdk-07] the layer table[^pdk-06] and
  {ref}`CAPM <step-137>` — in the flow described here those rules
  apply to via 3 (inference; see {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>`).
* **Uniformity through pitch.** Every via is the same square (via3.1,
  via3.3),[^pdk-periph] printed isolated and in arrays — large
  capacitors are contacted by arrays of vias to keep the plate
  resistance low (inference from the 5.8 Ω/sq `RSCAPM`[^pdk-07]) — and
  the proximity behaviour of hole arrays is why hole layers received
  {term}`OPC` and assist features early.[^yamamoto-2000][^socha-2004]

Without `VIM3` there would be no holes for {ref}`VIM3E <step-145>` to
etch; metal 4 would float over unbroken oxide and the capacitor top
plates would have no terminal.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic 248 nm via-hole lithography sequence for a
200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public); {ref}`VIM <step-118>`
and {ref}`VIM2 <step-129>` describe the shared steps at greater
length.

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake and {term}`HMDS` prime on
   the fresh {ref}`NCAPOX5 <step-143>` oxide, then an organic
   {term}`BARC` to suppress the pattern-dependent reflection from the
   buried metal and plates.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive-tone chemically amplified
   resist[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] of a few hundred nanometres
   (industry-typical[^mack-2007]); the
   PDK's 1.14 µm "Photoresist thickness"[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule
   assumption. Airborne amines must be excluded from the track
   (industry practice).[^hinsberg-1992]
3. **Exposure.** KrF at NA 0.7–0.8.[^asml-750e][^asml-800] At
   {math}`k_1 \approx 0.56` a binary reticle with {term}`OPC` and
   modest assist features[^yamamoto-2000][^socha-2004] is the era's
   usual choice; an {term}`attenuated PSM`[^chen-1999-psm] widens the
   window. The process-steps sheet records the latter for the via-3
   plate — an embedded attenuated PSM for 248 nm exposure, on our
   reading of its code;[^steps-sheet][^photronics-abr] whether OPC or
   assist features are added is not public.
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB`, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH
   develop, rinse and dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`MM3 <step-139>` pattern, on targets
   under 0.39 µm of oxide; the 0.060 µm metal-3 enclosure
   (via3.4)[^pdk-periph] and the unpublished plate enclosure are the
   registrations that matter.
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Hole CD by {term}`CD-SEM`; overlay on
   the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class SkyWater lists;[^skw-01] after-develop
   inspection for missing or scummed holes; dose and focus separated
   by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999]

## Open questions

* The process-steps sheet records a mask type for the via-3 plate,
  which we read as an embedded attenuated PSM for 248 nm
  exposure,[^steps-sheet] but not the exposure tool or its model, the
  resist or its tone, the BARC or whether OPC is applied; DUV exposure
  with OPC is an inference from the 0.2 µm hole and the reticle type.
  The tab does not define its codes (the 4× reading is less certain)
  or say to which runs the type applies.
* The values of the `capm` enclosure and spacing rules, and whether
  their `via2` wording[^pdk-periph] is to be read as `via3` in this
  flow, are not public; we read it so from the cross-section,[^pdk-07]
  and the published {term}`test tile` names a "Via-3 array, M4-M3 near
  CAPM (min via-3 space to CAPM)" and a "Via-3 chain, 5754 via-3,
  M4-CAPM over M3".[^raw-data-testtile-pads]
* How deep a via over a capacitor plate is, relative to the 0.39 µm
  over metal 3,[^pdk-04] depends on unpublished plate and dielectric
  thicknesses.
* The PDK does not define the "PLM" suffix; the mask table flags the
  "-PLM" entries as used in SKY130[^pdk-05] and the rule tables name an
  "SP8P*/SKY130P* (PLM) CADflow",[^pdk-periph] so this reference reads
  PLM as the label of the five-metal P flow (inference). The assumptions
  table's 2 µm metal-4 antenna thickness for the "S8Q*/SP8Q"
  flows[^pdk-03] suggests a thick-metal-4 variant whose via 3 is not
  separately listed (inference).
* Whether the 0.8 µm via3 allowed inside e-test modules (via3.1a)[^pdk-periph]
  is filled like the 0.2 µm via is not public; the only via-3 size
  the test-tile pad list states is "(0.2x0.2)".[^raw-data-testtile-pads]

## What this step is

`MM1` is the metal-1 lithography. On the blanket Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack
of {ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>` a resist is coated, exposed and developed
so that resist remains wherever the layout draws `met1` (GDS 68:20,
"Metal 1"[^pdk-06]) and is cleared everywhere else; the exposed metal
is removed at {ref}`MM1E <step-114>`. The PDK's mask table lists
"Metal 1, MM1" as used in SKY130,[^pdk-05] so the step code is the
mask name. Unlike {ref}`CTM1 <step-107>`, which prints holes, this
mask prints *lines*: the resist is the positive image of the wiring.

The public rules make it the tightest line layer in the flow. The
periphery rules give "Width of metal1" 0.140 µm (m1.1) and "Spacing
of metal1 to metal1" 0.140 µm (m1.2) — a 0.28 µm pitch — with
0.280 µm to or from "huge_met1" features (m1.3a, m1.3b), enclosure of
`mcon` by 0.030 µm (m1.4) and 0.060 µm on one of two adjacent sides
(m1.5), a minimum area of 0.083 µm² (m1.6) and a minimum hole area of
0.140 µm² (m1.7); the layer also carries a minimum oxide pattern
density (m1.pd.1, 0.7) and a waffle-drop fill algorithm (`cmm1
waffleDrop`) checked in 700 µm windows.[^pdk-periph] The minimum-CD
table repeats 0.14/0.14 µm as `MM1CD`/`MM1CDSP`,[^pdk-03] the same
values as poly (`P1MCDSP` 0.14 µm) and the {term}`local interconnect`
(`LI1MCD` 0.14 µm).[^pdk-03] The film under the resist is, per the
Cypress report for the S8 process, a 300 Å TiW cap over 3 200 Å of
Al–0.5%Cu on 100 Å Ti,[^cyp-qtp-113005] 0.36 µm in all,[^pdk-04] and
it is flat: the tungsten plugs were polished level at
{ref}`WCMP2 <step-111>`.

## Step category

`MM1` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *critical line-and-space* type, printed on metal. The
category page's account of resolution and {math}`k_1` applies with
particular force: at 0.14 µm on a 248 nm tool of {term}`NA` 0.70 (ASML's PAS
5500/750E[^asml-750e]) the process factor is
{math}`k_1 = 0.14 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.40`, at the
"0.4 for production" boundary the category page quotes,[^wiki-litho]
and only NA 0.80[^asml-800] lifts it to 0.45. The ITRS 2001
lithography chapter assigns 248 nm with resolution enhancement to the
critical layers of the 130 nm generation,[^itrs-03] and we infer that
`MM1` is exposed on the "ASML DUV stepper" or "ASML DUV scanner" that
SkyWater lists,[^skw-01] with a chemically amplified resist and some
combination of off-axis illumination, {term}`OPC` and possibly a
{term}`PSM` (inference from the geometry; nothing public states it).
The other distinguishing property is the substrate: a reflective
metal, on which standing waves and reflective {term}`notching` would ruin the
{term}`CD` control without an anti-reflective scheme.

## Why this step exists

Metal 1 is the first wiring level and, in standard-cell logic, the
densest; its pattern defines every intra-cell connection and the
local routing between cells. What this mask fixes:

* **Line width and space.** At 0.14 µm the printed width sets the
  resistance and the {term}`electromigration` current limit of every metal-1
  wire, and the space sets both the capacitance between neighbours
  and the gap that {ref}`NILD3 <step-115>` must fill. The
  through-pitch behaviour of dense and isolated lines — the
  iso-dense bias, line-end shortening and corner rounding that
  {term}`OPC` corrects[^wiki-opc] — is the classic problem of a
  low-{math}`k_1` line layer; Cheng and Neureuther analyse how
  residual lens aberrations shorten line ends,[^cheng-2002] and
  Brunner how aberrations turn into CD error at low
  {math}`k_1`.[^brunner-1997]
* **Overlay to the contacts.** With 0.030 µm enclosure of `mcon`
  (m1.4)[^pdk-periph] the registration of this layer to
  {ref}`CTM1 <step-107>` is the tightest {term}`overlay` in the back end;
  Levinson's treatment of overlay budgets applies.[^levinson-2005]
* **Reflectivity control.** Aluminium reflects strongly at 248 nm,
  and although the TiW cap of {ref}`TIAL6 <step-112>` is itself a
  partial anti-reflective layer (the role Rocke and Schneegans
  documented for refractory caps on aluminium[^rocke-1988]), a
  {term}`BARC` under the resist is the usual further step. Brunner's
  swing-curve analysis is the basis for choosing it.[^brunner-1991]
* **Resist–substrate compatibility.** Chemically amplified resists
  are poisoned by basic substrates and by nitrogen-containing films:
  Sturtevant et al.,[^sturtevant-1994] Mori et al.[^mori-1996] and
  Chun, Bok and Baik[^chun-1996] all document {term}`footing` or scumming on
  such surfaces, and Yamanaka et al. show how a SiON BARC deforms
  resist profiles and how to suppress it.[^yamanaka-1999] An organic
  BARC over the TiW isolates the resist from the metal (inference
  from practice).

Without `MM1` there would be no pattern for {ref}`MM1E <step-114>` to
transfer, and metal 1 would remain a short-circuiting sheet.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic 248 nm metal-1 lithography sequence for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public):

1. **Surface preparation.** The wafer comes straight from the {term}`PVD`
   cluster; a dehydration bake and, without {term}`HMDS` (which is
   for oxide), an organic {term}`BARC` spun and baked to a thickness
   chosen from the swing-curve minimum over TiW.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc]
   An inorganic SiON BARC is the alternative but needs its own etch
   step and can foot the resist.[^yamanaka-1999][^lin-2001]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive 248 nm chemically amplified
   resist[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] at a few hundred nanometres; the
   PDK's nominal 1.14 µm[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule assumption and
   need not describe this layer. The track's ammonia-filtered
   environment[^skw-01] protects the resist between coat and
   {term}`PEB`.[^hinsberg-1992]
3. **Exposure.** KrF at NA 0.7–0.8 with annular or quadrupole
   off-axis illumination — the technique Shiraishi et al. introduced
   for 64 M DRAM[^shiraishi-1992] — which raises the depth of focus
   of dense 0.28 µm-pitch lines, combined with OPC on the
   {term}`reticle`;[^wiki-opc][^wong-2001] an alternating or {term}`attenuated PSM`
   is a further option for the densest lines.[^levenson-1982][^wiki-psm]
   Which resolution-enhancement techniques SKY130 uses is not public;
   what is public is that the geometry is at the limit where they
   were adopted.[^itrs-03]
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB` to complete the
   acid-catalysed reaction, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH develop, rinse and
   dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`CTM1 <step-107>` contact layer (and
   indirectly to {ref}`LI1M <step-102>`), on box-in-box or grating
   targets etched into the metal.
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Line CD by {term}`CD-SEM` at several
   pitches; overlay on the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class;[^skw-01]
   after-develop inspection for bridging and missing lines; dose and
   focus tracked by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999] Rework —
   strip and re-coat — is possible on metal because no etch has yet
   happened.

## Machines typically used

* **{ref}`DUV (KrF, 248 nm) stepper or scanner <machine-duv-krf-stepper>`**, 200 mm: ASML PAS 5500/750E, /800
  or /850,[^asml-750e][^asml-800] Nikon NSR-S204B,[^nikon-s204b] Canon
  FPA-5000ES ({ref}`category-lithography`).
* **{ref}`Coat/develop track <machine-coat-develop-track>`** with BARC and PEB modules (TEL
  ACT/Lithius,[^tel-act] DNS, Sokudo).
* **{ref}`CD-SEM <machine-cd-sem-overlay-metrology>`**, **overlay tool**, **{ref}`after-develop inspection <machine-defect-inspection>`**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **ASML DUV stepper / DUV scanner.** SkyWater lists both.[^skw-01]
  Strength: **strong** for existence; **inference** for the
  assignment of metal 1 to {term}`DUV`, from the 0.14 µm rules and the ITRS
  layer allocation.[^itrs-03] The model and its NA are not public.
* **Tracks — DNS 80B, Sokudo RF3, TEL ProZ Lithius.**[^skw-01]
  Strength: strong for existence.
* **CD — AMAT Verity, VeraSEM; overlay — KLA 5200/5300/Archer.**[^skw-01]
  Strength: strong for existence (SkyWater statement); use at this
  mask is an inference.

## Resources required

* **248 nm positive chemically amplified photoresist** ({ref}`lithography materials <material-lithography-materials>`; suppliers
  named in SkyWater's 2021 S-1: Dow, JSR, Tokyo Ohka Kogyo[^sec-01]).
* **Organic BARC** for metal, **2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH developer** (the 2021
  S-1 names "Air Products & Chemicals, Inc., Moses Lake" for
  developer[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse solvents, DI water and
  nitrogen.[^txt-02]
* **The MM1 reticle**, generated from the drawn `met1` layer (68:20)
  with its `cmm1` fill and OPC,[^pdk-06][^pdk-periph] on our reading
  with OPC and possibly phase shifting.
* **KrF laser gas** for the exposure tool.

## Open questions

* The exposure tool, NA, illumination, reticle type (binary with
  OPC, or PSM), resist and BARC for `MM1` are not public; DUV with
  resolution enhancement is an inference from the 0.14 µm/0.14 µm
  rules.
* Whether the TiW cap alone gives adequate reflectivity control or
  an organic BARC is added is not public.
* These pages treat the resist strip and post-etch clean as part of
  the {ref}`MM1E <step-114>` etch step.

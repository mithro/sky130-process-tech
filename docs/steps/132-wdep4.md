## What this step is

`WDEP4` fills the via-2 holes with tungsten. A blanket
chemical-vapour-deposited tungsten film is grown over the whole
wafer — on the IMP TiN liner of {ref}`TIN4 <step-131>`, across the
field and into every 0.20 µm via — thick enough that the holes close
from their sidewalls inward and the surface above them is continuous
metal. The film is polished away from the field at
{ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>`, leaving tungsten only in the holes: the
{term}`W plug` that the PDK calls `via2`, "Contact from metal 2 to
metal 3".[^pdk-06] It is the fourth tungsten fill of the flow, after
{ref}`WDEP <step-099>`, {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>` and
{ref}`WDEP3 <step-121>`; {ref}`WDEP5 <step-147>` follows at via 3.

The holes being filled are 0.20 µm wide and 0.42 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04]
narrowed by the liner — an {term}`aspect ratio` of about 2.1:1, the
highest of the tungsten-filled holes so far — and their floor is the
TiN liner over the TiW cap of metal 2.[^cyp-qtp-113005] The deposited
thickness is not public; a blanket fill must exceed half the largest
hole width plus an over-fill margin, so a film of the order of
0.25–0.35 µm is the industry norm for a via of this size
(industry-typical value[^txt-05]) — thicker than at via 1, with
correspondingly more to polish. SkyWater describes its capability as
"Lam/Novellus PECVD Tungsten – plug fill – PNL option for high aspect
ratio (up to 10:1)".[^skw-01] The PDK's `via2` is 3 410 mΩ,[^pdk-08]
the plug included.

## Step category

`WDEP4` is a {ref}`Thin-film deposition <category-deposition>` step of
the *CVD tungsten* class; the category page and {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`
set out the process — a silane- or diborane-reduced nucleation layer,
then hydrogen reduction of WF₆ at roughly 400–450 °C and tens of
Torr, almost perfectly conformal — and the kinetics behind
it,[^rev-03][^mcconica-1986] and {ref}`WDEP3 <step-121>` what changes
when the plug sits between two aluminium levels. What is specific to
this instance is the hole and what will sit on it: a 2.1:1 via whose
seam, if the fill leaves one, lies deeper and is harder for the
polish to expose, and a metal-3 stack of 0.845 µm[^pdk-04] — or 2 µm
in the thick-metal option[^pdk-03] — that will carry the module's
largest currents through these plugs.

## Why this step exists

Tungsten plugs are how an aluminium back end connects its levels
through holes it cannot fill with sputtered metal, since Kaanta et
al.'s tungsten studs of 1987.[^kaanta-1987] The reasons are those of
{ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>` and {ref}`WDEP3 <step-121>`; the via-2 level
adds:

* **Conformality in a deeper hole.** Surface-reaction-limited CVD
  coats the sidewalls at the field rate, so the via fills from the
  outside in; Ireland's review of the tungsten-plug process for
  high-aspect-ratio contacts[^ireland-1997] and Saito et al.'s work on
  the reliability of blanket-tungsten filling of high-aspect-ratio
  holes[^saito-1993] describe the practice. The PNL option SkyWater
  names[^skw-01] — Novellus's pulsed nucleation layer, which gives
  near-ideal step coverage in high-aspect-ratio holes[^pat-pnl-novellus]
  — is more plausibly used here than at via 1 (inference from the
  ratio); Kim et al. describe the pulsed nucleation
  layer's benefit for plug fill.[^kim-2004]
* **Nucleation on the liner, not in the via.** Tungsten nucleates on
  the TiN of {ref}`TIN4 <step-131>`,[^srinivas-1992][^mcconica-1988]
  through a thin nucleation layer.[^hegde-1997] Where the sidewall
  outgasses or carries residue the nucleation stalls and the via is
  "poisoned";[^kobayakawa-1991][^pat-via-poison-umc] the longer via-2
  sidewall exposes more of the NILD4 stack to that risk.
* **Filling without voids.** A re-entrant profile from the etch or
  liner overhang at the via mouth leaves a seam or keyhole that
  {ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>` may open; Applied Materials' early patent on
  CVD tungsten describes the pressure and nitrogen additions used to
  control the film.[^pat-wcvd-amat]
* **Current and reliability.** Metal 3 is the coarse, thick level of
  the flow (0.3 µm width, 0.845 µm thick[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04]), and
  in a five-level design its wires carry power and long signals; the
  via-2 plugs feed them. Under current the plug is the
  flux-divergence point where the aluminium on either side
  voids,[^kwok-1990][^hu-1993] Kawasaki and Hu model the lifetime of
  tungsten-plug vias,[^kawasaki-1996] Le, Banerjee and McPherson show
  how it depends on via size,[^le-1996] Li analysed the "reservoir
  effect" and allowed via misalignment for Al–Cu lines with tungsten
  via plugs,[^li-2003] and Walls and Domae et al. report stress
  voiding in tungsten-plug and stacked-via systems.[^walls-1995][^domae-1998]
* **Stress.** CVD tungsten is strongly tensile — Shioya et al.
  measured it at temperature[^shioya-1987] — and a thicker blanket
  film over two aluminium levels bows the wafer more; the film is
  kept to the minimum that fills.

Without `WDEP4` the lined vias would be filled by the aluminium of
{ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>`, which cannot reach their bottoms; the vias
would be open or voided.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic blanket tungsten via fill for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's recipe is not public); the chemistry is set
out in full at {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`.

1. **Chamber.** A single-wafer or multi-station warm-wall CVD reactor
   with a heated pedestal at about 400–450 °C, showerhead delivery
   and a remote NF₃ clean (industry-typical values[^txt-01][^wiki-wf6]);
   Novellus's multi-station Altus family[^novellus-history] is the
   reference design for the "Lam/Novellus" tool SkyWater lists.[^skw-01]
   SkyWater's wording "PECVD Tungsten"[^skw-01] we read as a label
   rather than a statement of a plasma-driven deposition (inference).
2. **Nucleation layer.** WF₆ reduced by SiH₄ (or B₂H₆) for a few
   nanometres,[^wiki-wf6][^txt-01] pulsed if the PNL option is
   used;[^pat-pnl-novellus] the layer is fine-grained and resistive and
   is kept thin.[^hegde-1997]
3. **Bulk fill.** Hydrogen reduction, WF₆ + 3H₂ → W + 6HF, at tens of
   Torr with a large H₂ excess,[^rev-03][^mcconica-1986] at several
   hundred nanometres per minute (industry-typical[^txt-01]); the
   liner keeps the HF and WF₆ from the TiW and any exposed aluminium.
4. **Thickness.** To close a 0.20 µm via, more than 0.10 µm on each
   sidewall plus an over-fill; a film of the order of 0.25–0.35 µm on
   the field is typical (industry-typical value[^txt-05]). Too thick a
   film costs polish time at {ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>` and adds
   stress.[^shioya-1987]
5. **Chamber clean and edge.** NF₃ plasma clean between wafers or
   lots; edge-exclusion or bevel removal of tungsten where the liner
   is absent (industry practice[^txt-05]).
6. **Metrology.** Thickness and sheet resistance on monitors by
   {term}`four-point probe`; stress by wafer bow; cross-section SEM
   for seams and voids; particle counts; via-2 chain resistance and
   electromigration on test structures at {term}`e-test`.

## Machines typically used

* **Blanket tungsten CVD reactor**, 200 mm: Novellus Altus
  (multi-station),[^novellus-history] Applied Materials Centura WxZ,
  Genus/ULVAC and Tokyo Electron tungsten CVD chambers
  ({ref}`category-deposition`).
* **Remote plasma clean** (NF₃) on the reactor.
* **Four-point probe**, **stress gauge**, **cross-section SEM**,
  **particle inspection**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Lam/Novellus tungsten CVD with PNL.** SkyWater lists
  "Lam/Novellus PECVD Tungsten – plug fill – PNL option for high
  aspect ratio (up to 10:1)".[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for vendor,
  process and the pulsed nucleation option; the tool model (Altus is
  the Novellus tungsten platform[^novellus-history]) is an
  **inference**. Whether the PNL option is used on this via level is
  not public; its 2.1:1 ratio is still far below the 10:1 the entry
  quotes.

## Resources required

* **Tungsten hexafluoride** (WF₆)[^wiki-wf6] and **hydrogen**; **silane**
  (or diborane) for the nucleation layer; **argon** and **nitrogen**
  carrier and purge gases (gas suppliers per SkyWater's
  filings[^sec-01][^sec-02]).
* **NF₃** for the chamber clean; HF-tolerant exhaust and abatement.
* **Heater, showerhead and pedestal consumables**; **monitor wafers**
  with TiN for thickness and resistance checks.

## Open questions

* The nucleation chemistry (silane or diborane, pulsed or
  continuous), the bulk thickness, temperature and pressure of the
  SKY130 via-2 fill are not public.
* Whether SkyWater's "PNL option"[^skw-01] is used at this via level
  is not public; the 2.1:1 ratio makes it plausible but not
  necessary.
* SkyWater's phrase "PECVD Tungsten"[^skw-01] is read here as a label
  for the tungsten CVD tool rather than evidence of a plasma-assisted
  deposition; no public source clarifies it.
* How the 0.8 µm via 2 of the thick-metal ("S8TM") option[^pdk-03] is
  filled — a blanket film of this class cannot close a hole that
  wide, so a lined, partly filled via is the likely outcome
  (inference) — is not public.

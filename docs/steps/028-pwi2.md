## What this step is

`PWI2` is the second P-well implant, run through the same
{ref}`PWBM <step-026>` resist as {ref}`PWI <step-027>` and directly
after it. Like its N-well twin {ref}`NWI2 <step-019>`, it has no
public description beyond its name; from the numbering and position
we infer that it is a second boron dose at a different energy, making
the SKY130 P-well a two-energy chained implant. No public source
describes the pair; chaining is, however, how every published
retrograde P-well of the era is built (PAT-WELL-IBM, PAT-WELL-HYNIX,
PAT-TWIN-HARRIS; TXT-01, ch. 2). The category page draws the same
inference.

The P-well is characterised in the PDK by a single peak of
4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ at 0.42 µm with a "y.char" of 0.13 µm (PDK-03); a
two-implant well fits such a description if the second implant is
either shallower and lighter (broadening the profile towards the
surface) or deeper and lighter (extending it towards the 0.75 µm
vertical dimension).

## Step category

`PWI2` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step of the
*well* class, sharing mask, species and anneal with
{ref}`PWI <step-027>`; its implanter depends on its energy.

## Why this step exists

A single boron implant leaves the region between its peak and the
surface too lightly doped for three purposes that a P-well must
serve, each of which has a public design rule attached:

* **Field isolation.** Under the trench oxide between two n⁺
  diffusions the doping must be high enough to prevent inversion and
  punch-through; the PDK's assumption for "n+ - n+" punch-through
  spacing at 3.3 V is 0.23 µm (PDK-03). A Hynix triple-well flow uses
  a separate "n channel field stop" boron implant at "about 50 keV to
  about 80 keV" and 5 × 10¹¹–1 × 10¹³ cm⁻² for this (PAT-DNW-HYNIX);
  in a chained well the shallower member does the same job.
* **Punch-through under the channel.** A moderate peak a few tenths
  of a micrometre below the gate is the retrograde-well mechanism for
  short-channel control (ITRS-01; PAT-VT-LSI describes "punch-through
  barriers" built from chained boron implants at 100, 70 and 50 keV).
* **Lateral profile at the well edge.** The shallower members set the
  N-well/P-well junction position — 0.034 µm from the drawn edge in
  the PDK's assumptions (PDK-03) — and hence the n⁺/p⁺ isolation
  rules (RUBIN-2002).

Published examples of the split: IBM's 550/150/45 keV boron chain
(PAT-WELL-IBM); Hynix's 350 keV at 1.5 × 10¹³ cm⁻², 150 keV at
6.0 × 10¹² cm⁻² and 80 keV at 1.0 × 10¹² cm⁻² (PAT-WELL-HYNIX);
Harris's 360/185/55 keV (PAT-TWIN-HARRIS). SKY130's `PWI` + `PWI2` is
the two-member version.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic second P-well implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab
(illustrative values only):

* **Species.** Boron (¹¹B⁺) from BF₃ (WIKI-IMPLANT), as for `PWI`.
* **Energy.** Different from `PWI`. The published shallow members lie
  between about 45 and 185 keV (PAT-WELL-IBM, PAT-WELL-HYNIX,
  PAT-TWIN-HARRIS). Whether SKY130's second implant is the shallow or
  the deep one is not stated.
* **Dose.** Lower than the main implant — a few 10¹² cm⁻² is typical
  (PAT-WELL-HYNIX's shallower members are 6.0 × 10¹² and
  1.0 × 10¹² cm⁻²).
* **Tilt/twist.** 7° with twist; a shallower boron implant is the
  most channelling-prone case in the module, so the pad oxide's role
  as a randomising screen matters (category page; TXT-02).
* **Sequence.** Back-to-back with `PWI` on the same tool where the
  energies allow; otherwise a cassette move to the medium-current
  tool. The two implants share the resist's thermal budget.
* **Anneal.** {ref}`RTAI <step-034>` (PAT-03).

## Machines typically used

* **High-energy batch implanter** (Axcelis GSD/HE, Varian VIISta
  3000) or **medium-current implanter** (Axcelis 8250HT "3keV to
  750keV", AXCELIS-8250; Varian E500), depending on energy.
* **Four-point probe / thermal-wave** metrology.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Axcelis GSD** ("B11 … 10-3000kev", SKW-01). Strength: **strong**
  for existence; assignment is an inference. Running both P-well
  implants on the one batch tool is the simplest reading of the
  back-to-back steps.
* **Axcelis 8250** ("B11, BF2, As … 1e11 to 1e14", SKW-01) is equally
  capable of a boron implant in the 100–200 keV band. Strength:
  strong for existence, weak for assignment.

## Resources required

* **Boron trifluoride (BF₃)**; support gases; cryopump and source
  consumables; monitor wafers (category page).
* No new resist: the {ref}`PWBM <step-026>` resist is reused.

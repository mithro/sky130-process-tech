## What this step is

`MM2` is the metal-2 lithography. On the blanket Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack of
{ref}`TIAL12 <step-123>` a resist is coated, exposed and developed so
that resist remains wherever the layout draws `met2` (GDS 69:20,
"Metal 2"[^pdk-06]) and is cleared everywhere else; the exposed metal
is removed at {ref}`MM2E <step-125>`. The PDK's mask table lists
"Metal 2, MM2" as used in SKY130,[^pdk-05] so the step code is the
mask name. Like {ref}`MM1 <step-113>` it prints *lines* — the resist
is the positive image of the wiring — and its rules are the same as
metal 1's.

The periphery rules give "Width of metal 2" 0.140 µm (m2.1) and
"Spacing of metal 2 to metal 2" 0.140 µm (m2.2) — a 0.28 µm pitch —
with 0.280 µm to or from "huge_met2" features (m2.3a, m2.3b), a
minimum area of 0.0676 µm² (m2.6) and a minimum hole area of 0.140 µm²
(m2.7); the layer must enclose the via-1 plugs beneath it by 0.055 µm
(m2.4) and by 0.085 µm on one of two adjacent sides (m2.5), and the
via-2 plugs that will later land on it by 0.040 µm (via2.4) and
0.085 µm (via2.5); it carries a minimum oxide pattern density of 0.7
(m2.pd.1) checked in 700 µm windows and a `cmm2 waffleDrop` fill
algorithm.[^pdk-periph] The minimum-CD table repeats 0.14/0.14 µm as
`MM2CD`/`MM2CDSP`.[^pdk-03] The film under the resist is, per the
Cypress report for the S8 process, a 300 Å TiW cap over 3 200 Å of
Al–0.5%Cu on 100 Å Ti,[^cyp-qtp-113005] 0.36 µm in all,[^pdk-04] and
it is flat: the via plugs were polished level at
{ref}`WCMP3 <step-122>`.

## Step category

`MM2` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *critical line-and-space* type, printed on metal — the
same class as {ref}`MM1 <step-113>`, which sets out the imaging
argument in full. At 0.14 µm on a 248 nm tool of NA 0.70 (ASML's PAS
5500/750E[^asml-750e]) the process factor is
{math}`k_1 = 0.14 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.40`, at the
production boundary the category page quotes,[^wiki-litho] and only
NA 0.80[^asml-800] lifts it to 0.45. The ITRS 2001 lithography chapter
assigns 248 nm with resolution enhancement to the critical layers of
the 130 nm generation,[^itrs-03] and we infer that `MM2` is exposed on
the "ASML DUV stepper" or "ASML DUV scanner" SkyWater
lists,[^skw-01] with a chemically amplified resist and some
combination of off-axis illumination, {term}`OPC` and possibly a
{term}`PSM` (inference from the geometry). What differs from metal 1
is the registration target — buried tungsten vias rather than
contacts — and the surface below the metal, which is now a via level
with plugs the polish may have recessed (a topography of a few
nanometres at most, on our reading, and invisible to the exposure).

## Why this step exists

Metal 2 is the second wiring level and, in standard-cell logic, the
first that carries the block-level routing over the cells; its pattern
fixes:

* **Line width and space.** At 0.14 µm the printed width sets the
  resistance (125 mΩ/sq for the level[^pdk-08]) and the
  electromigration current limit of every metal-2 wire — a limit that
  the tungsten vias at each end raise for short segments, the
  short-length effect discussed at {ref}`TIAL12 <step-123>` — and the
  space sets the capacitance to neighbours and the gap that
  {ref}`NILD4 <step-126>` must fill. Through-pitch behaviour, line-end
  shortening and corner rounding are the low-{math}`k_1` problems
  {term}`OPC` corrects.[^wiki-opc][^cheng-2002][^brunner-1997]
* **Overlay to the vias.** With 0.055 µm enclosure of `via` (m2.4)
  and 0.085 µm on one of two adjacent sides (m2.5),[^pdk-periph] the
  registration of this layer to {ref}`VIM <step-118>` is looser than
  metal 1's 0.030 µm to its contacts, but a metal-2 line that misses
  its via leaves the plug top exposed to the {ref}`MM2E <step-125>`
  etch and the {ref}`NILD4 <step-126>` dielectric. Levinson's
  treatment of overlay budgets applies.[^levinson-2005]
* **Reflectivity control.** The TiW cap is a partial anti-reflective
  layer — the role Rocke and Schneegans documented for refractory caps
  on aluminium[^rocke-1988] and Chen, Chen and Tsou for a sputtered
  TiN cap in back-end lithography[^chen-1991-tin] — but a {term}`BARC`
  under the resist is the usual further step; Brunner's swing-curve
  analysis is the basis for choosing it.[^brunner-1991]
* **Resist–substrate compatibility.** Chemically amplified resists
  are poisoned by basic and nitrogen-bearing surfaces:
  Sturtevant et al., Mori et al. and Chun, Bok and Baik document the
  footing and scumming,[^sturtevant-1994][^mori-1996][^chun-1996] and
  an organic BARC over the TiW isolates the resist (inference from
  practice).
* **Fill for the polish.** The `cmm2` waffle-drop fill and the 0.7
  oxide-density rule[^pdk-periph] exist for {ref}`CMPM2 <step-127>`,
  and are printed here.

Without `MM2` there would be no pattern for {ref}`MM2E <step-125>` to
transfer, and metal 2 would remain a short-circuiting sheet.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic 248 nm metal-line lithography sequence for a
200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public); {ref}`MM1 <step-113>`
describes the shared steps at greater length.

1. **Surface preparation.** The wafer comes straight from the PVD
   cluster; a dehydration bake and an organic {term}`BARC` spun and
   baked to a thickness chosen from the swing-curve minimum over
   TiW.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc] An inorganic SiON BARC is the
   alternative but needs its own etch and can foot the
   resist.[^yamanaka-1999][^lin-2001]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive 248 nm chemically amplified
   resist[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] at a few hundred nanometres; the
   PDK's nominal 1.14 µm[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule assumption. The
   track's ammonia-filtered environment[^skw-01] protects the resist
   between coat and {term}`PEB`.[^hinsberg-1992]
3. **Exposure.** KrF at NA 0.7–0.8 with annular or quadrupole
   off-axis illumination[^shiraishi-1992] and OPC on the
   reticle;[^wiki-opc][^wong-2001] an alternating or attenuated PSM is
   a further option for the densest lines.[^levenson-1982][^wiki-psm]
   Which resolution-enhancement techniques SKY130 uses is not public.
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB`, 0.26 N TMAH
   develop, rinse and dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`VIM <step-118>` via layer, and through
   it to {ref}`MM1 <step-113>`, on box-in-box or grating targets; the
   via targets are tungsten squares in oxide under the metal stack,
   read through the metal by the alignment sensor's topography
   signal or by using metal-1 targets directly (industry practice;
   which SKY130 does is not public).
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Line CD by {term}`CD-SEM` at several
   pitches; overlay on the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class;[^skw-01]
   after-develop inspection for bridging and missing lines; dose and
   focus tracked by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999] Rework is
   possible because no etch has yet happened.

## Machines typically used

* **KrF step-and-scan or stepper**, 200 mm: ASML PAS 5500/750E, /800
  or /850,[^asml-750e][^asml-800] Nikon NSR-S204B,[^nikon-s204b] Canon
  FPA-5000ES ({ref}`category-lithography`).
* **Coat/develop track** with BARC and PEB modules (TEL
  ACT/Lithius,[^tel-act] DNS, Sokudo).
* **CD-SEM**, **overlay tool**, **after-develop inspection**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **ASML DUV stepper / DUV scanner.** SkyWater lists both.[^skw-01]
  Strength: **strong** for existence; **inference** for the
  assignment of metal 2 to DUV, from the 0.14 µm rules and the ITRS
  layer allocation.[^itrs-03] The model and its NA are not public.
* **Tracks — DNS 80B, Sokudo RF3, TEL ProZ/Lithius; "Track ammonia
  clean".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence.
* **CD — AMAT Verity, VeraSEM; overlay — KLA 5200/5300/Archer.**[^skw-01]
  Strength: strong.

## Resources required

* **248 nm positive chemically amplified photoresist** (suppliers
  named in SkyWater's S-1: Dow, JSR, Tokyo Ohka Kogyo[^sec-01]).
* **Organic BARC** for metal, **0.26 N TMAH developer** (Air Products
  per the S-1[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse solvents, DI water
  and nitrogen.[^txt-02]
* **The MM2 reticle**, generated from the drawn `met2` layer (69:20)
  with its `cmm2` fill,[^pdk-06][^pdk-periph] on our reading with OPC
  and possibly phase shifting.
* **KrF laser gas** for the exposure tool.

## Open questions

* The exposure tool, NA, illumination, reticle type (binary with
  OPC, or PSM), resist and BARC for `MM2` are not public; DUV with
  resolution enhancement is an inference from the 0.14 µm/0.14 µm
  rules.
* Which layer's alignment targets `MM2` uses — the via layer, or
  metal 1 through the via oxide — is not public.
* Whether the TiW cap alone gives adequate reflectivity control or
  an organic BARC is added is not public.
* Whether the resist strip after {ref}`MM2E <step-125>` is a separate
  step in SkyWater's flow is not shown in the step list used in this
  reference.

## What this step is

`DNI` is a high-energy phosphorus ion implant through the resist windows
opened at {ref}`DNM <step-007>`. It places a buried layer of N-type
doping a micrometre or more below the surface, forming the *deep N-well*
(`dnwell`)[^pdk-06] — the floor of the isolated-P-well "tub" that the
PDK's device pages describe.[^pdk-07] The label "Deep N+ implant" used
in this reference should not necessarily be read as a heavily doped
N⁺ source/drain-type layer: we infer from comparable triple-well
patents (~10¹³ cm⁻²)[^pat-dnw-hynix] a modest well-type dose, and read
the "+" as denoting N-type polarity.

At this point in the flow the isolation trenches are etched but
unfilled, so the implant enters bare silicon at the trench floors and
passes through the nitride/pad-oxide stack over the active areas.
The buried layer therefore sits slightly deeper under the field than
under the active regions; at MeV energies the difference is small
compared with the implant range and {term}`straggle`.

## Step category

`DNI` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step, and the
only *high-energy* (MeV-class) implant in the isolation module. Later
well implants ({ref}`NWI <step-018>`, {ref}`PWI <step-027>`) are also
high-energy but shallower; the source/drain and channel implants are
low-energy.

## Why this step exists

The deep N-well isolates P-wells from the p-substrate. In SKY130 that
serves several documented purposes:

* **Isolated NMOS for high-side and high-voltage use.** The 20 V
  isolated NMOS "is built over a Deep N-Well. This permits the p-well
  to be isolated from the substrate and permit 'high-side' usage
  (where the PW body is held above ground)".[^pdk-07] A Cypress {term}`SONOS`
  patent describes a deep N-well formed under a high-voltage MOS
  transistor region.[^pat-04]
* **Body biasing and noise isolation for 1.8 V NMOS.** The 1.8 V NMOS
  "can be made either with or without the DNW under the p-well";[^pdk-07]
  RF NMOS "must be enclosed by deep nwell" (rule dnwell.6).[^pdk-periph]
  Published measurements at 130 nm show deep N-well isolation reducing
  substrate-noise coupling into RF and analogue
  devices.[^chew-2002][^yang-2005]
* **Vertical NPN.** "The NPN uses the deep n-well as the
  collector".[^pdk-07]
* **Latch-up margin** on a bulk (non-epitaxial) substrate,[^skw-02]
  which the 2001 ITRS notes can be recovered by "alternate doping
  means" once {term}`STI` has replaced {term}`LOCOS`.[^itrs-01]

The published SKY130 {term}`test tile` measures the resulting well
directly: a "Deep Nwell Resistor W/L = 25/25: 1 sq.", "DNW to DNW
Space" isolation at 11.5, 9.5, 7.5 and 5.5 µm, "Nwell to DNW Space" at
7.0, 6.0, 5.0 and 4.5 µm, and area- and perimeter-intensive "Deep N
well-Pwell" and "Deep N well-Psub" diodes.[^raw-data-testtile-pads]

Without `DNI`, none of the isolated, high-side, RF or NPN devices in
the PDK could exist; the plain 1.8 V and 5 V transistors would be
unaffected.

In the published test-tile data, placing a 1.8 V NMOS in deep N-well
does not visibly change it at zero body bias. Structures the pad list places "in DNW"
and structures without it give thresholds of 0.535 V and 0.534 V at
7/8 µm and 0.719 V and 0.707 V at 7/0.15 µm
(maximum-transconductance extrapolation at V_DS = 0.1 V, less half the
drain bias), and drain currents of 3.45 mA and 3.40 mA at 7/0.15 µm
with V_GS = V_DS = 1.8 V (our extraction from the published
measurements; one structure of each).[^raw-data-lv-mosfets][^raw-data-testtile-pads]

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic deep N-well implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab:

* **Species and source.** Phosphorus (³¹P⁺), produced in the ion source
  from phosphine (PH₃) gas or from solid red phosphorus; Wikipedia
  lists "Arsine gas or phosphine gas" as the sources for arsenic and
  phosphorus.[^wiki-implant] Phosphorus is preferred over arsenic for
  deep wells because its lighter mass gives a much longer range at a
  given energy.
* **Energy.** In the MeV range. A Hynix triple-well patent of the same
  era specifies "an ion implantation energy of about 0.6 MeV to about
  1.6 MeV";[^pat-dnw-hynix] Wikipedia gives typical energies of "10 to
  500 keV" and adds that "accelerators capable of 5 MeV (800,000 aJ)
  are common".[^wiki-implant] SkyWater's own implanter list includes an
  "Axcelis GSD High current/energy B11, BF2, P, As, 10-3000kev",[^skw-01]
  i.e. up to 3 MeV, which covers this range.
* **Dose.** Of the order of 10¹³ cm⁻²; the same Hynix patent gives
  "about 5×10¹² ions/cm² to about 1.5×10¹³ ions/cm²".[^pat-dnw-hynix]
  SkyWater quotes 1×10¹¹ to 5×10¹⁵ cm⁻² for the GSD.[^skw-01] A
  published study of a 1 MeV phosphorus triple-well implant shows that
  the dose trades NMOS performance against diode leakage, which is why
  the dose is kept modest.[^bourdelle-2002]
* **Tilt and twist.** A few degrees of tilt (7° is the textbook
  convention)[^txt-02] with a twist to avoid {term}`channelling` — Wikipedia
  notes that most implantation is done "a few degrees off-axis" for
  this reason;[^wiki-implant] for a deep well some fabs deliberately
  implant at 0° to *use* channelling for extra depth, but that is
  unusual.
* **Wafer handling.** Batch (spinning-disk) end station for a
  high-energy implanter; wafer cooling to keep the resist below its
  flow temperature during the long implant; charge neutralisation
  with an electron shower.
* **Anneal.** No dedicated anneal — the liner oxidation at
  {ref}`LINOX <step-010>` and later furnace steps provide it (see the
  discussion on {ref}`DNM <step-007>`). MeV implants leave a buried
  damage band whose residual dislocations can raise junction leakage if
  the anneal is inadequate.[^jang-1998][^ro-1999]
* **Monitoring.** {term}`Sheet resistance <sheet resistance>` on bare monitor wafers after a
  monitor anneal, and thermal-wave (modulated-reflectance) damage
  measurement on product wafers.

## Machines typically used

* **High-energy ion implanter**, 200 mm, batch end station:
  Axcelis/Eaton GSD/HE and the earlier NV-GSD series, Varian VIISta 3000
  (single-wafer), Applied Materials Quantum X; Genus 1500 (later
  Varian). High-energy machines use an RF linear accelerator or a
  tandem stage after the analyser magnet to reach MeV
  energies.[^txt-02][^txt-09] Multiply charged phosphorus ions (P²⁺,
  P³⁺) are a common way to reach MeV-equivalent energies on a lower
  terminal voltage, at the cost of beam current.[^spinelli-1985]
* **{term}`Four-point probe <four-point probe>`** and **thermal-wave** metrology.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Axcelis GSD high-current/high-energy implanter.** SkyWater lists "Axcelis GSD
  High current/energy B11, BF2, P, As, 10-3000kev, 1e11 to 5e15"[^skw-01]
  — the only tool on the site list that reaches MeV energies. Strength:
  strong (SkyWater statement) for the tool; the assignment to `DNI`
  follows from it being the only tool capable of the energy. Axcelis
  describes the GSD family as "the industry benchmark for the longest
  manufactured and supported batch ion implanter" and its GSD/HE
  variant as reaching "energies up to 3 MeV".[^axcelis-gsd]
* A SkyWater maintenance-technician profile shows technicians
  troubleshooting "an implanter" and, separately, servicing tool robots
  and a vacuum system[^skw-07] — corroboration that implanters are
  serviced in house, not tool evidence.

## Resources required

* **Phosphine (PH₃)** or solid phosphorus source charge;[^wiki-implant]
  PH₃ is a toxic gas delivered in sub-atmospheric or dilute cylinders
  with gas-cabinet monitoring.
* **Source-support gases** — argon or xenon for source stabilisation;
  **hydrogen** in some sources.
* **Liquid nitrogen / cryopump regeneration**, high-purity nitrogen
  vent gas.
* **Source consumables** — filaments/cathodes, arc-chamber liners,
  extraction electrodes; beam-line apertures.
* **Wafer-cooling** water and helium for the platen.
* **Monitor wafers** for dose and damage control.

## Open questions

* The SKY130 deep N-well energy, dose, tilt and resulting depth are
  not public; the MeV / ~10¹³ cm⁻² figures are from a contemporaneous
  third-party patent and from SkyWater's tool capability range.
* Whether the species is phosphorus (assumed here on physical grounds)
  is not stated publicly.
* Whether the implant is single-energy or a chain of energies (as some
  foundries use to shape the well) is unknown.
* No public source confirms which implanter runs this step; the
  Axcelis GSD is the only MeV-capable tool on SkyWater's public list.

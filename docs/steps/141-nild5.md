| **Next step** | {ref}`CMPM3 <step-142>` |

## What this step is

`NILD5` deposits the inter-level dielectric between metal 3 and
metal 4. Over the freshly etched metal-3 lines of
{ref}`MM3E <step-140>` — 0.845 µm tall on the PDK's stack
diagram,[^pdk-04] as close as 0.300 µm apart (m3.2[^pdk-periph]) —
and over the {term}`MiM capacitors <MiM capacitor>` that now sit on some of them, a silicon
dioxide film is deposited thick enough to fill every gap and bury the
lines and the capacitor top plates under a continuous layer, which
{ref}`CMPM3 <step-142>` then polishes flat and
{ref}`NCAPOX5 <step-143>` caps before the via-3 mask
({ref}`VIM3 <step-144>`). The name follows the PDK: its stack diagram
labels the dielectric between `met3` and `met4` "NILD5" with a
relative permittivity of 4.1.[^pdk-04] Unlike NILD3 and NILD4, it is
drawn with no separate "_C" film beside it,[^pdk-04] and the
diagram's levels — `met3` bottom at 2.7861 µm, `met4` bottom at
4.0211 µm — differ by 1.235 µm, exactly the 0.845 µm metal plus a
finished dielectric of 0.39 µm above it,[^pdk-04] the via-3 height
(our arithmetic from the diagram).

Two things make this gap fill different from the two below it. The
gap is deeper: a 0.30 µm space between 0.845 µm lines is an
{term}`aspect ratio` of about 2.8:1, or about 2.6:1 if the metal is
the 0.765 µm of the Cypress S8TNV description[^cyp-qtp-113005] (our
arithmetic; the thickness question is set out at
{ref}`MM3E <step-140>`). And the surface is not only metal: every
`capm` capacitor contributes a top plate — of the order of 0.1 µm
thick on our reading of {ref}`CAPTIW1 <step-136>` — on a thin
dielectric on top of its metal-3 bottom plate, and the PDK's
cross-section draws "CAPM" as a layer inside the dielectric between
"M3 (plate 1)" and the via-3 level.[^pdk-07] The oxide deposited here
is therefore also the dielectric that surrounds the capacitor's plate
edge, the site of its periphery capacitance `CMIMP` (0.19 fF/µm) and
of its edge leakage.[^pdk-07]

## Step category

`NILD5` is a {ref}`Thin-film deposition <category-deposition>` step of
the *dielectric {term}`gap-fill <gap fill>`* class, the second repeat of
{ref}`NILD3 <step-115>` (after {ref}`NILD4 <step-126>`), which sets out
the two routes a 130 nm-era
fab had between aluminium lines: {term}`HDP-CVD`, with simultaneous
argon sputtering to keep the gap mouth open, and {term}`PECVD`
{term}`TEOS` oxide with etch-back. SkyWater's HDP tool is listed with
"high aspect (5:1) fill capability",[^skw-01] above the 2.8:1 needed
here. What is specific to this instance is the depth of the gap, the
larger {term}`overburden` a thicker metal needs before it can be polished,
and a device — the MiM capacitor, with a floating top plate on a thin
dielectric — that is exposed to the deposition plasma directly
rather than through a long antenna, as the gates below are.

## Why this step exists

The inter-metal dielectric does the jobs set out at
{ref}`NILD3 <step-115>`; metal 3 and its capacitors sharpen them:

* **Insulation without voids in a deeper gap.** A 2.8:1 space between
  0.845 µm lines is the deepest line-to-line gap in the flow so far;
  Nguyen's review explains how the HDP deposition/sputter balance
  fills such a gap without a seam,[^nguyen-1999] the Novellus and
  Applied Materials patents give production recipes for
  high-aspect-ratio gaps,[^pat-hdp-novellus][^pat-hdp-amat] and Yoo
  and Swope show a PECVD fluorinated oxide filling inter-metal gaps of
  this class.[^yoo-1996] A seam left in a metal-3 space is a path for
  the {ref}`VIM3E <step-145>` etch and a moisture trap next to the
  capacitor.
* **Protecting the capacitor from the plasma.** A MiM capacitor whose
  plates connect to exposed conductors is charged by the plasma
  ({term}`plasma charging`)
  across its thin dielectric: Wang, Ackaert et al. showed how
  plasma-charging damage of floating MiM capacitors depends on the
  antenna areas connected to each plate and proposed layout rules
  against it,[^wang-2004-mim] and Cheung analysed the charging
  mechanism during plasma-enhanced dielectric
  deposition.[^cheung-2000] Here the top plate floats until via 3 is
  made; Hwang and Giapis's mechanism for charging during interlevel
  HDP oxide deposition[^hwang-1998] and Byun et al.'s and Kim, Lee and
  Seo's damage-reduction measures[^byun-2005][^kim-2004-hdp] apply
  (inference that the capacitor, not only the gates, is the concern).
* **Capacitance to two neighbours.** The film sets the line-to-line
  capacitance within metal 3 and the level-to-level capacitance to
  metal 4 that the PDK's extraction tables encode; they give a
  metal-3-to-metal-4 plate capacitance of 84.03 aF/µm²,[^pdk-08] which
  for {math}`k = 4.1` corresponds to about 0.43 µm of oxide (our
  arithmetic, {math}`d = \varepsilon_0 k / C`), close to the diagram's
  0.39 µm.[^pdk-04] Stamper, Fuselier and Tian set out why these RC
  terms matter at the sub-0.25 µm generation,[^stamper-1998] and the
  ITRS 2001 interconnect chapter the permittivities of the era.[^itrs-02]
* **Protection of the metal without damage.** The oxide must not
  stress a thick aluminium line into voiding[^yue-1985] or let it
  {term}`hillock` during deposition[^chaudhari-1974] — a thicker film is more
  prone to hillocks, as discussed at {ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>` — and
  must stay below the Al–Cu temperature limit (roughly 400–450 °C,
  industry-typical[^txt-05]).
* **A polishable overburden.** {ref}`CMPM3 <step-142>` needs enough
  oxide above the highest feature — the capacitor plates stand above
  the metal-3 lines — to reach a flat surface at the 0.39 µm
  target[^pdk-04] with margin; the PDK's "Oxide Bias for MM3" of 1.15,
  against 0.6 for MM1 and MM2, and the assumptions table's global "Min
  pattern density for oxide" of 0.75[^pdk-03] — beside the metal-3
  periphery rule m3.pd.1's 0.7[^pdk-periph] — are, on our reading, the
  fill and density controls that keep the overburden uniform over a
  thicker metal.

Without `NILD5` there is nothing to carry metal 4, metal 3 is exposed,
and the capacitor plates have no dielectric around them.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic inter-metal dielectric deposition over 0.3 µm
spaces between thick aluminium lines in a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab
(SKY130's recipe is not public); the sequence is that of
{ref}`NILD3 <step-115>`.

1. **Liner.** A thin PECVD oxide (silane or TEOS) at 350–400 °C
   (industry-typical[^txt-05][^raupp-1992]), deposited without
   high-energy ion bombardment so that the aluminium, the gates at
   the far end of the antennas and the floating capacitor plates are
   not charged before they are covered;[^hwang-1998][^byun-2005] the
   PDK draws no liner at this level.[^pdk-04]
2. **Gap fill by HDP-CVD.** SiH₄/O₂/Ar in an {term}`inductively coupled <ICP>`
   plasma with RF bias, a sputter-to-deposition ratio tuned so that
   the mouth of each 0.3 µm gap stays open until it fills from the
   bottom;[^nguyen-1999][^pat-hdp-novellus][^pat-hdp-amat] backside
   helium and a cooled chuck hold the wafer at an
   aluminium-compatible temperature, and the recipe is tuned against
   charging damage as Byun et al. and Kim, Lee and Seo
   describe.[^byun-2005][^kim-2004-hdp] Lieberman and Lichtenberg
   treat the ion-bombardment physics.[^lieberman-2005] SkyWater lists
   "Lam/Novellus High Density Plasma (HDP) doped and phos doped with
   sputter etch – high aspect (5:1) fill capability",[^skw-01] and a
   maintenance-page photograph names "a Novellus high density plasma
   tool".[^skw-07]
3. **Gap fill by PECVD TEOS (alternative).** TEOS/O₂ plasma oxide at
   about 400 °C[^raupp-1992][^wiki-teos] in a deposition–etch–deposition
   sequence; SkyWater's "PECVD TEOS, C2 and Producer"[^skw-01] is the
   capability. At 2.8:1 an etch-back is needed to avoid a seam
   (inference from the geometry).
4. **Overburden.** Deposition continues — HDP or a faster PECVD TEOS
   cap — until the oxide over the tallest features is thick enough to
   polish; with 0.845 µm lines and plates on some of them, the as-
   deposited film is of the order of the metal thickness plus the
   finished 0.39 µm and a polish margin (industry practice;[^txt-05]
   the exact thickness is not public).
5. **Chamber clean.** NF₃ remote-plasma clean between wafers or lots.
6. **Metrology.** Thickness and refractive index by ellipsometry on
   monitors; stress by wafer bow; gap fill by cross-section SEM on
   dense metal-3 arrays; charging by antenna and MiM test structures
   at {term}`e-test`; particles.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Lam/Novellus HDP-CVD.** SkyWater lists "Lam/Novellus High Density
  Plasma (HDP) doped and phos doped with sputter etch – high aspect
  (5:1) fill capability"[^skw-01] and its maintenance page names "a
  Novellus high density plasma tool".[^skw-07] Strength: **strong** for
  the tool class; the assignment to this step is an **inference** from
  the gap geometry, and the public entry names only "doped and phos
  doped" HDP films,[^skw-01] while the undoped gap-fill oxide
  described here is not on the list; whether SKY130's NILD5 is doped
  is an open question (below). The
  model (SPEED is the Novellus HDP product[^novellus-history]) is
  not public.
* **PECVD TEOS "C2 and Producer".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for
  existence; medium for depositing the liner and/or the overburden
  here (inference); as the gap fill itself it would need the
  deposition–etch–deposition sequence above.
* **PECVD silane oxide "C1"**[^skw-01] as a liner candidate (weak for
  assignment).

## Open questions

* The route (HDP-CVD, PECVD TEOS with etch-back, or a combination),
  the liner, the deposited thickness and whether the film is doped
  are not public.
* Why NILD5 is drawn without the thin "_C" film shown at NILD3 and
  NILD4[^pdk-04] is not public.
* The extraction tables' metal-3-to-metal-4 plate capacitance implies
  about 0.43 µm of k 4.1 oxide against the diagram's 0.39 µm (our
  arithmetic[^pdk-08][^pdk-04]); whether the difference reflects a
  different permittivity, a thicker finished dielectric, or modelling
  convention is not public.
* No public source describes a MiM-specific measure (a charging-safe
  first layer, a plate-edge spacer) at this level.
* The metal-3 thickness itself (0.765 µm or 0.845 µm) is an open
  question discussed at {ref}`MM3E <step-140>`.

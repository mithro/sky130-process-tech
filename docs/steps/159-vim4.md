## What this step is

`VIM4` is the via-4 lithography: the mask step that defines the holes
through which metal 5, the top metal, will reach metal 4 and the top
plates of the second {term}`MiM capacitor`. On the cap oxide of
{ref}`NCAPOX6 <step-158>` a resist is coated, exposed and developed so
that a square opening stands over every place the layout draws a
`via4` — GDS 71:44, "Contact from metal 4 to metal 5".[^pdk-06] The
holes are etched at {ref}`VIM4E <step-160>`. In the step list used in
this reference no liner, tungsten fill or plug polish follows that
etch; the next step is the metal-5 deposition of
{ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`, where the reading of how the holes are filled
is set out.

The PDK's mask table lists "Via4, VIM4", flagged as used in SKY130, and
separately a "Pad Via, VIPDM" that is not flagged;[^pdk-05] the
minimum-CD table gives `VIM4CD` and `VIM4CDSP` of 0.8 µm, and
`VIPDMCD` 1.2 µm and `VIPDMCDSP` 1.27 µm for the pad via.[^pdk-03] The
PDK's via-4 rules (0.800 µm, via4.1) match the flagged `VIM4` entry and
its 0.8 µm `VIM4CD`, not the unflagged 1.2 µm `VIPDM` pad
via,[^pdk-05][^pdk-03][^pdk-periph] so this page describes the `VIM4`
level; the step list used in this reference does not explain the
"(pad via)" in this step's title.

The periphery rules define via 4 narrowly. "Via4 connects met4 to met5
in the SKY130P*/SP8P* flow"; the "Min and max L and W of via4" is
0.800 µm (via4.1), the "Spacing of via4 to via4" 0.800 µm (via4.2),
"Only min. square via4s are allowed except die seal ring" (via4.3),
metal 4 must enclose the via by 0.190 µm (via4.4) and metal 5 by
0.310 µm (m5.3); via 3 and via 4 may be drawn only at multiples of 90°
(x.2).[^pdk-periph] The extraction tables give a finished `via4`
380 mΩ, against 3 410 mΩ for `via3`.[^pdk-08] The hole is 0.80 µm wide
and 0.505 µm deep — the via-4 height of the stack diagram[^pdk-04] — an
{term}`aspect ratio` of about 0.63:1 as drawn (our arithmetic), against
about 1.95:1 at via 3. A via 4 has sixteen times the area of the
ordinary 0.200 µm via 3 (our arithmetic from via3.1 and
via4.1[^pdk-periph]; the rules also allow a 0.800 µm square via 3
inside `areaid.mt`, via3.1a[^pdk-periph]), so area alone
would account for more than the ninefold difference in resistance —
scaled by area alone a via 4 would be 213 mΩ, against the published
380 mΩ, so per unit area via 4 is about 1.8 times the more resistive of
the two (our arithmetic); interface and liner terms dominate both, and
the PDK's numbers do not by themselves show what fills the via
(inference).

## Step category

`VIM4` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *contact-hole* type, like {ref}`VIM <step-118>`,
{ref}`VIM2 <step-129>` and {ref}`VIM3 <step-144>`, but at a scale that
removes the resolution problem those pages discuss. At 0.80 µm on a
0.80 µm space an i-line (365 nm) stepper of {term}`NA` 0.6 would work
at a process factor {term}`k₁ <k1>` of
{math}`k_1 = 0.80 \times 0.6 / 0.365 \approx 1.3` (our arithmetic), far
from the limit, and ASML describes older exposure tools that "migrate
to the lithography of choice for less critical layers";[^asml-30] on
geometry alone, a level this coarse is one a
mixed line could print on i-line with a DNQ/novolac
resist[^dammel-1993] ({ref}`category-lithography`).

The process-steps sheet, however, records a mask type for the via-4
plate. A tab headed "Sheet4" gives a coded mask type for three masks
only,[^steps-sheet] and, as the {ref}`mask index <masks-index>` does,
we read the code for the via 4 (`VIM4`) plate as denoting a binary
(chrome-on-quartz) mask for 248 nm exposure: the code uses the
abbreviations mask makers and the patent literature use for these
types.[^photronics-abr][^pat-bim-tsmc] We also read a digit 4 in the
code, less certainly, as the 4× reduction ratio; the tab does not
define its codes, does not say to which runs the type applies, and
names no exposure tool, resist or tone. A reticle made for 248 nm
points to exposure on a KrF tool — the "ASML DUV stepper" or "ASML DUV
scanner" SkyWater lists[^skw-01] — rather than on its i-line tools,
and so to a chemically amplified resist ({term}`CAR`) (inference). On
a 248 nm tool of NA 0.70 (ASML's PAS 5500/750E[^asml-750e]) the level
works at {math}`k_1 = 0.80 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 2.3` (our
arithmetic), with no need for phase shifting (inference).

What is specific to this instance is the purpose the size serves. On
our reading (see {ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`), a 0.8 µm square only
0.505 µm deep is a hole that a sputtered aluminium film can enter:
Skelly and Gruenke found that 1.3 µm-wide, straight-walled vias 1 µm
deep were planarised (100 % step coverage) by bias sputtering under
conditions that gave only 60 % coverage in 2.8 µm vias, while unbiased
deposition gave 20 %;[^skelly-1986] their result is not a simple
function of aspect ratio, and neither of their geometries is via 4's,
so it shows that a hole of this class can be filled by sputtering, not
that this one is (inference). Simulations by Bär, Lorenz and
Ryssel and by Kim and Lee show how tapering the sidewall changes the
coverage of a sputtered film inside a via.[^bar-2002][^kim-2011] The
single allowed size (via4.3[^pdk-periph]) then gives every via 4 the
same aspect ratio, which a fill process tuned to one geometry needs
(inference).

## Why this step exists

Every connection from metal 5 down to metal 4, and every connection to
a `cap2m` top plate, passes through a `via4`; the mask fixes what the
connection inherits:

* **Hole size and fill.** The drawn 0.800 µm is both minimum and
  maximum (via4.1)[^pdk-periph] — a fixed geometry. For a {term}`tungsten plug <W plug>`,
  as at vias 1–3, a small hole is filled by conformal CVD; for a hole
  filled by the metal above, the aspect ratio decides whether the metal
  reaches the bottom corners, and Gn, Liu and Guo compared tungsten and
  aluminium plugs for sub-micrometre contacts and vias.[^gn-1994] The
  resist profile printed here is the starting point for any taper the
  etch transfers: the Chartered patent pulls the resist back to form a
  tapered via profile that improves metal step
  coverage,[^pat-taper-chartered] and the Motorola patent alternates
  resist and oxide etching to slope contact walls.[^pat-sloped-motorola]
* **Placement on metal 4.** With 0.190 µm of metal-4 enclosure
  (via4.4) and 0.310 µm of metal-5 enclosure (m5.3),[^pdk-periph] the
  {term}`overlay` budget is generous compared with via 3's 0.060 µm to
  metal 3; Levinson's book has a chapter on overlay.[^levinson-2005]
* **Placement on the capacitor plate.** The PDK's stacked `cap_mim`
  cross-section draws vias from `CAP2M` up to "M5 (plate 1)" and from
  "M4 (plate 2)" up to "M5 (plate 2)";[^pdk-07] a via on a plate must
  land wholly on it and away from its edge, where the dielectric is
  thinnest. The PDK publishes no `cap2m` enclosure or spacing rule for
  via 4,[^pdk-periph] so the margins are not known.
* **Arrays for current.** Since only one via size is allowed, wide
  metal-4 and metal-5 connections are made with arrays of 0.8 µm vias;
  the "via4.irdrop" rules count via-4s "on the same connector" in sets
  of 1–4, 5–10, 11–100 and more than 100, though they are flagged for
  the copper flow.[^pdk-periph] Le, Banerjee and McPherson found that
  W-plug via {term}`electromigration` lifetime dispersion depends on
  via size for one electron-flow direction in a TiN-capped
  metallisation, and not at all when titanium is part of the capping
  layer;[^le-1996] how either case carries over to a via filled by
  aluminium is not public.

Without `VIM4` there would be no holes for {ref}`VIM4E <step-160>` to
etch; metal 5 would lie on unbroken oxide and the second capacitor's top
plates would have no terminal.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic large-via lithography sequence for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab, written for 248 nm exposure because the process-steps
sheet records a 248 nm reticle type for this plate (our reading, see
above);[^steps-sheet] SKY130's recipe is not public.

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake and {term}`HMDS` prime on
   the fresh {ref}`NCAPOX6 <step-158>` oxide; a {term}`BARC` or dyed
   resist suppresses reflection from the buried metal and capacitor plates
   (Brunner's {term}`swing-curve <swing curve>` analysis[^brunner-1991]), though at this
   feature size the need is smaller than at the 0.15–0.2 µm vias below
   (inference).
2. **Resist coat.** A positive-tone chemically amplified
   resist[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] (inference from the KrF reading;
   on an i-line tool it would be a DNQ/novolac
   resist[^dammel-1993][^reichmanis-1989]); the PDK's
   1.14 µm "Photoresist thickness"[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule
   assumption. At 0.8 µm a thicker film than at the 0.15–0.2 µm vias is
   affordable (inference), and a somewhat thicker resist tolerates the erosion of a
   deliberately tapered etch (inference from the patents
   above[^pat-taper-chartered][^pat-sloped-motorola]). Airborne amines
   must be excluded from the track (industry practice).[^hinsberg-1992]
3. **Exposure.** KrF (248 nm) on a reduction stepper or scanner — 4× on
   our less certain reading of the sheet's code[^steps-sheet] — with
   conventional illumination; the ASML PAS 5500 family spans i-line and
   DUV models on one platform.[^asml-30][^chiphistory-pas5500] The
   reticle is binary, the type the sheet records for this plate (our
   reading[^steps-sheet][^photronics-abr]), and at
   {math}`k_1 \approx 2.3` no {term}`OPC` is needed (inference from the
   geometry).
4. **Post-exposure bake, develop and bake.** {term}`PEB`, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH
   develop, rinse and dry;[^txt-02] a post-develop bake hardens the
   resist, and a hotter one rounds its edges, the resist-profile lever
   for a sloped via (industry practice[^mack-2007]).
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`MM4 <step-154>` pattern, on targets under
   0.505 µm of oxide.[^pdk-04]
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Hole CD by optical CD or {term}`CD-SEM`
   on the AMAT Verity/VeraSEM class SkyWater lists; overlay on the KLA
   5200/5300/Archer class;[^skw-01] after-develop inspection for missing
   or scummed holes; dose and focus tracked by Ausschnitt's
   method.[^ausschnitt-1999]

## Resources required

* **248 nm positive chemically amplified photoresist** ({ref}`lithography materials <material-lithography-materials>`; on the KrF
  reading, an inference); SkyWater's 2021 S-1 names Dow, JSR and Tokyo
  Ohka Kogyo as photoresist suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **HMDS**, **BARC** or dyed resist, **2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH developer** (the
  2021 S-1 names "Air Products & Chemicals, Inc., Moses Lake" for
  developer[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, {ref}`DI water <material-ultrapure-water>` and {ref}`nitrogen <material-process-gases>`.[^txt-02]
* **The VIM4 reticle**, generated from the drawn `via4` layer
  (71:44)[^pdk-06] — a binary (chrome-on-quartz) mask for 248 nm
  exposure, on our reading of the type the process-steps sheet records
  for this plate.[^steps-sheet][^photronics-abr][^pat-bim-tsmc]
* **KrF excimer laser gas** for the exposure tool — krypton and
  fluorine,[^wiki-excimer] typically supplied as premixes in a neon
  buffer, with periodic replacement of the laser gas and optics
  (industry-typical; {ref}`category-lithography`).

## Open questions

* The step list used in this reference does not explain the "(pad via)"
  in this step's title; the unflagged "Pad Via, VIPDM" entry[^pdk-05] is
  left outside the flow described here because its 1.2 µm
  size[^pdk-03] does not match the via-4 rules.[^pdk-periph]
* The process-steps sheet records a mask type for the via-4 plate,
  which we read as a 248 nm binary reticle,[^steps-sheet] but not the
  exposure tool or its model, the resist or its tone, the BARC or any
  resist-profile treatment; that the level is exposed on a KrF tool is
  an inference from the reticle type. The tab does not define its codes
  (the 4× reading is less certain) or say to which runs the type
  applies.
* The sheet does not say why a 0.8 µm level, which its geometry would
  allow on an i-line tool, is recorded with a reticle type for
  248 nm.
* Whether the via-4 holes are printed and etched with a taper, and the
  sidewall angle, are not public.
* The enclosure and spacing rules for via 4 on a `cap2m` plate are not
  published.[^pdk-periph]
* The "via4.irdrop" rules are flagged for the copper flow; whether the
  aluminium flow has equivalent via-array rules is not public.[^pdk-periph]

## What this step is

`RTAD2` is described in this reference as a second rapid thermal
anneal, a counterpart of the source/drain activation anneal
{ref}`RTAD <step-088>` four steps earlier but placed *after* the
sacrificial {term}`PSG`, its polish and the
{term}`cap oxide`, and immediately before the contact lithography. The wafer
— transistors now buried under a polished, capped phosphosilicate
glass — is heated for seconds in an inert ambient in a lamp-heated
single-wafer chamber and cooled again. Nothing is deposited or
removed; what changes is the state of the dielectric stack and of the
dopant beneath it.

The step list used in this reference does not explain the purpose of
a second anneal at this point. We read two purposes, from the state
of the wafer here and from published practice, and present both as
inferences:

* **A source/drain anneal**: the final, stable
  activation of the arsenic and boron source/drains under a
  dielectric cap, after the dose-loss and out-diffusion risks of an
  anneal on a bare surface are past. The {ref}`RTAD <step-088>` page
  sets out the physics — arsenic that was over-activated by a fast
  anneal relaxes during later heating,[^rousseau-1994][^luning-1992]
  and a two-step anneal changes the end-of-range defect
  population[^camillo-castillo-2002] — and notes this step as a
  possible reason.
* **A dielectric anneal**, suggested by the freshly polished and
  capped glass on the wafer: a thermal cycle
  that densifies the HDP PSG and the {term}`PECVD` cap, drives out the water
  the glass absorbed during the polish and its cleans (Levin[^levin-1982]),
  and stabilises the phosphorus in it (Miyazaki et al. used exactly
  such an {term}`RTA` to improve PSG's passivation[^miyazaki-1994]). Alvi and
  Kwong showed that PSG can be reflowed by rapid thermal
  annealing,[^alvi-1986] and Thar et al. and Maxim et al. used RTA
  to fuse {term}`BPSG` and activate implants in the *same* step,[^thar-1993][^maxim-1994]
  which is precisely the double purpose considered here. Hsiao, Liu
  and Wang measured how HDP PSG responds to
  {term}`thermal budget`.[^hsiao-2005]

On either reading the anneal is done here, and not later, because
this is the last moment before titanium meets silicon: after
{ref}`TI/TIN1 <step-097>` and {ref}`CSIL <step-098>` no anneal above
the {term}`silicide`'s stability limit is possible, and after tungsten and
aluminium are on the wafer the ceiling falls to about 400–450 °C
(category page[^txt-02]).

## Step category

`RTAD2` is an {ref}`Anneal / thermal processing <category-anneal>`
step of the *rapid thermal* type — the fourth and last RTA of the
implant-anneal class in the flow, after {ref}`RTAI <step-034>`,
{ref}`TIPRTAD <step-075>` and {ref}`RTAD <step-088>`; the silicide
anneal {ref}`CSIL <step-098>` that follows is, on our reading, cooler.
What is specific to it is the stack it heats through: a lamp-heated
RTA
couples to the wafer through its films, and a wafer carrying
0.5 µm[^pdk-03] of glass and a cap oxide has a different emissivity
and absorptance from the bare, nitride-capped wafer of
{ref}`RTAD <step-088>` — Sorrell and Gyurcsik and Chen et al. treat
the pyrometry corrections this demands.[^sorrell-1993][^chen-2002-rtp]
It is also the anneal with the least freedom: the junctions are
already at their 0.1 µm design depth,[^pdk-03] so its temperature
and time must add almost nothing to the diffusion budget, which
argues for a spike or a short soak at or below the
{ref}`RTAD <step-088>` peak (inference).

## Why this step exists

The reasons follow from the two readings above.

* **Stable activation.** The highest activation is obtained by
  annealing hot and fast, but arsenic above its equilibrium
  solubility clusters and deactivates during any later heating,
  injecting interstitials as it does so;[^rousseau-1994][^luning-1992][^nobili-1983]
  a second, controlled anneal *before* the contact module fixes the
  active concentration that the {term}`contact silicide`
  ({ref}`CSIL <step-098>`) and the PDK's {term}`sheet resistances <sheet resistance>` (N⁺
  120 000 mΩ/sq, P⁺ 197 000 mΩ/sq[^pdk-08]) will see, instead of
  letting it drift during the silicide RTA. Camillo-Castillo et al.
  studied what a two-step anneal does to end-of-range
  defects.[^camillo-castillo-2002]
* **Dielectric densification.** PSG deposited by {term}`HDP-CVD` at
  350–450 °C is not fully dense and takes up water during wet
  processing;[^levin-1982][^hsiao-2005] an anneal densifies it,
  lowers and stabilises its wet-etch rate — which the contact etch
  and the sacrificial etch ({ref}`SACETCH <step-095>`) depend on —
  and drives off water that would otherwise be released onto the
  titanium {term}`liner`. PECVD oxides likewise shrink and lose hydrogen on
  their first anneal.[^adams-1981-pecvd]
* **Mobile-ion gettering.** The phosphorus in the glass getters
  sodium only when the glass has been heated enough for the ions to
  reach it; the anneal activates the {term}`gettering` the
  {ref}`PSG <step-089>` page describes.[^meek-1974]
* **Thermal budget bookkeeping.** Doing the dielectric anneal as a
  rapid thermal step rather than a furnace {term}`reflow` keeps the junction
  movement negligible — the reason Alvi and Kwong and Thar et al.
  proposed RTA reflow in the first place.[^alvi-1986][^thar-1993]

Without `RTAD2`, on this reading, the glass would enter the contact
module wet and under-dense, and the source/drain activation would be
set by whatever thermal history followed rather than by a controlled
anneal.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic post-PMD rapid thermal anneal for a 200 mm,
130 nm-era fab (SKY130's recipe is not public):

* **Tool and ambient.** Single-wafer lamp-heated RTA in nitrogen or
  argon: {term}`RTP` "heats silicon wafers to temperatures exceeding 1,000°C
  for not more than a few seconds" with "high intensity lamps" and
  "in situ pyrometry".[^wiki-rtp] Oxygen is excluded: the cap
  oxide seals the surface, so no oxidation is wanted, and any
  exposed poly heads in the {term}`nitride cuts <nitride cut>` must stay clean.
* **Temperature and time.** A soak of seconds at a temperature at
  or below the source/drain anneal's peak (typical
  industry range 900–1050 °C[^txt-05][^txt-10]), or a spike; Alvi
  and Kwong report PSG reflow by RTA in this range,[^alvi-1986] and
  Thar et al. combined BPSG fusion with implant activation in one
  RTA.[^thar-1993] The two reseller documents disagree on the 8800's
  ramp rate: the product page gives "Programmable, 1 – 180°C per
  second",[^ag-8800] while the specification PDF's own 8800 section
  gives "up to 100°C per second with Ceramic Shield; up to 150°C per
  second without Ceramic Shield" and reserves "1 - 180°C per second"
  for the 8108.[^ag-8108] Both put the steady-state range at
  400–1200 °C.
* **Sequence.** Load; purge; low-temperature stabilisation for
  pyrometer lock; ramp; soak or spike; controlled ramp-down; unload.
  Because the glass is hygroscopic, the queue between the cap
  deposition and this anneal is kept short (industry practice).
* **Control.** Emissivity correction for the dielectric stack;[^sorrell-1993]
  model-based temperature control of the kind Vandenabeele and
  Renken describe;[^vandenabeele-1998] sheet-resistance maps of
  implanted monitors and wet-etch-rate or FTIR checks of PSG
  monitors after each lot (Roozeboom and Parekh review RTP
  systems[^roozeboom-1990]).
* **Furnace alternative.** A furnace densification — typically
  700–800 °C for tens of minutes for a phosphosilicate
  glass[^txt-02] — would densify the glass but add a diffusion
  budget the 0.1 µm junctions[^pdk-03] cannot afford; "Increasingly,
  furnace anneals are being supplanted by Rapid Thermal
  Anneal".[^wiki-furnace] The 0.1 µm junctions[^pdk-03] and
  SkyWater's single listed RTA[^skw-01] make an RTA the plausible
  reading (inference).

  1150C"[^skw-01]) are the batch alternative but, for the
  thermal-budget reason above, an unlikely one. Strength: strong for
  existence; weak for assignment.

## Open questions

* The purpose of a second source/drain RTA at this place in the
  sequence this reference describes is an
  open question; both readings above (stable activation, dielectric
  densification) are our inferences from the physics and published
  practice described above.
* The temperature, time and ambient are not public.
* Whether the anneal reflows the glass at all, or merely densifies
  it, is not public; at 130 nm a true reflow is unlikely
  (inference from the junction depth).

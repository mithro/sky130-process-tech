|---|---|

| **Step code** | `VIM4E` |
| **Category** | {ref}`Etch <category-etch>` |
| **Phase** | BEOL — MiM capacitors, metal 3–5, via 3–4 |
| **Previous step** | {ref}`VIM4 <step-159>` |
| **Next step** | {ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>` |

## What this step is

`VIM4E` etches the via-4 holes. Through the 0.80 µm resist openings of
{ref}`VIM4 <step-159>` a fluorocarbon plasma cuts the
{ref}`NCAPOX6 <step-158>` cap and the {ref}`NILD6 <step-156>` oxide down
to two kinds of floor: the TiW cap of the metal-4 lines, 0.505 µm below
the surface on the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] and the top plates
of the second {term}`MiM capacitors <MiM capacitor>`, which stand above the metal-4 cap on the
capacitor dielectric — the PDK's stacked `cap_mim` cross-section draws
vias rising from `CAP2M` as well as from the metal-4 shapes.[^pdk-07]
The step list used in this reference has no separate strip step after
`VIM4E`; this page treats the resist strip and clean as part of the
etch.

What follows the etch sets this via apart from vias 1–3. At
{ref}`VIME <step-119>`, {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` and
{ref}`VIM3E <step-145>` the hole is next lined with TiN and filled with
tungsten, and the {term}`plug <W plug>` is polished; in the step list used in this
reference, `VIM4E` is followed directly by the metal-5 deposition of
{ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`. On the reading set out on that page, the
aluminium of metal 5 fills the hole, so the shape this etch leaves — its
sidewall angle, its top corner and the cleanliness of its floor — is the
shape the sputtered metal must cover (inference). The geometry is
forgiving: a 0.800 µm square (via4.1) on a 0.800 µm space (via4.2),
enclosed by metal 4 by 0.190 µm (via4.4),[^pdk-periph] 0.505 µm
deep — an {term}`aspect ratio` of about 0.63:1 (our arithmetic). The
PDK's `via4` resistance is 380 mΩ.[^pdk-08]

## Step category

`VIM4E` is an {ref}`Etch <category-etch>` step of the *dielectric,
fluorocarbon* class and the last of the four via etches;
{ref}`VIME <step-119>` sets out the class — the stop layer is a metal
that fluorine can etch, since tungsten forms volatile WF₆ (Petri, Henry
and Sadeghi[^petri-1992]), so the etch must reach the TiW in a
polymerising regime and stop on time — and {ref}`CTME <step-108>` the
surface chemistry.[^flamm-1981][^winters-1992] It shares with
{ref}`VIM3E <step-145>` the *dual-depth* landing on line caps and
capacitor plates; Freescale's MiM patent states that requirement for its
via etch, "selective for etch stop onto the plate electrodes and onto
the conductor in the interconnect layer".[^pat-mim-freescale]

Two things are specific to this instance. At an aspect ratio below 1
the {term}`ARDE` and loading effects that Gottscho, Jurgensen and
Vitkavage reviewed[^gottscho-1992] are small, and the larger holes
expose more of the wafer than a via-3 layer, which makes the endpoint
signal stronger (inference from the geometry; Wodecki describes the
low-open-area problem of smaller vias[^wodecki-1999]). And the profile
becomes a design choice rather than a by-product: a vertical wall is
easiest to control, but a sloped or partly tapered wall is what the
public literature on sputter-filled vias recommends — the Motorola
patent slopes contact walls by alternating oxide and resist
etching,[^pat-sloped-motorola] the Chartered patent tapers via profiles
by resist pull-back to enhance step coverage,[^pat-taper-chartered]
and Bär, Lorenz and Ryssel and Kim and Lee simulate how a taper changes
the sidewall coverage of a sputtered film.[^bar-2002][^kim-2011] Whether
SKY130's via 4 is tapered is not public.

## Why this step exists

The via-4 hole sets the resistance and reliability of every connection
from metal 5 to metal 4 and to the `cap2m` plates; the etch decides:

* **A clean, complete landing on the metal-4 cap.** Polymer or oxide
  left on the floor raises the via resistance and, as Bui et al. showed
  for the cap under a tungsten-plug via, degrades {term}`electromigration`
  performance;[^bui-1994] on the via-4 fill reading at {ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`, the floor is
contacted directly by the metal-5 underlayer after an in-situ sputter
pre-clean (inference; the pre-clean is discussed on that page).
* **Not punching through the plate or the cap.** A via over a capacitor
  reaches its floor before a via over bare metal 4 and then sits in the
  plasma for the rest of the etch (inference). If the fluorine-lean
  regime fails, the etch thins the plate and could expose the thin
  capacitor dielectric; over metal 4 it could consume the TiW cap and
  expose aluminium to fluorine, which forms involatile AlF₃[^hess-1982] —
  the problem Texas Instruments' etch-stop patent for fluorine-bearing
  via etches addresses.[^pat-etchstop-ti]
* **A profile the metal can fill.** On the reading at
  {ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`, sputtered aluminium must cover the via wall
  and bottom corners; Skelly and Gruenke showed how strongly coverage
  in straight-walled vias depends on the deposition
  conditions,[^skelly-1986] and a rounded top corner or a slope eases
  it (inference from the tapering
  literature[^bar-2002][^kim-2011][^pat-taper-chartered]).
* **A dry via.** Water left in the dielectric or adsorbed on the wall
  outgasses during the hot metal deposition: Taguchi, Maeda and Aoyama
  improved aluminium via filling by controlling water outgassing from
  via holes,[^taguchi-1998] and Kobayakawa et al. and Romero et al.
  studied the outgassing of spin-on-glass films.[^kobayakawa-1991][^romero-1991]
* **Charging of the capacitor.** This etch is the step at which each
  `cap2m` top plate is first opened to the plasma; Wang, Ackaert et al.
  showed that {term}`plasma-charging <plasma charging>` damage of floating MiM capacitors depends
  on the antenna areas connected to each plate,[^wang-2004-mim] and Fang
  and McVittie described the thin-dielectric damage such charging
  causes.[^fang-1992]

Without `VIM4E` the resist pattern of {ref}`VIM4 <step-159>` would be
stripped without effect, metal 5 would sit on unbroken oxide, and the
second capacitor would have no top-plate terminal.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic large-via dielectric etch for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era
fab (SKY130's recipe is not public); the shared physics is at
{ref}`CTME <step-108>` and {ref}`VIME <step-119>`.

1. **Chamber.** A single-wafer dielectric etcher — medium-density
   capacitively coupled or high-density inductively coupled, with
   separate bias, helium backside cooling and optical-emission endpoint
   ({ref}`category-etch`); Lam's Exelan was launched for this generation
   in 2001.[^lam-exelan]
2. **BARC open.** If a {term}`BARC` was used at {ref}`VIM4 <step-159>`,
   a short O₂/N₂ or CF₄-containing step opens it.
3. **Profile step (optional).** For a sloped top, an isotropic or
   resist-eroding step first — the alternating oxide/resist etches of the
   Motorola patent,[^pat-sloped-motorola] or a resist pull-back as in the
   Chartered patent[^pat-taper-chartered] — then the anisotropic main etch
   (industry practice; whether SKY130 does this is not public).
4. **Main oxide etch.** A polymerising fluorocarbon — C₄F₈, C₂F₆ or CHF₃
   with argon and a little O₂ or CO — at tens of mTorr;[^oehrlein-1994b][^perry-2001]
   Standaert et al. describe etching through a steady-state
   fluorocarbon layer on a non-oxide surface,[^standaert-1998] and
   Schaepkens et al. the fluorocarbon-film mechanism behind the
   selectivity of oxide etching to nitride and
   silicon.[^schaepkens-1999]
5. **Endpoint and {term}`over-etch`.** Emission endpoint as the oxide clears
   over metal 4, or a time set from the thickness the polish and cap
   left ({ref}`CMPM4 <step-157>`, {ref}`NCAPOX6 <step-158>`), with an
   over-etch sized for the deepest vias and a chemistry lean enough in
   fluorine to protect the TiW.[^petri-1992]
6. **Strip and clean.** Oxygen or O₂/N₂ downstream {term}`ash` — SkyWater
   lists "Gasonic PEP", Iridia and Mattson Aspen II strippers[^skw-01] —
   then a solvent clean ("EKS265, EKC270 solvents"[^skw-01]) to remove
   fluorocarbon polymer and fluoride residue from both floors without
   attacking TiW; no hydrogen peroxide, which etches TiW as Danzl and
   McLaurin used it to[^danzl-1997] (inference).
7. **Queue time and dehydration.** A limited delay before
   {ref}`WTIAL5 <step-161>`, whose degas chamber drives adsorbed water
   from the vias before the metal is deposited (industry
   practice;[^txt-05] the outgassing literature is the
   reason[^taguchi-1998]).
8. **Metrology.** Top and bottom CD by {term}`CD-SEM`; wall angle and TiW
   loss on both floors by cross-section SEM on monitors; via-4 chain
   resistance to metal 4 and to capacitor plates at {term}`e-test` after
   metal 5, against the PDK's 380 mΩ `via4`.[^pdk-08]

## Machines typically used

* **Dielectric etcher**, 200 mm single-wafer: Lam Exelan,[^lam-exelan]
  Applied Materials MxP+ class, TEL Unity ({ref}`category-etch`).
* **Resist asher** and **solvent wet bench** for post-etch residue.
* **CD-SEM**, **cross-section SEM**, **e-test** for via chains.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **No dielectric etcher is named on SkyWater's public list.** The list
  gives, under poly/silicon etch, "AMAT DPSII, HBR, Cl2, NF3, CF4, CHF3,
  O2", "Lam 9400 TCP, poly/nitride, HBr, CF4, SF6, O2" and "Lam 4400,
  HBr, Cl2, C2F6, CF4, SF6, O2".[^skw-01] All three carry
  fluorine-bearing gases that can etch oxide; strength: **weak** for
  assignment of any of them to the via etch.
* **Lam Exelan.** No public source places an Exelan at SkyWater; it
  appears here only as the era's typical dielectric etcher, a line Lam's
  own 10-K lists.[^lam-10k]
* **Strip and clean — "Gasonic PEP", "Iridia RF microwave", "Mattson
  Aspen2"; batch rotational tools with "EKS265, EKC270
  solvents".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence; assignment is an
  inference.

## Resources required

* **C₄F₈, C₂F₆, CHF₃, CF₄**, **Ar**, **O₂** (and possibly CO) for the
  oxide etch (industry practice;[^nojiri-2015] SkyWater lists CF₄, CHF₃,
  C₂F₆ and O₂ on its etchers[^skw-01]).
* **Helium** for backside cooling; **NF₃** or O₂-based chamber clean.
* **O₂/N₂** for the ash;[^skw-01] **amine or semi-aqueous solvent** and DI
  water for the post-etch clean.[^skw-01]
* **Chamber consumables**; **monitor wafers** with blanket oxide over
  TiW for rate and selectivity checks.

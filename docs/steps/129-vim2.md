## What this step is

`VIM2` is the via-2 lithography: the mask step that defines the holes
through which metal 3 will reach metal 2. On the cap oxide of
{ref}`NCAPOX4 <step-128>`, which seals the polished
{ref}`NILD4 <step-126>` dielectric, a resist is coated, exposed and
developed so that a square opening stands over every place the
layout draws a `via2` — GDS 69:44, "Contact from metal 2 to
metal 3".[^pdk-06] The holes are etched at {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>`,
lined at {ref}`TIN4 <step-131>`, filled at {ref}`WDEP4 <step-132>` and
polished at {ref}`WCMP4 <step-133>`. The PDK's mask table lists the
`VIM2` mask three times — "Via 2-TNV", "Via 2-S8TM" and "Via 2-PLM" —
and flags only the PLM variant as used in SKY130;[^pdk-05] the
minimum-CD table gives the three their sizes, 0.28 µm, 0.8 µm and
0.2 µm respectively, as `VIM2CD`/`VIM2CDSP`.[^pdk-03] The via 2 of
the flow described here is therefore the 0.2 µm one, and the 0.8 µm
"S8TM" variant belongs, on our reading, to the thick-metal-3 option
discussed at {ref}`WTIAL3 <step-134>`.

The periphery rules give "Min and max L and W of via2" 0.200 µm
(via2.1a), with larger squares of 0.280, 1.2 and 1.5 µm permitted
inside `areaid.mt` (via2.1b) — the layer the PDK defines as the
"Location of e-test modules within the frame"[^pdk-06] — a spacing of
0.200 µm (via2.2), the rule that "Only min. square via2s are allowed
except die seal ring" (via2.3), and enclosures by metal 2 of 0.040 µm
(via2.4) and 0.085 µm on one of two adjacent sides (via2.5); metal 3
must enclose the via by 0.065 µm (m3.4).[^pdk-periph] The rule
table's own statement of purpose is that "Via2 connects met2 to met3
in the SKY130T*/SKY130P*/SP8Q/SP8P* flows and met2/capm to met3 in
the SKY130DI* flow"[^pdk-periph] — the latter a variant in which a
MiM capacitor plate sits at this level. The extraction tables put a
finished `via2` at 3 410 mΩ (3.4 Ω), below the 4 500 mΩ of a
`via`.[^pdk-08]

The hole is both larger and deeper than via 1: 0.20 µm wide against
0.15 µm, and 0.42 µm deep against 0.27 µm,[^pdk-04] an
{term}`aspect ratio` of about 2.1:1 as drawn. Its floor is the top of
the metal-2 stack — on the public description of the S8 process, a
300 Å TiW cap over Al–0.5%Cu[^cyp-qtp-113005] — as at via 1.

## Step category

`VIM2` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *contact-hole* type, like {ref}`VIM <step-118>` and
{ref}`CTM1 <step-107>`, which set out the imaging problem of an
isolated small square.[^mack-2007] It is the loosest of the three: at
0.20 µm on a 0.40 µm pitch, a 248 nm tool of NA 0.70 (ASML's PAS
5500/750E[^asml-750e]) gives a process factor
{math}`k_1 = 0.20 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.56`, comfortable for
holes without phase shifting; an i-line tool at 365 nm and NA 0.63
would give only 0.35, below what contact holes can be printed at
in production ({ref}`category-lithography`). We therefore infer that
`VIM2` is exposed on the "ASML DUV stepper" or "ASML DUV scanner"
SkyWater lists,[^skw-01] like the two hole masks below it, with a
chemically amplified resist ({term}`CAR`) and {term}`OPC`; nothing
public states the tool or resist.

The process-steps sheet does record a mask type for the via-2 plate.
A tab headed "Sheet4" gives a coded mask type for three masks
only,[^steps-sheet] and, as the {ref}`mask index <masks-index>` does,
we read the codes for the via 2 (`VIM2`) and via 3 (`VIM3`) plates as
denoting embedded {term}`attenuated phase-shift masks <attenuated PSM>`
for 248 nm exposure: the codes use the abbreviation a mask maker uses
for this type, "EAPSM" for "embedded attenuated phase-shift masks" at
248 nm and 193 nm.[^photronics-abr] We also read a digit 4 in each
code, less certainly, as the 4× reduction ratio; the tab does not
define its codes, does not say to which runs these types apply, and
names no exposure tool, resist or tone. A mask made for 248 nm fits
the DUV reading above (inference), and a phase-shift type widens the
process window[^chen-1999-psm] of a hole that the {math}`k_1`
arithmetic says could be printed without one.
The substrate is again a thin oxide over a patterned reflective metal,
so the pattern-dependent reflection discussed at
{ref}`VIM <step-118>` and Brunner's swing-curve analysis[^brunner-1991]
apply, now through 0.42 µm of oxide.

## Why this step exists

Every connection between metal 2 and metal 3 passes through a
`via2`; the step fixes what the plug that follows inherits:

* **Hole size.** The drawn 0.200 µm is the top opening; with an
  aspect ratio above 2:1 the etch of {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` and the
  liner of {ref}`TIN4 <step-131>` need a hole printed close to size,
  and the 3.4 Ω of the PDK's `via2`[^pdk-08] — lower than via 1's
  4.5 Ω, consistent with the larger area (our arithmetic) — is
  measured on plugs whose bottom this mask sets. Le, Banerjee and
  McPherson showed that the electromigration performance of a
  tungsten-plug via depends on its size.[^le-1996]
* **Placement.** With 0.040 µm of metal-2 enclosure (via2.4) and
  0.065 µm of metal 3 (m3.4),[^pdk-periph] the {term}`overlay` of this
  layer to {ref}`MM2 <step-124>` works to an enclosure that is tighter
  than via 1's 0.055 µm, though looser than the contact's 0.030 µm
  (m1.4),[^pdk-periph] even though the hole is larger; a via that
  slips off its 0.14 µm metal-2 line lands on oxide and etches a
  trench beside it. Levinson treats how such
  budgets are allocated.[^levinson-2005]
* **Uniformity through pitch.** Every via is the same square
  (via2.1a, via2.3),[^pdk-periph] printed isolated and in arrays; the
  proximity behaviour of contact arrays is why hole layers received
  {term}`OPC` and assist features early.[^yamamoto-2000][^socha-2004][^wiki-opc]
* **Coupling to the thick-metal option.** The mask table's three
  `VIM2` variants[^pdk-05] and the assumptions table's three via-2
  sizes[^pdk-03] show that this level is where the flow forks: a
  0.8 µm via under a 2 µm metal 3 is a different lithography and a
  different fill from a 0.2 µm via under a 0.845 µm one.

Without `VIM2` there would be no holes for {ref}`VIM2E <step-130>` to
etch and metal 3 would float above metal 2 on unbroken oxide.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic 248 nm via-hole lithography sequence for a
200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public); {ref}`VIM <step-118>`
and {ref}`CTM1 <step-107>` describe the shared steps at greater
length.

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake and {term}`HMDS` prime on
   the fresh {ref}`NCAPOX4 <step-128>` oxide, then an organic
   {term}`BARC` of a few tens of nanometres to suppress the
   pattern-dependent reflection from the buried metal.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive-tone chemically amplified
   resist[^ito-2005][^wallraff-1999] of a few hundred nanometres — a
   thicker film than at via 1 is affordable at 0.2 µm, and helps the
   deeper etch (inference); the PDK's 1.14 µm[^pdk-03] is an
   antenna-rule assumption. The track's ammonia-filtered
   environment[^skw-01] protects the resist.[^hinsberg-1992]
3. **Exposure.** KrF at NA 0.7–0.8.[^asml-750e][^asml-800] At
   {math}`k_1 \approx 0.56` a binary reticle with {term}`OPC` and
   modest assist features[^yamamoto-2000][^socha-2004] is the era's
   usual choice for a hole layer of this size; an attenuated
   PSM[^chen-1999-psm][^lu-1999] widens the process window further.
   The process-steps sheet records the latter for the via-2 plate —
   an embedded attenuated PSM for 248 nm exposure, on our reading of
   its code;[^steps-sheet][^photronics-abr] whether OPC or assist
   features are added is not public.
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB`, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH
   develop, rinse and dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`MM2 <step-124>` metal-2 pattern, on
   targets now buried under 0.42 µm of oxide; the 0.040 µm enclosure
   (via2.4)[^pdk-periph] is the registration that matters.
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Hole CD by {term}`CD-SEM`, overlay on
   the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class SkyWater lists,[^skw-01]
   after-develop inspection for missing or scummed holes; dose and
   focus separated by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999]

## Resources required

* **248 nm positive chemically amplified photoresist** ({ref}`lithography materials <material-lithography-materials>`); SkyWater's 2021 S-1
  names Dow, JSR and Tokyo Ohka Kogyo as photoresist
  suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **Organic BARC**, **HMDS**, **2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH developer** (the 2021
  S-1 names "Air Products & Chemicals, Inc., Moses Lake" for
  developer[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse solvents, {ref}`DI water <material-ultrapure-water>` and
  {ref}`nitrogen <material-process-gases>`.[^txt-02]
* **The VIM2 reticle** — typically a 6-inch quartz reticle, generated
  from the drawn `via2` layer (69:44);[^pdk-06] an embedded attenuated
  phase-shift mask for 248 nm exposure, on our reading of the type the
  process-steps sheet records for this plate.[^steps-sheet][^photronics-abr]
* **KrF excimer laser gas** for the exposure tool.

## Open questions

* The process-steps sheet records a mask type for the via-2 plate,
  which we read as an embedded attenuated PSM for 248 nm
  exposure,[^steps-sheet] but not the exposure tool or its model, the
  resist or its tone, the BARC or whether OPC is applied; DUV exposure
  with OPC is an inference from the 0.2 µm hole and the reticle type.
  The tab does not define its codes (the 4× reading is less certain)
  or say to which runs, or to which of the three `VIM2`
  variants,[^pdk-05] the type applies.
* The PDK does not define "TNV", "S8TM" or "PLM" in its three `VIM2`
  entries.[^pdk-05] That S8TM is the thick-metal-3 option follows from
  the assumptions table's thickness entries[^pdk-03] (inference). The
  mask table flags the "-PLM" entries as used in SKY130[^pdk-05] and
  the rule tables name an "SP8P*/SKY130P* (PLM) CADflow";[^pdk-periph]
  this reference reads PLM as the label of the five-metal P flow
  (inference). TNV is unexplained.
* Whether the via-2 lithography is shared with the "SKY130DI*" flow's
  `capm` landing[^pdk-periph] is not relevant to the flow described
  here, whose MiM plate is on metal 3 ({ref}`CAPM <step-137>`).
* Whether a chemical-shrink or resist-trim step is used is not
  public; at 0.2 µm it is unlikely to be needed (inference).

## What this step is

`ONO` forms the charge-storing gate dielectric of the {term}`SONOS` memory
transistor: an oxide–nitride–oxide sandwich ({term}`ONO`) in which a
very thin *{term}`tunnel oxide`* is grown on the silicon exposed at
{ref}`TUNME <step-039>`, a silicon-nitride (or silicon-oxynitride)
*{term}`charge-trapping layer`* is deposited on it by {term}`LPCVD`, and a
*blocking* (top) oxide is formed over the nitride. This reference files
it under thermal oxidation because the sequence begins with one; in
practice it is, we infer, a furnace sequence that mixes oxidation and
deposition (SkyWater lists an oxide/nitride/oxide furnace
capability[^skw-01]), and this reference describes the whole stack as
one step. The whole
wafer is coated — the stack lies on bare silicon in the tunnel windows
and on the (inferred) pad oxide everywhere else — and it is removed
again from everything except the memory cells at
{ref}`ONOM <step-041>`/{ref}`ONOME <step-042>`.

The public description of the stack comes from Cypress's patents and
articles. Wikipedia's generic SONOS figures are "a 2 nm thick oxide
lower layer, a 5 nm thick silicon nitride middle layer, and a 5–10 nm
oxide upper layer".[^cyp-26] A Cypress ONO patent describes several
embodiments, not one recipe: in one, a tunnel dielectric of "less than
about 25 Å" and a charge storing layer of "less than about 200 Å,
preferably less than about 150 Å, more preferably less than about
100 Å", formed by LPCVD from base materials that "may include
ammonia (NH₃), dichlorosilane (DCS), and/or nitrous oxide (N₂O)"; in
another, a
{term}`top insulating layer <blocking oxide>` of "about 100 Å, preferably
less than about 50 Å"; and in a third, all three layers formed at
temperatures "from about 700° C. to about 875° C.", the top layer by
LPCVD from "dichlorosilane (DCS), and nitrous oxide (N₂O)";[^pat-01] a
later one gives a tunnel oxide "from about 15 angstrom (Å) to about
22 Å", a multi-layer trapping layer of "about 70 Å to about 150 Å" and a
blocking oxide of "about 30 Å to about 70 Å".[^pat-02] The integration
patent describes a "tunneling layer 304A" of "between about 1.5 nm and
3.0 nm", {term}`oxynitride` trapping layers deposited from "N₂O, NH₃ and
SiH₂Cl₂" at "5-500 mTorr" and "700-850° C." for "2.5-20 minutes", and a
blocking layer of "between about 3.0 nm and about 5.0 nm".[^pat-03] For
the SKY130 stack itself no thickness is public; these are the bounds
within which it presumably lies.

## Step category

`ONO` is a {ref}`Thermal oxidation <category-oxidation>` step of the
*gate-quality thin oxide* class — the tunnel oxide is, on the published
patent ranges, the thinnest oxide in the SONOS module — the Cypress
patents put it at 1.0–3.0 nm for thermal and 1.0–4.0 nm for radical
oxidation[^pat-04] and 1.5–3.0 nm[^pat-03] — and
thinner than the 1.8 V gate oxide of {ref}`LVGOX <step-047>` (an
inference: SKY130 publishes none of these thicknesses) — combined with
an LPCVD nitride deposition of
the kind described under {ref}`ISONIT <step-003>` and a top-oxide
formation. The category page's *Nitrided oxides and ONO stacks* section
gives the generic picture; this page gives the memory-specific one.

## Why this step exists

The ONO stack *is* the memory. "A SONOS memory uses an insulating
layer such as silicon nitride with traps as the charge storage
layer";[^cyp-24] "Charge injected from the Si substrate across the thin
tunnel oxide by Fowler-Nordheim tunneling is trapped in the Nitride (N)
layer", and "the trapped charge changes the threshold voltage (Vt) of
the CG between a 'Program (VTP)' and an 'Erase (VTE)' state".[^cyp-25]
Each layer has one job:

* **Tunnel oxide** — thin enough for {term}`Fowler–Nordheim <Fowler–Nordheim tunnelling>` (or direct)
  tunnelling at the PDK's programme/erase bias of 10.5 V across the cell
  (+6.7 V gate, −3.8 V on source/drain/body, or the reverse — on the
  reading of the published table's programme source bias set out on
  {ref}`PTSI <step-037>`),[^pdk-07]
  thick enough that the trapped charge does not leak back over the
  customary ten-year retention target.[^bu-2001] The scaling of SONOS
  retention with tunnel-oxide thickness is the central theme of White's
  group's work.[^french-1994][^yang-2000][^bu-2001]
* **Nitride** — a dense population of deep traps that hold the charge
  in place. Because the charge is trapped rather than stored on a
  conductor, "A short circuit created by an oxide defect between the
  charge trapping layer and the channel will drain off only the
  electrons in immediate contact with the short, leaving the other
  electrons in place".[^wiki-ctf] Cypress engineers the nitride as
  layers of different stoichiometry — an oxygen-rich layer next to
  the tunnel oxide and a silicon-rich, oxygen-lean layer above
  it[^pat-03][^pat-04] — so that the first "results in it being
  substantially trap free" and the second in "being trap
  dense",[^pat-radox-cyp] keeping the charge centroid away from the
  tunnel oxide.
* **Blocking oxide** — stops the gate from injecting or extracting
  charge and sets the field division across the stack.

The stack is formed *here*, after the wells and channel implants and
before the logic gate oxides, for a reason Cypress spells out: every
post-implant resist strip removes about 0.5 nm of oxide, "between
1.5 nm and 2.5 nm" over a flow, and "the well and channel implant
strips may also etch a non-volatile charge trapping dielectric
stack";[^pat-03] forming the ONO after the implants protects it. Forming
it *before* the logic gate oxide "has the advantage of additionally
serving to reoxidize the ONO charge trapping dielectric stack", which
"may have the effect of densifying a CVD formed blocking
oxide layer".[^pat-03] Without `ONO` there is no non-volatile memory; the PDK
guarantees "100K cycles" endurance and shows retention at 85 °C for
the cell that this stack makes.[^pdk-07]

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic ONO sequence for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era embedded
SONOS module (SKY130's recipe is not public; values are from the
Cypress patents unless marked typical):

1. **Pre-clean and load.** Wafers arrive from {ref}`TUNME <step-039>`
   with bare silicon in the tunnel windows. They are loaded into a
   vertical furnace under nitrogen; the whole sequence can run in one
   tool "without removing the substrate between operations".[^pat-03]
2. **Tunnel oxide.** Grown in the furnace by dry oxidation — "dry
   oxidation at 750 degrees centigrade (° C.)-800° C. in an oxygen
   containing gas … for a duration approximately in the range of 50 to
   150 minutes" for "about 1.0 nanometers (nm) to about 3.0 nm"[^pat-04]
   — or by radical (ISSG-type) oxidation, in which H₂ and O₂ "at a ratio
   to one another of approximately 1:1" react "at a temperature
   approximately in the range of about 900° C. to about 1000° C. at a
   pressure approximately in the range of about 0.5 to about 5 Torr" for
   "about 1 to about 10 minutes"; such an oxide "is both denser and is
   composed of substantially fewer hydrogen atoms/cm³ than a tunnel
   dielectric formed by wet oxidation" and can be grown "in a
   batch-processing chamber or furnace".[^pat-04] Growth in this regime
   is faster than {term}`Deal–Grove <Deal–Grove model>` predicts and is calibrated empirically on
   the tool ({ref}`category-oxidation`).[^massoud-1985] On the
   (inferred) pad oxide outside the windows the same step adds only a
   fraction of a nanometre.[^deal-1965]
3. **Charge-trapping nitride/oxynitride.** LPCVD from dichlorosilane
   and ammonia, with nitrous oxide added to make oxynitride: "N₂O, NH₃
   and SiH₂Cl₂" at "5-500 mTorr", "700-850° C.", "2.5-20 minutes",
   with "N₂O:NH₃" ratios of "about 1:1 to about 3:1" for the oxygen-rich
   layer and a "SiH₂Cl₂ to NH₃" ratio "from about 3.5:1 to 8:1" for the
   silicon-rich one.[^pat-03] The generic LPCVD nitride reaction[^wiki-sin] and
   the 700–850 °C, 5–500 mTorr window[^pat-02] are those of
   {ref}`ISONIT <step-003>`. Total trapping-layer thickness
   in the patents: 9–11 nm,[^pat-03] 7–15 nm,[^pat-02] and "less than
   about 200 Å, preferably less than about 150 Å, more preferably less
   than about 100 Å".[^pat-01]
4. **Blocking oxide.** Three routes appear in the public record:
   (a) a high-temperature LPCVD oxide ("HTO") from dichlorosilane and
   N₂O in the same furnace — the "LPCVD oxide/nitride/oxide"
   capability SkyWater lists;[^skw-01] (b) thermal or radical
   oxidation of a sacrificial top nitride "cap layer" so that the
   oxide is grown, not deposited, with the oxidation happening at the
   logic gate oxidation ({ref}`GOX100 <step-043>`);[^pat-04] (c) a
   {term}`CVD` oxide later "reoxidized or nitridized during a thermal
   oxidation or nitridation of a logic MOS gate insulator layer to
   densify the blocking layer".[^pat-03] A further Cypress patent,
   whose claimed embodiment forms the stack over a polysilicon
   channel, describes a radical-oxidation route to the top oxide, in
   which the blocking layer is formed by "a second oxidation process"
   that "can comprise a plasma oxidation process or a radical
   oxidation process".[^pat-radox-cyp] Thickness 3–5 nm.[^pat-03][^pat-04]
5. **Optional sacrificial cap.** A thin oxide "of between 2.0 nm and
   4.0 nm" over the stack, to be removed at the gate-oxide pre-clean,
   protects the blocking oxide through the ONO patterning.[^pat-04]
6. **Metrology.** Ellipsometry of each layer on monitor wafers
   (refractive index distinguishes oxide, oxynitride and nitride:
   ~1.45 for SiO₂, ~2.0 for Si₃N₄[^pat-04]); C–V and programme/erase
   window on the first {term}`e-test`.

The furnace {term}`thermal budget` of this step is also, we infer, what
activates the {ref}`PTSI <step-037>` and {ref}`DEPI <step-038>` implants;
no public source describes a separate anneal for them.

## Machines typically used

* **{ref}`Vertical LPCVD <machine-vertical-furnace-lpcvd>`/{ref}`oxidation furnace <machine-vertical-furnace-oxidation>`**, 200 mm, with O₂, H₂ (or
  H₂/O₂ for in-furnace {term}`radical oxidation`), DCS, NH₃ and N₂O gas
  panels: ASM A400, TEL Alpha-8, Aviza/Thermco
  ({ref}`category-oxidation`, {ref}`category-deposition`).
* **{ref}`Single-wafer <machine-rapid-thermal-processor>` {term}`RTP` with ISSG** as the alternative for the tunnel
  and blocking oxides: Applied Materials RTP Centura (Radiance);
  {term}`ISSG` is described on the category page and characterised in
  the literature.[^yu-1999][^roze-2017]
* **{ref}`Spectroscopic ellipsometer <machine-film-thickness-metrology>`**; **{ref}`C–V <machine-parametric-tester>`** test structures.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Aviza vertical furnaces.** SkyWater states "Furnaces are all made
  by Aviza" with "wet oxidation to 1150C", "dry oxidation to 1150C",
  "LPCVD nitride" and "LPCVD
  oxide/nitride/oxide".[^skw-01] A furnace capability listed under the
  name of this stack is the closest public tool evidence in the
  module; it does not say that SKY130's ONO is grown on it.
  Strength: **strong** for the tool and the ONO capability;
  the assignment of each layer of SKY130's stack to it is an
  **inference**. A dealer listing describes the Aviza AVP-8000 as "a
  vertical batch furnace with a flexible platform for diffusion,
  oxidation, and LPCVD processes … 150-200mm wafers".[^aviza-avp]
* **AG Associates Heatpulse 8808** ("NH3, Ar, N2, O2, up to
  1200C")[^skw-01] — a single-wafer RTP that could grow a tunnel oxide
  by rapid thermal oxidation or nitride it in NH₃. Strength: strong
  for existence; weak for assignment, since the ONO patents describe
  furnace processing.

## Open questions

* SKY130's tunnel, nitride and blocking thicknesses are not public;
  the Cypress patents bound them.
* Whether the tunnel oxide is grown dry, by in-furnace radical
  oxidation or by single-wafer ISSG is not public.
* Whether the blocking oxide is an LPCVD HTO, an oxidised nitride cap,
  or a CVD oxide densified at {ref}`GOX100 <step-043>` is not public;
  all three appear in Cypress's patents.
* Whether the trapping layer is a single nitride or the multi-layer
  oxynitride of the later patents is not public.
* Whether the pre-oxidation clean belongs to this step or to
  {ref}`TUNME <step-039>` is not stated publicly; this reference
  treats it as part of {ref}`TUNME <step-039>`.

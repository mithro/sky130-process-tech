## What this step is

`CTM1` is the lithography step that defines the *metal contacts* — the
holes through which metal 1 reaches the local interconnect. On the
CMP-planarised inter-level dielectric over the local interconnect (on
our reading of {ref}`CMPL <step-106>`), a resist is coated, exposed and
developed so that a small square opening stands over every place the
layout draws an `mcon`. The holes are etched at {ref}`CTME <step-108>`,
lined with titanium nitride at {ref}`TIN2 <step-109>`, filled with
tungsten at {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>` and polished flat at
{ref}`WCMP2 <step-111>`; the resulting plug is what the PDK calls
`mcon`, "Contact from local interconnect to metal1" (GDS
67:44).[^pdk-06] The PDK's mask table lists "Contact, CTM1" as used in
SKY130,[^pdk-05] so the step code is also the mask name. It is the
first {term}`BEOL` mask.

The public geometry is unusually complete for this layer. The
periphery rules give the contact a fixed size — "Min and max L and W
of mcon" 0.170 µm (ct.1), spacing 0.190 µm (ct.2), "Only min. square
mcons are allowed except die seal ring" (ct.3) — and require no
enclosure by the local interconnect at all (ct.4, 0.000 µm), while
metal 1 must enclose it by 0.030 µm (m1.4) and by 0.060 µm on one of
two adjacent sides (m1.5).[^pdk-periph] The minimum-CD table repeats
the numbers as `CTM1CD` 0.17 µm and `CTM1CDSP` 0.19 µm,[^pdk-03] and
the physical-criteria table adds a "min. etch and fill capability for
mcon" of 0.14 µm, an "Mcon enclosure by Li" of 0, a "Minimum mcon
overlap onto LI for reproducible contact resistance" of 0.12 µm, and
— in its laser-fuse table — a "Standard contact bottom CD" of
0.09 µm.[^pdk-03] The extraction tables give the finished contact a
resistance of 152 000 mΩ (152 Ω) each, an order of magnitude above the
15 000 mΩ of a `licon` and far above the 4 500 mΩ of a
`via`.[^pdk-08]

What lies under the resist is the {ref}`NILD2 <step-105>` oxide,
planarised at {ref}`CMPL <step-106>`; under that, the 0.10 µm local
interconnect (`li`, titanium nitride per the LITIN step name) with
its "LINT" nitride cap of 0.075 µm and relative permittivity 7.3 that
the stack diagram labels.[^pdk-04] The diagram places the top of `li`
at 1.0111 µm and the bottom of `met1` at 1.3761 µm above its
reference, a separation of 0.365 µm, while labelling the NILD2
dielectric 0.265 µm; the drawing is "not to scale" and does not say
how the labels combine, so we take the contact depth as roughly
0.3–0.37 µm (on our reading).[^pdk-04] A 0.17 µm hole of that depth
has an {term}`aspect ratio` of about 2:1 as drawn and steeper if the
bottom really narrows to 0.09 µm.

## Step category

`CTM1` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *contact-hole* type, the hardest class of pattern for a
projection printer: an isolated small square transmits little light,
has the smallest depth of focus of any feature at a given pitch, and
prints with a strong dependence on its neighbours.[^mack-2007]
At 0.17 µm on a 0.36 µm pitch it is, with {ref}`MM1 <step-113>`, one
of the two tightest back-end layers of the flow; the ITRS 2001
lithography chapter assigns 248 nm exposure to the critical layers of
the 130 nm generation,[^itrs-03] and the category page's reading is
that SKY130's contact, metal 1 and via 1 are among them. We therefore
infer a {term}`DUV` (KrF, 248 nm) exposure on the "ASML DUV stepper"
or "ASML DUV scanner" that SkyWater lists,[^skw-01] with a
chemically amplified resist ({term}`CAR`) rather than the novolak of
the implant masks. Nothing public states the tool or resist for this
layer.

## Why this step exists

Every transistor terminal, resistor head and capacitor plate that
metal 1 must reach is, in SKY130, first brought to a titanium nitride
local-interconnect pad ({ref}`LI1M <step-102>`); `mcon` is the only
path from `li` up to `met1`. No metal-1 contact, no circuit. The step
also fixes three things that the later plug steps inherit:

* **Contact size and profile.** The drawn 0.17 µm is the top opening;
  the resist profile and the etch that follows set how much the hole
  narrows on the way down. The PDK's "Standard contact bottom CD" of
  0.09 µm[^pdk-03] is, on our reading, a statement that the finished
  hole is markedly tapered — which eases the liner and tungsten fill
  ({ref}`TIN2 <step-109>`, {ref}`WDEP2 <step-110>`) at the price of
  contact area and hence the 152 Ω per contact of the extraction
  tables.[^pdk-08]
* **Placement.** The contact must land on `li` with zero enclosure
  (ct.4)[^pdk-periph] and be covered by metal 1 with only 0.030 µm to
  spare (m1.4),[^pdk-periph] so {term}`overlay` to the
  {ref}`LI1M <step-102>` pattern and, one level up, of
  {ref}`MM1 <step-113>` to this pattern are both tight; Levinson
  treats how such overlay budgets are allocated.[^levinson-2005]
* **Uniformity.** Because every `mcon` is the same square (ct.1,
  ct.3),[^pdk-periph] the layer prints one feature at many pitches;
  the through-pitch behaviour of a contact array is the classic
  optical-proximity problem, and the reason contact layers were among
  the first to receive {term}`OPC`.[^yamamoto-2000][^wiki-opc]

Without `CTM1` there would be no holes for {ref}`CTME <step-108>` to
etch and the whole metal system would be isolated from the devices.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic 248 nm contact-hole lithography sequence for a
200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public):

1. **Surface preparation.** The wafer arrives from the
   {ref}`CMPL <step-106>` polish and its post-CMP clean; a dehydration
   bake and {term}`HMDS` prime, or a {term}`BARC`, precede the coat.
   On a flat oxide the reflectivity problem is the thin-film swing
   curve rather than topography, and an organic BARC of a few tens of
   nanometres is the usual cure.[^brunner-1991][^wiki-arc]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive-tone {term}`CAR` — the chemistry Ito
   and Willson introduced[^ito-1983] and Ito later reviewed[^ito-2005]
   — spun to a few hundred nanometres for a contact layer (the PDK's
   single nominal resist thickness is 1.14 µm,[^pdk-03] an
   antenna-rule assumption that need not describe this layer).
   Chemically amplified resists are sensitive to airborne amines and
   to the substrate they sit on;[^hinsberg-1992][^sturtevant-1994] the
   track ammonia clean SkyWater lists[^skw-01] is the kind of
   countermeasure that implies.
3. **Exposure.** KrF at 248 nm on a stepper or scanner of numerical
   aperture 0.6–0.8 (ASML's PAS 5500/750E offered NA 0.70 and 130 nm
   resolution in 2000; the /800 NA 0.80[^asml-750e][^asml-800]). For
   a 0.17 µm hole at NA 0.7 the process factor
   {math}`k_1 = 0.17 \times 0.70 / 0.248 \approx 0.48` is
   comfortable for lines but not for isolated holes, whose depth of
   focus is much smaller; the era's answers were an
   {term}`attenuated PSM` (industry practice; ITRS 2001 names
   "attenuated shifter" as a primary PSM choice[^itrs-03][^wiki-psm]),
   contact-specific resists chosen for compatibility with it,[^lu-1999]
   {term}`OPC` and assist features,[^yamamoto-2000][^socha-2004] and
   focus-latitude tricks such as FLEX.[^fukuda-1987] Whether SKY130
   uses any of these is an inference from the geometry; the drawn
   0.17 µm is also close to where fabs of the period turned to
   chemical shrink (RELACS) to print sub-resolution holes.[^toyoshima-1998]
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** {term}`PEB` on a hot plate to
   drive the acid-catalysed deprotection, then 0.26 N TMAH develop,
   rinse and dry;[^wallraff-1999][^txt-02] the {term}`PEB` temperature
   and delay are the tightest controls of a CAR process.
5. **Alignment.** To the {ref}`LI1M <step-102>` pattern, with
   box-in-box or grating targets in the {term}`scribe line`; the zero
   `li` enclosure (ct.4)[^pdk-periph] makes this the critical
   registration of the layer.
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Hole CD by {term}`CD-SEM` (an SEM is
   needed at this size), overlay on the KLA-class tools SkyWater
   lists,[^skw-01] after-develop inspection for missing or scummed
   holes, with dose and focus separated by the method Ausschnitt
   describes.[^ausschnitt-1999]

## Machines typically used

* **KrF step-and-scan or stepper**, 200 mm: ASML PAS 5500/700–/800
  series, Nikon NSR-S204B (248 nm scanner),[^nikon-s204b] Canon
  FPA-5000ES ({ref}`category-lithography`).
* **Coat/develop track** with BARC coat, chill and PEB modules (TEL
  ACT/Lithius, DNS, SVG/ASML, Sokudo).[^tel-act]
* **CD-SEM** (Hitachi S-9200 class,[^semimarket-s9200] KLA 8100,
  AMAT VeraSEM), **overlay tool** (KLA 5200/Archer), **after-develop
  inspection**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **ASML DUV stepper / DUV scanner.** SkyWater lists "ASML DUV
  stepper" and "ASML DUV scanner" among its lithography
  tools.[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for existence; the assignment
  of the contact layer to DUV is an **inference** from the 0.17 µm
  rule and the ITRS layer allocation.[^itrs-03] The model is not
  public.
* **Tracks — DNS 80B, Sokudo RF3, TEL ProZ/Lithius** and a "Track
  ammonia clean".[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence.
* **CD — AMAT Verity, AMAT VeraSEM; overlay — KLA
  5200/5300/Archer.**[^skw-01] Strength: strong.

## Resources required

* **248 nm positive chemically amplified photoresist**; SkyWater's S-1
  names Dow, JSR and Tokyo Ohka Kogyo as photoresist
  suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **Organic BARC**, **HMDS**, **0.26 N TMAH developer** (developer
  from Air Products per the S-1[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse
  solvents, DI water and nitrogen.[^txt-02]
* **The CTM1 reticle** — on our reading an attenuated phase-shift
  type with OPC, generated from the drawn `mcon` layer (67:44).[^pdk-06]
* **KrF excimer laser gas** (Kr/F₂/Ne fills) for the exposure tool.

## Open questions

* The exposure tool, wavelength, resist, BARC and reticle type
  (binary or attenuated PSM, with or without OPC) for `CTM1` are not
  public; DUV with an attenuated PSM and OPC is an inference from the
  0.17 µm/0.19 µm rules and industry practice of the period.
* Whether the "Standard contact bottom CD" of 0.09 µm in the PDK's
  laser-fuse table[^pdk-03] describes every `mcon` or only those near
  fuses is not stated; we read it as the generic finished profile.
* How the 0.365 µm li-top-to-met1-bottom separation of the stack
  diagram relates to its 0.265 µm NILD2 label[^pdk-04] — and hence
  the exact contact depth — is not public.
* Whether a chemical-shrink or resist-trim step is used to reach the
  printed contact size is not public.

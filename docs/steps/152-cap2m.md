|---|---|

| **Step code** | `CAP2M` |
| **Category** | {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>` |
| **Phase** | BEOL — MiM capacitors, metal 3–5, via 3–4 |
| **Previous step** | {ref}`CAPTIW2 <step-151>` |
| **Next step** | {ref}`CAP2ME <step-153>` |

## What this step is

`CAP2M` is the lithography that defines the top plates of the second
{term}`MiM capacitor`. On the blanket plate film of {ref}`CAPTIW2 <step-151>` — which
lies on the thin dielectric of {ref}`CAPILD2 <step-150>` and the
still-unpatterned metal-4 stack of {ref}`WTIAL4 <step-149>` — a resist
is coated, exposed and developed so that resist remains wherever the
layout draws `cap2m`, "MiM capacitor plate over metal 4" (GDS
97:44),[^pdk-06] and is cleared everywhere else. The
{ref}`CAP2ME <step-153>` etch then removes the exposed film and leaves
the plates. It is one of the two capacitor-plate masks of the flow, with
{ref}`CAPM <step-137>`, and like that one it is printed on a metal that
is itself patterned afterwards ({ref}`MM4 <step-154>`).

Less is public about this mask than about any neighbour. The PDK's
mask table lists "Capacitor MiM, CAPM" but has no entry for a second
capacitor mask;[^pdk-05] the minimum-CD table gives `CAPMCD` 2 µm and
`CAPMCDSP` 0.84 µm and nothing for `cap2m`;[^pdk-03] and the periphery
rules have a `capm` rule set — with every value shown as "N/A" — and
no `cap2m` rule set.[^pdk-periph] What is public is that the layer
exists (97:44[^pdk-06]), that the device page lists "CAP2M over
Metal-4" as a construction "identical" to "CAPM over Metal-3",[^pdk-07]
that the second capacitor has its own area and periphery capacitances
(`CMIM2A` 2 fF/µm², `CMIM2P` 0.19 fF/µm),[^pdk-07] and that the stack
diagram draws `cap2m` between `metal4` and `metal5`.[^pdk-04] Because
the constructions are called identical, we read the plate geometry as
that of `capm` — 2 µm minimum width and 0.84 µm minimum space — and
the mask as a non-critical, large-feature layer (inference). The PDK's
inconsistencies about which metal the MiM capacitors sit on are set out
at {ref}`CAPILD2 <step-150>`.

The surface is the same kind as at {ref}`CAPM <step-137>`: flat,
since the metal-4 stack was sputtered onto the polished via-3 level of
{ref}`WCMP5 <step-148>`; highly reflective (a refractory plate film
over aluminium, on this reference's reading); and thermally and
chemically delicate, with only some 20–30 nm of
dielectric (our estimate at {ref}`CAPILD2 <step-150>`) between the
resist's substrate and the aluminium below.

## Step category

`CAP2M` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *non-critical, large-feature* type. At the 0.84 µm `capm`
space on an i-line (365 nm) stepper of {term}`NA` 0.6 the process factor
{term}`k₁ <k1>` would be {math}`k_1 = 0.84 \times 0.6 / 0.365 \approx 1.4` (our
arithmetic, with the geometry assumed equal to `capm`[^pdk-03]), far
above the resolution limit; the ITRS 2001 lithography chapter assigns
such layers to the cheaper exposure tools of a generation.[^itrs-03]
We therefore infer that `CAP2M` is printed on the "ASML I-line stepper"
or "ASML I-line scanner" SkyWater lists,[^skw-01] with a conventional
DNQ/novolak resist. Nothing public states the tool or resist.

What is specific to this instance is its place in a stack. If the
layout stacks the two capacitors, as the device page
allows,[^pdk-07] this plate is drawn over a `capm` plate one level
below, and the PDK's stacked cross-section draws the two plates with
the same outline.[^pdk-07] A {term}`CD` error on either plate changes
the capacitance of its half — 0.1 µm of bias on a 2 µm plate is a 10 %
change for that half (our arithmetic) — and a misregistration between
the two changes the area over which their fringing fields overlap
(inference from the geometry). The mask-to-etch bias must be absorbed
in the `CMIM2P` periphery term, as it is in `CMIMP` for the first
capacitor (inference from the model form[^pdk-07]).

## Why this step exists

* **The plate area is the capacitance.** The PDK models the second
  capacitor as `CMIM2A` × area plus `CMIM2P` × perimeter;[^pdk-07] this
  mask and the etch that follows fix that area on the wafer. The PDK's
  MiM-capacitor aspect-ratio limit of 20 (`MiM_AR`)[^pdk-03] and the
  rectangles-only `capm` rule (capm.7)[^pdk-periph] are published for
  the first capacitor; on our reading the same limits apply here
  (inference from the "identical" construction[^pdk-07]).
* **Top plate first, bottom plate later.** Printing `cap2m` on the
  unpatterned metal puts the resist on a flat, continuous film and lands
  every plate edge on dielectric over metal. The Newport Fab patent
  patterns its top plate on unpatterned interconnect metal and the
  bottom plate afterwards,[^pat-mim-newportfab] and the sequence described in this reference does the same at both capacitor levels.
* **A second capacitor over the first.** The Newport Fab composite-MiM
  patent stacks an upper and a lower MiM between interconnect levels
  and joins them in parallel,[^pat-mim-stack-newportfab] and the TSMC
  patent cross-connects stacked plates through filled
  vias;[^pat-mim-stack-tsmc] the PDK's device page offers the SKY130 form
  of the same idea ("may be stacked to maximize total
  capacitance").[^pdk-07] Ng et al. review MiM integration in Al–Cu and
  copper back ends.[^ng-2005]
* **Alignment and enclosure.** Each plate must lie inside a metal-4
  shape patterned later and must enclose the via-4 openings that contact
  it; the PDK publishes no `cap2m` enclosure values.[^pdk-periph] Because
  metal 4 has not yet been printed, `CAP2M` is aligned to the last
  patterned layer — the via-3 holes of {ref}`VIM3 <step-144>` under the
  metal — and {ref}`MM4 <step-154>` is later aligned to the same marks
  or to `cap2m` itself (inference; Levinson treats the overlay
  budget[^levinson-2005]). A plate that must also sit over a `capm`
  plate adds a third registration (inference).
* **Plate area and {term}`plasma charging`.** Wang, Ackaert et al. showed that
  plasma-charging damage of floating MiM capacitors depends on the
  antenna areas connected to each plate,[^wang-2004-mim] so the plate
  area this mask draws is also one term in the charging exposure of
  every later plasma step until via 4 connects it.

Without `CAP2M` the plate film of {ref}`CAPTIW2 <step-151>` would stay a
blanket sheet and be removed, with the dielectric, at
{ref}`MM4E <step-155>`; there would be no second capacitor.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic large-feature lithography step on a reflective
refractory film for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not public);
{ref}`CAPM <step-137>` describes the same sequence.

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake, then an organic
   {term}`BARC` or a dyed resist to control the reflectivity of
   the plate film over aluminium — Brunner's {term}`swing-curve <swing curve>` analysis is the
   basis,[^brunner-1991] and Rocke and Schneegans documented the
   anti-reflective role of a refractory cap on aluminium.[^rocke-1988]
2. **Resist coat.** A positive i-line DNQ/novolak resist — the chemistry
   of Dammel's tutorial text and Reichmanis and Thompson's
   review[^dammel-1993][^reichmanis-1989][^wiki-dnq] — around 1 µm thick
   (industry-typical[^txt-02]); the PDK's 1.14 µm "Photoresist
   thickness" is an antenna-rule assumption and need not describe this
   layer.[^pdk-03]
3. **Exposure.** i-line on a 5× or 4× stepper of NA 0.5–0.6 — the ASML
   PAS 5500 family spans i-line and DUV models on one
   platform[^asml-30][^chiphistory-pas5500] — with conventional
   illumination; large features need no resolution enhancement
   (inference from the geometry[^itrs-03]).
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** A {term}`PEB` to smooth standing
   waves, then 0.26 N TMAH develop, rinse and dry.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To marks under the opaque metal-4 stack, read through
   the topography they print into the sputtered films (industry
   practice[^levinson-2005]).
6. **Inspection and metrology.** Plate {term}`CD` by optical CD or
   {term}`CD-SEM` on the AMAT Verity/VeraSEM class SkyWater
   lists;[^skw-01] overlay on the KLA 5200/5300/Archer class;[^skw-01]
   after-develop inspection for residue over the plates; dose and focus
   tracked by Ausschnitt's method.[^ausschnitt-1999]

## Machines typically used

* **i-line stepper or scanner**, 200 mm: ASML PAS 5500 i-line
  class,[^asml-30][^chiphistory-pas5500] Nikon NSR-i and Canon FPA i-line
  systems ({ref}`category-lithography`).
* **Coat/develop track** (TEL ACT/Lithius,[^tel-act] DNS, Sokudo).
* **Optical CD / CD-SEM**, **overlay tool**, **after-develop
  inspection**.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **ASML I-line stepper / I-line scanner.** SkyWater lists both.[^skw-01]
  Strength: **strong** for existence; the assignment of `CAP2M` to
  i-line is an **inference** from the `capm` rules[^pdk-03] applied to
  an "identical" construction[^pdk-07] and the ITRS layer
  allocation.[^itrs-03]
* **Tracks — "DNS 80B track", "Sokudo RF3 track", "TEL ProZ Lithius
  track".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence.
* **CD — "AMAT Verity (multiple versions) CD", "AMAT VeraSEM"; overlay —
  "KLA 5200/5300/Archer overlay".**[^skw-01] Strength: strong.

## Resources required

* **i-line positive photoresist** (DNQ/novolak); SkyWater's S-1 names
  Dow, JSR and Tokyo Ohka Kogyo as photoresist suppliers.[^sec-01]
* **Organic BARC** or dyed resist, **0.26 N TMAH developer** (Air
  Products per the S-1[^sec-01]), edge-bead remover, rinse solvents, DI
  water and nitrogen.[^txt-02]
* **The capacitor-2 reticle**, generated from the drawn `cap2m` layer
  (97:44)[^pdk-06] — on our reading a binary chrome-on-glass mask
  without OPC.
* **Mercury-lamp consumables** for the i-line tool.

## Open questions

* The PDK's mask table, minimum-CD table and periphery rules have no
  entry for a second capacitor mask or for `cap2m`;[^pdk-05][^pdk-03][^pdk-periph]
  the geometry used on this page is inferred from `capm` and the
  "identical" construction of the device page.[^pdk-07]
* The exposure tool, resist and reflectivity scheme are not public;
  i-line with a conventional resist is an inference from the inferred
  geometry.
* Which layer `CAP2M` is aligned to, whether stacked `cap2m` and `capm`
  plates are registered to each other, and the enclosure values that
  govern them, are not public.
* Whether every SKY130 lot carries the second capacitor module is not
  public; the README calls MiM capacitors "optional".[^pdk-10]

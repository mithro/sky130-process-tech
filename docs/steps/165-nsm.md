## What this step is

`NSM` is a lithography step on the finished metal stack: a resist is
coated over the thin oxide of {ref}`NFUSOX <step-164>`, exposed through
the `NSM` {term}`reticle` and developed, so that the {ref}`NSME <step-166>` etch
can open the dielectric where the layout draws `nsm`. The PDK names the
layer and mask but does not describe their function: the layer table
lists `nsm` (GDS 61:20) as "Nitride seal mask" and a mask layer `cnsm`
(22:0),[^pdk-06] the mask table lists "Nitride Seal Mask, NSM" as used
in SKY130,[^pdk-05] and the periphery rule table for the layer opens
with the function line "Defines Nitride Seal Mask (FIXME)".[^pdk-periph]
The step list used in this reference does not explain the mask either;
what follows is read from the rules and from a published seal-ring
layout.

**Where `nsm` is drawn.** The rules keep the layer away from every
device and wiring layer. Its minimum width is 3.000 µm (nsm.1) and
spacing 4.000 µm (nsm.2); an `NSM_keepout` must be at least 1.000 µm
from diffusion, tap, poly, local interconnect, metals 1–5 and their
mask layers, with exemptions for cells named "nikon*" and for the
"diff ring inside areaid.sl" (nsm.3); and device and wiring layers must
be enclosed by the frame boundary `areaid.ft` by at least 3.000 µm
(nsm.3a) and kept 3.000 µm clear of the die area `areaid.dt`
(nsm.3b).[^pdk-periph] The
minimum-CD table repeats 3 µm and 4 µm as `NSMCD` and
`NSMCDSP`.[^pdk-03] `areaid.sl` 81:1 is the "Seal ring identifier" (the
PDK reuses the name `areaid.sl` for 81:7, "Signal pad well identifier
(for latchup DRC checks)"; the Caravel cell below draws 81:1),
`areaid.dt` the "Location of dice within the frame" and `areaid.ft` the
"Boundary of the frame".[^pdk-06] The seal-ring cell distributed with
Efabless's Caravel harness for SKY130 shuttles, `advSeal_6um_gen`, shows
the result: along the outer edge of the die it draws a 6 µm-wide
`areaid.sl` band, and inside it a 5 µm-wide `nsm` band over four
0.3 µm-wide diffusion rings. Apart from field-oxide mask-drop slots
(`cfom` 22:22) along the ring, small mask-level shapes filling the
corners and the `areaid.cr` and `areaid.cd` identifiers the PDK marks
"used in seal ring only for stress DRC",[^pdk-06] the cell draws no
process layer other than the diffusion rings — no poly, local
interconnect or metal.[^caravel-sealring] (Our reading of the GDS file.)

We therefore read `NSM` as defining a continuous ring-shaped opening, at
least 3 µm wide, along the edge of every die, in a region that carries
no wiring and whose only drawn device layer is the seal ring's
diffusion (inference from the rules and the layout). What the opening
is for — on our reading, a path for the {ref}`NTSD <step-167>` nitride
to seal the edge of the dielectric stack — is set out under *Why this
step exists*.

## Step category

`NSM` is a {ref}`Photolithography (mask step) <category-lithography>`
step of the *non-critical, large-feature* type. At 3 µm features on
4 µm spaces[^pdk-03] the tighter of the two is the feature, and the
process factor {term}`k₁ <k1>` on an i-line (365 nm) tool with an
assumed, typical {term}`NA` of 0.6, taking the smaller of the feature
and space dimensions as the printed half-pitch-equivalent, is
{math}`k_1 = 3 \times 0.6 / 0.365 \approx 4.9`, far above any
resolution limit (our arithmetic), and ASML describes older exposure
tools that "migrate to the lithography of choice for less critical
layers";[^asml-30] we infer an i-line exposure and a DNQ/novolac
resist. What is specific to this instance is the substrate and the etch
the resist must survive: the wafer carries the full five-level
aluminium stack with 1.26 µm-tall metal-5 lines under a thin
oxide,[^pdk-04] and, on our reading of {ref}`NSME <step-166>`, the
resist must protect everything except the seal ring during an oxide etch
that may be several micrometres deep. {term}`Overlay <overlay>` is not
critical; the 1.000 µm keepout (nsm.3)[^pdk-periph] is the alignment budget.

## Why this step exists

* **The dielectric stack has an exposed edge.** When a wafer is sawn,
  every dielectric layer of the die is cut through at the die edge;
  on the PDK's stack diagram the bottom of metal 5 carries the level
  5.3711 µm, measured from a datum the drawing does not state (this
  reference reads it as the trench floor, {ref}`STIE <step-006>`), and
  outside the wiring everything below it is PSG, the thin LINT nitride
  and the inter-level oxides.[^pdk-04] Oxide takes up
  water, and moisture together with ionic contamination is the classic
  driver of corrosion in electronic devices, as Comizzoli et al.
  reviewed;[^comizzoli-1986] Peck derived an acceleration model for
  humidity tests.[^peck-1986] A {term}`seal ring` — metal, via and
  diffusion rings around the die, capped by the passivation — blocks
  that lateral path. A mixed-signal seal-ring patent draws such a ring
  under a passivation oxide and a passivation nitride, calls the nitride
  "a very good barrier of moisture and ionic contamination", and still
  shows "some moisture exposure through a moisture path … through the
  passivation oxide" at the die periphery, noting that "this same
  situation occurs at the bond pad openings as well".[^pat-sealring-zeevo]
  A thin oxide under the nitride is itself such a path.
* **Letting the passivation nitride reach down.** A nitride deposited
  only on top of the stack leaves the oxide layers beneath it
  continuous from the saw edge to the circuit. An opening etched
  through the dielectrics around the die, and then lined by the
  passivation nitride, replaces that path with a nitride wall. A
  GlobalFoundries patent that may still be in force describes a
  structure of this kind, in the collapsed note below this list. The
  `nsm` geometry — a die-edge ring kept
  clear of all wiring, allowed to overlap only the seal ring's
  diffusion[^pdk-periph][^caravel-sealring] — and the PDK's name for
  the layer, "Nitride seal mask",[^pdk-06] fit that purpose; that SKY130's `NSM` opening serves it, and
  how deep it goes, are our inference.
* **Crack arrest at the saw line.** Discontinuities in the dielectric
  along the dicing channel also stop saw cracks from running into the
  circuit: a Siemens and IBM patent creates "discontinuities in the thickness of
  the dielectric layer in the channel regions … near the edges of the
  active regions".[^pat-crackstop-ibm] An `nsm` trench would form such
  a discontinuity (inference).
* **Why a resist mask and not the pad mask.** The pad opening
  ({ref}`PDM <step-168>`) must stop on metal 5; on the deep readings of
  {ref}`NSME <step-166>` the seal opening goes further into the
  dielectric stack and needs its own etch, and a nitride can line only
  an opening that exists before it is deposited (inference from the
  construction of the GlobalFoundries edge seal, in the collapsed note
  below this list).

:::{dropdown} From a patent shown as in force (US 10,062,748; estimated expiry 2038-02-27) — open to read
A GlobalFoundries patent describes a structure of this kind for low-k
dielectrics — an "edge seal structure formed through the low-k
dielectric material to at least a substrate", its trench sidewalls
covered by a PECVD oxide or nitride passivation — and notes that chip
edge sealing structures "can be provided in a final passivation
nitride/polyimide film, which requires only one mask".[^pat-edgeseal-gf]
:::

Without `NSM`, on this reading, the passivation nitride would lie only
on top of the dielectric stack and the stack's saw-cut edge would lead
directly to the seal ring and the circuit.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic large-feature lithography step over a finished
aluminium back end for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab (SKY130's is not
public):

1. **Surface preparation.** Dehydration bake and {term}`HMDS` prime on
   the {ref}`NFUSOX <step-164>` oxide; the surface has the full
   metal-5 topography.
2. **Resist coat.** A positive i-line DNQ/novolac
   resist[^dammel-1993][^reichmanis-1989][^wiki-dnq] thick enough to
   survive a long dielectric etch and to cover the 1.26 µm metal-5
   steps[^pdk-04] — a TSMC fuse-window patent uses a resist "of
   approximately 1 micron" to withstand a two-step etch through a
   plasma-oxide/plasma-nitride passivation and three inter-metal
   dielectric layers (its claim 1 says "at least
   two").[^pat-fusewin-tsmc] Thickness
   uniformity over topography follows Mack's treatment of resist
   coating and swing curves.[^mack-2007][^brunner-1991]
3. **Exposure.** i-line {term}`stepper`, conventional illumination, a
   binary chrome reticle generated from `nsm` (mask layer `cnsm`
   22:0[^pdk-06]); no resolution enhancement is needed at
   {math}`k_1 \approx 4.9` (inference from the geometry).
4. **Post-exposure bake and develop.** PEB, 2.38 % (0.26 N) TMAH develop, rinse
   and dry; a hard bake to harden the resist for the etch.[^txt-02]
5. **Alignment.** To the metal-5 or earlier frame marks; the 1 µm
   keepout[^pdk-periph] leaves generous margin (our reading; Levinson's book has a
   chapter on overlay[^levinson-2005]).
6. **Inspection.** Opening width on the ring and in the corners; resist
   coverage over the metal-5 edges beside the ring; overlay on the KLA
   class SkyWater lists.[^skw-01]

## Open questions

* The PDK names `nsm` but does not describe its purpose ("FIXME" in the
  rule table[^pdk-periph]); the reading of the opening as a die-edge
  path for the passivation nitride is an inference.
* How deep the {ref}`NSME <step-166>` opening goes — through the thin
  oxide only, through part of the stack, or to the seal ring's
  diffusion — is not public; the exemption of the "diff ring inside
  areaid.sl" from the keepout[^pdk-periph] and the diffusion rings under
  the `nsm` band in the Caravel cell[^caravel-sealring] are consistent
  with the deepest reading.
* The Caravel `advSeal_6um_gen` cell draws no metal or via
  rings;[^caravel-sealring] the PDK's general rules say `areaid.sl`
  "must not overlap li1 and metX for pcell "advSeal_6um"" (x.23e) and
  that the pcell "must overlap diff" (x.26), while its contact and via
  rules still allow bar-shaped contacts and vias in a "die seal ring"
  (licon.3, ct.3, via.3, via2.3, via3.3,
  via4.3).[^pdk-periph] Whether any SKY130 seal ring has metal rings is
  not public.
* The exposure tool, resist and resist thickness are not public.

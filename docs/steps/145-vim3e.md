## What this step is

`VIM3E` etches the via-3 holes. Through the 0.20 µm resist openings
of {ref}`VIM3 <step-144>` a fluorocarbon plasma cuts the
{ref}`NCAPOX5 <step-143>` cap and the {ref}`NILD5 <step-141>` oxide
down to two kinds of floor at once: the TiW cap of the metal-3 lines,
0.39 µm below the surface on the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] and
the top plates of the {term}`MiM capacitors <MiM capacitor>` (TiW, as
assumed at {ref}`CAPTIW1 <step-136>`), which stand above the
metal-3 cap on the capacitor dielectric — the PDK's `cap_mim`
cross-section draws a via from metal 4 landing on "CAPM" beside one
landing on "M3 (plate 1)".[^pdk-07] The holes are then lined at
{ref}`TIN5 <step-146>` and filled at {ref}`WDEP5 <step-147>` to make
the `via3` plug, "Contact from metal 3 to metal 4".[^pdk-06] The resist
is stripped afterwards; this reference treats the strip and post-etch
clean as part of this step, as at {ref}`VIME <step-119>` and
{ref}`VIM2E <step-130>`.

The geometry of the deeper hole repeats via 2 with a slightly
shallower depth: 0.20 µm squares (via3.1) on a 0.200 µm space
(via3.2), enclosed by metal 3 by 0.060 µm (via3.4),[^pdk-periph] etched
0.39 µm deep — an {term}`aspect ratio` of about 1.95:1 as drawn. The
metal-3 floor is, on the public description of the S8 metal stacks,
a 300 Å TiW cap over Al–Cu.[^cyp-qtp-113005] The plate floor is higher
by the capacitor dielectric and plate — of the order of 0.1–0.2 µm on
our reading of {ref}`CAPILD <step-135>` and
{ref}`CAPTIW1 <step-136>` — so a via over a capacitor reaches its
floor well before a via over bare metal 3 and then sits in the
plasma for the remainder of the etch (inference from the
construction). The PDK's `via3` is 3 410 mΩ, the same as
`via2`.[^pdk-08]

## Step category

`VIM3E` is an {ref}`Etch <category-etch>` step of the *dielectric,
fluorocarbon* class and the third of the four via etches;
{ref}`VIME <step-119>` sets out what distinguishes the class within
the flow — the stop layer is a metal that fluorine can etch, since
tungsten forms volatile WF₆ (Petri, Henry and Sadeghi[^petri-1992]) and
titanium TiF₄, so the etch must reach the TiW in a polymerising,
fluorine-lean regime and stop on time — and {ref}`CTME <step-108>` the
underlying surface chemistry.[^flamm-1981][^winters-1992] What is
specific to this instance is the *dual-depth* landing. Every
oxide etch that must reach floors at different depths — the
{term}`ARDE` and loading behaviour Gottscho, Jurgensen and Vitkavage
reviewed[^gottscho-1992] is the uniform-depth version of the problem —
must over-etch the shallow floor by the depth difference; here that
shallow floor is a capacitor electrode on a few tens of nanometres of
dielectric. Freescale's MiM patent states the requirement directly:
the via etch is "selective for etch stop onto the plate electrodes
and onto the conductor in the interconnect layer".[^pat-mim-freescale]

## Why this step exists

The via-3 hole sets the resistance and reliability of every connection
from metal 4 to metal 3 and to the capacitor top plates; the etch
decides:

* **A clean, complete landing on the metal-3 cap.** Polymer or oxide
  left on the floor raises the via resistance and, as Bui et al.
  showed for the cap under a tungsten-plug via, degrades its
  electromigration performance.[^bui-1994]
* **Not punching through the plate.** A via over a capacitor sees an
  over-etch of the order of the plate height in oxide-equivalent time
  (inference). If the fluorine-lean protection Schaepkens et al.
  describe — a steady-state fluorocarbon film on the non-oxide
  surface[^schaepkens-1999] — fails, the etch thins the TiW plate, and
  a through-plate hole would expose the thin capacitor dielectric and
  let the liner and plug short or stress it. The plate's thickness,
  several times the 300 Å cap of the lines on our reading of
  {ref}`CAPTIW1 <step-136>`, is what gives this margin (inference).
* **Not punching through the metal-3 cap.** If the 300 Å
  TiW[^cyp-qtp-113005] is consumed, fluorine reaches aluminium and forms
  involatile AlF₃[^hess-1982] — the problem Texas Instruments' etch-stop
  patent for "fluorine-bearing" via etches addresses.[^pat-etchstop-ti]
* **Tolerating a partly unlanded via.** With 0.060 µm of metal-3
  enclosure[^pdk-periph] a via that slips off a line etches down
  beside it; beside a capacitor plate the same slip exposes the plate
  edge and the dielectric beneath (inference from the rules; the
  plate-enclosure values are not published[^pdk-periph]).
* **Charging of the capacitor.** The via etch is the step at which
  each top plate is first opened to the plasma; Wang, Ackaert et al.
  showed that {term}`plasma-charging <plasma charging>` damage of floating MiM capacitors
  depends on the antenna areas connected to each plate,[^wang-2004-mim]
  and Fang and McVittie described the thin-dielectric damage such
  charging causes.[^fang-1992]
* **A via that tungsten will fill.** Residue or outgassing from the
  sidewall can stop tungsten nucleating in {ref}`WDEP5 <step-147>` —
  the {term}`poisoned via <via poisoning>` of Kobayakawa et al. and Romero et
  al.[^kobayakawa-1991][^romero-1991] that a UMC patent addresses by
  treating the exposed dielectric.[^pat-via-poison-umc]

Without `VIM3E` the resist pattern of {ref}`VIM3 <step-144>` would be
stripped without effect, metal 4 would sit on unbroken oxide and the
capacitors would have no top-plate terminal.

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic dual-depth via etch for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab
(SKY130's recipe is not public); the shared physics is at
{ref}`CTME <step-108>` and {ref}`VIME <step-119>`.

1. **Chamber.** A single-wafer dielectric etcher — medium-density
   capacitively coupled or high-density inductively coupled, with
   separate bias, helium backside cooling and optical-emission
   endpoint ({ref}`category-etch`); Lam's Exelan was launched for this
   generation in 2001.[^lam-exelan]
2. **BARC open.** The organic {term}`BARC` of {ref}`VIM3 <step-144>` is
   opened with a short O₂/N₂ or CF₄-containing step.
3. **Main oxide etch.** A polymerising fluorocarbon — C₄F₈, C₂F₆ or
   CHF₃ with argon and a little O₂ or CO — at tens of mTorr and high
   bias,[^oehrlein-1994b][^perry-2001][^standaert-1998] with high
   {term}`selectivity` to TiW so that the plate vias survive while the
   line vias finish (industry practice;[^schaepkens-1999] the
   requirement is stated in the Freescale patent[^pat-mim-freescale]).
4. **Endpoint and over-etch.** Via layers expose little of the wafer,
   so emission endpoint is weak — Wodecki describes endpoint on
   low-open-area dielectric etches[^wodecki-1999] — and the etch is run
   by time from the thickness the polish and cap left
   ({ref}`CMPM3 <step-142>`, {ref}`NCAPOX5 <step-143>`), with an
   over-etch sized for the deepest line vias and a chemistry lean
   enough in fluorine to protect the TiW.[^schaepkens-1999][^petri-1992]
5. **Strip and clean.** Oxygen or O₂/N₂ downstream {term}`ash` —
   GaSonics, Iridia or Mattson class in SkyWater's list[^skw-01] — then
   a solvent or semi-aqueous clean ("EKS265, EKC270 solvents"[^skw-01])
   to remove fluorocarbon polymer and fluoride residue from both
   floors without attacking TiW; no HF and no peroxide (inference;
   H₂O₂ etches TiW, as Danzl and McLaurin used it to[^danzl-1997]).
6. **Queue time.** Limited between clean and {ref}`TIN5 <step-146>`,
   with a degas before the liner (industry practice;[^txt-05] the
   poisoned-via literature is the reason[^kobayakawa-1991]).
7. **Metrology.** Top and bottom CD by {term}`CD-SEM`; profile and
   TiW loss on both floors by cross-section SEM on monitors; via-3
   chain resistance to metal 3 and to capacitor plates at
   {term}`e-test`, against the PDK's 3.4 Ω `via3`[^pdk-08]; capacitor
   leakage after the via module. The published SKY130 {term}`test tile`
   holds an "s8p via3 (0.2x0.2) contact chain (16500 contacts)" with a
   via-3 Kelvin structure, a "Via-3 array, M4-M3 near CAPM (min via-3
   space to CAPM)" of 1120 vias "For Via Shorts", and a "Via-3 chain,
   5754 via-3, M4-CAPM over M3 (min via
   enclosure)".[^raw-data-testtile-pads]

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **No dielectric etcher is named on SkyWater's public list.** The
  list gives, under poly/silicon etch, "AMAT DPSII, HBR, Cl2, NF3,
  CF4, CHF3, O2", "Lam 9400 TCP, poly/nitride, HBr, CF4, SF6, O2" and
  "Lam 4400, HBr, Cl2, C2F6, CF4, SF6, O2".[^skw-01] All three carry
  fluorine-bearing gases that can etch oxide; strength: **weak** for
  assignment of any of them to the via etch.
* **Lam Exelan.** No public source places an Exelan at SkyWater; it
  appears here only as the era's typical dielectric etcher, a line
  Lam's own 10-K lists.[^lam-10k]
* **Strip and clean — GaSonics PEP, Iridia, Mattson Aspen II; batch
  rotational tools with "EKS265, EKC270 solvents"; SEZ 223 /
  Da Vinci.**[^skw-01] Strength: strong for existence.

## Resources required

* **{ref}`C₄F₈ <material-etch-gases>`, C₂F₆, CHF₃, CF₄**, **{ref}`Ar <material-process-gases>`**, **O₂** (and possibly CO or N₂) for the
  oxide etch (industry practice;[^nojiri-2015] SkyWater lists CF₄, CHF₃,
  C₂F₆ and O₂ on its etchers[^skw-01]).
* **Helium** for backside cooling; **NF₃** or O₂-based chamber clean.
* **O₂/N₂** (and {ref}`forming gas <material-anneal-ambients>`) for the ash;[^skw-01] CF₄, listed on the
  Iridia and Mattson ashers,[^skw-01] would attack the exposed TiW and
  would, we infer, be left out. **Amine or semi-aqueous solvent**
  ({ref}`wet chemicals <material-wet-chemicals>`; EKC265/EKC270 class; SkyWater lists "EKS265, EKC270
  solvents"[^skw-01]) and {ref}`DI water <material-ultrapure-water>` for the post-etch clean.
* **{ref}`Chamber consumables <material-hardware-consumables>`**; **{ref}`monitor wafers <material-substrates>`** with oxide over TiW for
  rate and selectivity checks.

## Open questions

* The etch chemistry, chamber, endpoint scheme and over-etch of
  `VIM3E`, and the TiW lost on each floor, are not public.
* The depth difference between vias over capacitor plates and over
  metal 3 depends on unpublished dielectric and plate thicknesses; the
  0.1–0.2 µm used here is our estimate.
* Whether the plate vias need any special measure (a thicker plate, a
  separate etch, a different density rule) is not public.
* Which etcher runs the step is not public; SkyWater's list names no
  dedicated dielectric etcher.[^skw-01]
* This page treats the resist strip and post-etch clean as part of
  the etch.

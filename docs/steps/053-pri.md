## What this step is

`PRI` is the implant that sets the {term}`sheet resistance` of the SKY130
precision {term}`poly resistor`. Through the windows opened at
{ref}`RRPM <step-052>`, a p-type dose is placed into the still-undoped
resistor bodies of the gate film, while the resist covers the n⁺ gate
poly everywhere else. The resist is stripped at {ref}`PRIS <step-054>`.

Before the step resist covers a film everywhere except one window over the middle of the oxide-filled trench; the film under the resist is already drawn in the colour of one doping type and the film in the window is not. After it arrows come down on the whole slice, and the film in the window is drawn in a second colour through its whole thickness.

Before, the RRPM window over the still-undoped resistor body in an n-type gate film; after, the body p-type, the film this slice reads as a 300 Ω/sq P+ poly resistor (`res_high_po`),[^pdk-07] while the resist keeps the dose off the n⁺ gate film. The colour fills the whole thickness of the body because it marks the type of the doping; its depth in the film is not public, and whether the resistor doping is one implant or several is not stated. The target is public: the PDK's "P+ poly precision resistors"
(`res_high_po`) have a sheet resistance of 300 Ω/sq, offered in five
fixed widths from 0.35 µm to 5.73 µm,[^pdk-07] and the extraction
table gives 319.8 Ω/sq for the "XHR poly resistor" layer.[^pdk-08]
The drawn layer that defines the body, `rpm`, is described as the
"300 ohms/square polysilicon resistor implant",[^pdk-06] which names
this step's purpose exactly. Species, energy and dose are not public.
For a 0.18 µm film,[^pdk-03] 300 Ω/sq corresponds to a resistivity of
about 5 mΩ·cm, which the published resistivity-versus-concentration
data for boron-doped poly place at an average concentration of order
10¹⁹–10²⁰ cm⁻³,[^kamins-1998][^seto-1975] i.e. a dose of order
10¹⁴–10¹⁵ cm⁻² spread through the film — an illustrative estimate,
not a SkyWater number.

**Single or multiple implants.** Whether the resistor doping is one
implant or more than one (for example two energies to dope the film
top and bottom evenly) is not stated publicly. This reference does
not choose between these, and nothing on this page depends on which
is right.

## Step category

`PRI` is an {ref}`Ion implantation <category-implant>` step of the
*poly and resistor implant* class (category page). Its defining
feature is that it is a *precision* implant: for a channel implant a
few percent of dose error moves a threshold by millivolts, whereas
here the sheet resistance is a steep function of dose in exactly the
range used,[^seto-1975] so dose repeatability and uniformity translate
directly into resistor tolerance and matching.

## Why this step exists

Analogue circuits need resistors whose value, temperature coefficient
and matching are known. Diffused resistors have large voltage and
temperature coefficients and a junction to the substrate; n⁺ gate poly
at 48.2 Ω/sq[^pdk-08] is too conductive and too "metallic" in its
temperature behaviour. A separately implanted p-type poly film lets the
designer choose a sheet resistance and, through the doping level, a
temperature coefficient near zero — Lu et al. worked out the
optimisation of monolithic poly resistors,[^lu-1981] Lane and Wrixon
the design rules for analogue use,[^lane-1989] and Wright et al. are
the non-poly route to a high-sheet-resistance, low-TCR film — a
sputtered Cr–Si–B film reaching 20 kΩ/sq, the yardstick the
implanted-poly resistor is measured against.[^wright-2010] The
physics is Seto's grain-boundary trapping
model: carriers are trapped at grain boundaries until the traps are
filled, so conductivity depends on the doping level relative to the
trap density and on the grain size.[^seto-1975][^mandurah-1981] That
is also why the dose window is narrow and why poly resistors show a
non-zero voltage coefficient at high fields.[^chen-2000]

The reason the implant is separate from every other doping step is
the same steepness: the film must receive this dose and no other. The
resistor bodies were protected from the gate implant at
{ref}`RPM <step-049>`, and the design rules keep them out of the n⁺
source/drain mask (rpm.6) while enclosing them in the p⁺ one
(rpm.4),[^pdk-periph] consistent with a p-type body.

The published SKY130 {term}`test tile` carries the structures such a
film is characterised with: eleven modules of "300 ohm/sq P+  POLY RESISTOR - 2
micron trench contacts oriented || to current flow" (spacing as printed
in the CSV) cover widths of
0.33, 0.69, 1.41, 2.85 and 5.73 µm from 0.5 to 20 squares, many as
"Mismatch" pairs, and a van der Pauw structure measures "RSRP - 300
ohm/sq poly resistor sheet resistance".[^raw-data-testtile-pads]
The SKY130 raw-data repository publishes two-terminal current–voltage
sweeps of 88 of those resistors.[^raw-data-passives] Taking the
difference between the median 20-square and 4-square resistances at
each drawn width as 16 squares of body, which cancels the contacts and
heads, and fitting those per-square values against width gives a
sheet resistance of about 310 Ω/sq and an electrical width about
0.05 µm narrower than drawn; the van der Pauw structure of module 3360 reads 327 Ω/sq from its one
published force/sense configuration (the module-3379 file has a sense
channel that stays near 0 V) (our extraction from the
published measurements; the files record no temperature, date or
wafer).[^raw-data-passives] The fitted value lies between the
300 Ω/sq of the device page and the 319.8 Ω/sq of the extraction
table, and the van der Pauw reading a little above both.[^pdk-07][^pdk-08]
The PDK's e-test table gives nominal values and limits for 0.5- to
20-square resistors at each fixed width — for 20 squares at 5.73 µm,
6 556 Ω (5 636–7 475 Ω) — and the same arithmetic on its nominal values
gives about 320 Ω/sq (our arithmetic).[^pdk-07] The measured 20-square,
5.73 µm resistors read 6 293–6 340 Ω, and every measured structure
0.69 µm or wider lies inside the e-test limits for its geometry except
the 2.85 µm pair of module 6210, which the pad list gives four squares
but whose two resistors read 350 and 530 Ω, close to one square's
resistance (our extraction).[^raw-data-passives][^raw-data-testtile-pads]

## How it is typically performed

An industry-generic poly-resistor implant for a 200 mm, 130 nm-era fab
(SKY130 values are not public):

* **Species.** Boron, as ¹¹B⁺ or as BF₂⁺ from BF₃ gas.[^wiki-bf3] BF₂
  gives a shallower profile at a given energy and the fluorine has
  known effects on boron diffusion and activation.[^wang-1997] Which
  SKY130 uses is not stated; SkyWater's implanters list both "B11" and
  "BF2".[^skw-01]
* **Energy.** Tens of keV, chosen to place the dose inside the 0.18 µm
  film without reaching the gate oxide beneath — for a resistor that
  sits on field oxide the constraint is weaker, but the same recipe
  must serve resistors drawn anywhere. As for {ref}`P1I <step-050>`,
  the amorphous film means no {term}`channelling` tail.
* **Dose.** Of order 10¹⁴–10¹⁵ cm⁻² (the illustrative estimate above),
  which falls in the range of either SkyWater's medium-current
  implanter ("1e11 to 1e14") or its high-dose GSD ("5e12 to
  5e16").[^skw-01] Dose control is the whole point: the beam current
  integrator and charge neutralisation must be tight (category page),
  and the implant is normally run on a tool whose dose repeatability
  is the best in the fab.
* **Tilt.** Normal or near-normal incidence; the resistor windows are
  wide (rpm.1a 1.270 µm minimum[^pdk-periph]) so {term}`shadowing` is not an
  issue, but lateral {term}`straggle` at the resist edge sets the effective
  length of the doped body.[^hook-2003]
* **Anneal.** None dedicated; activation and redistribution occur in
  the thermal steps that follow ({ref}`IOX45 <step-063>` onwards). The
  final sheet resistance therefore depends on the whole downstream
  {term}`thermal budget`, which is one reason the PDK's {term}`e-test` specifications
  for these resistors are measured on finished wafers.[^pdk-07]
* **Monitoring.** Sheet resistance of a companion monitor wafer after
  a standard anneal; in-line, the dose is monitored by the tool's own
  Faraday system and, on product, by the e-test resistor structures.

## Machines typically used

* **{ref}`Medium-current implanter <machine-medium-current-implanter>`** (Axcelis/Eaton 8250, Varian E220/E500,
  Applied Materials xR80) for doses up to ~10¹⁴ cm⁻², or a
  **{ref}`high-current implanter <machine-high-current-implanter>`** (Axcelis GSD, Varian VIISta 80) above
  that (category page).
* **{term}`Four-point probe <four-point probe>`** for monitor {ref}`sheet resistance <machine-sheet-resistance-metrology>`.

## Machines likely used at SkyWater

* **Axcelis 8250 medium-current or Axcelis GSD high-dose implanter.**
  SkyWater lists "Axcelis 8250 Mid current B11, BF2, As … 1e11 to 1e14"
  and "Axcelis GSD Hi dose B11, BF2, P, As 2-180kev, 5e12 to
  5e16".[^skw-01] Strength: **strong** for the tools; which one runs
  this implant depends on the (unpublished) dose and is not stated. The
  8250HT is described as a medium-current implanter "designed for 0.18µm
  and below processes" with an energy range of 3–750 keV.[^axcelis-8250]

## Open questions

* Species (B or BF₂), energy, dose, tilt and whether the resistor
  doping is one implant or several are not public; the step list does
  not explain the step's name.
* Whether the `urpm` (2000 Ω/sq) bodies also receive this implant is
  not public (see {ref}`RRPM <step-052>`).
* Which implanter runs it is not stated; both listed classes could.
* How much of the final 300 Ω/sq is set here versus by the later p⁺
  source/drain implant that the rules require to enclose the resistor
  (rpm.4[^pdk-periph]) — through whatever cap is present at that point
  — is not public.
* The published test-tile measurements put the two ends of a P+
  resistor — slot contacts and heads — at about 70 Ω at 5.73 µm width,
  190 Ω at 1.41 µm and 390 Ω at 0.69 µm (the median 4-square resistance
  less four squares of body; our extraction), against the 68, 256 and
  392 Ω that the e-test nominal values imply at those widths (our
  arithmetic).[^raw-data-passives][^pdk-07] How that divides between
  the contacts and the p⁺ head doping is not public.

# Progress: qualification-report evidence, round 2 (done)

Round 2 extended `data/history/qtp.yaml` from 24 to 50 reports. Sources: Cypress's own
qualification-report library as archived by the Wayback Machine in 2020 (the page list is
`tmp/cyhist-cache/web/cdx-cypress-qualreports.txt`, the archived PDFs
`tmp/cyhist-cache/web/cdx-cypress-file-pdfs.txt`), fetched at one request every 10 seconds, and
Infineon's public asset URLs.

What the reports state about how the codes relate (derivatives, transfers, suffixes) is summarised on
`docs/history/naming.md`, and the inconsistencies between reports are in each record's `notes`. Reports
whose pages are archived but whose PDFs are not were left out; round 3 is in `progress-cyhist-qtp3.md`.

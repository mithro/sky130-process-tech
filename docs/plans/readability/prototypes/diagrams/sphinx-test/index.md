`STIE` (shallow-trench-isolation etch) is the plasma etch that cuts the isolation trenches into the
silicon. Using the patterned nitride/pad-oxide hard mask, a halogen plasma etches a few hundred nanometres
into the wafer wherever the field is open.[^pdk-04]

:::{figure} /_static/figures/sti-006-stie.svg
:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step, photoresist, the nitride hard mask and the pad oxide cover two active areas and the silicon between them is bare. After the step, a trench with tapered walls and rounded bottom corners has been etched into the bare silicon and the resist is gone.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-sti-006-stie

The wafer before and after `STIE`. The resist is drawn as left on for the trench etch and stripped at the end of the step. No trench depth is public; about 0.33 µm is this page's reading of the PDK stack drawing.[^pdk-04] Not to scale.

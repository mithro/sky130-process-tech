The wafer before and after `DNI`, a high-energy phosphorus implant through the windows opened at `DNM`. It places a buried layer of N-type doping a micrometre or more below the surface, forming the deep N-well (`dnwell`).[^pdk-06] The implant enters bare silicon at the trench floors and passes through the nitride/pad-oxide stack over the active areas, so the buried layer sits deeper under the field than under the active regions by roughly the trench depth; the layer is not flat, and no public source gives the SKY130 profile. Not to scale.

A close-up of via 2; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the aluminium of the metal-2 line under it. Before, the tungsten and its liner over the whole wafer; after, both polished off the field down to the cap oxide, leaving the via-2 plug: a 0.20 µm square in the PDK's rules,[^pdk-periph] drawn here flush with the oxide. How far the polish recesses the plug and thins the 0.42 µm dielectric[^pdk-04] under the field is not public, and neither is drawn. The plug is drawn about 1.4 times as tall as it is wide, and its liner with one thickness, thicker on the floor, in proportion, than it is. The liner, the metal-2 line and, in the upper panel, the oxides are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

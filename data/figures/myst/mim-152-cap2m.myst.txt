A close-up of part of the metal-4 level, at the left edge of a second capacitor that runs on past the right-hand edge of the view; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 4. Before, the blanket top-plate film on the dielectric; after, the resist of the second capacitor mask left where the layout draws a `cap2m` plate, with the film bare everywhere else. The PDK publishes no `cap2m` rules; this page reads the plate geometry as that of `capm`, 2 µm minimum width and 0.84 µm minimum space[^pdk-03] (inference). Where the plate edge falls is an illustrative choice, and neither the plate nor the resist is drawn to scale; the dielectric, some 20–30 nm on the estimate of the CAPILD2 page, and the plate film are drawn thicker in proportion than they are. The two oxides and the bottom film of the metal stack are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the aluminium–copper. Not to scale.

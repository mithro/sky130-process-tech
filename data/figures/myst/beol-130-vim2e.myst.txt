Before the step a violet resist with a window lies on the oxide above the line. :width: 560px
:name: fig-beol-130-vim2e

A close-up of the via 2, over the left-hand metal-2 line; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the aluminium of that line. Before, the via-2 resist; after, the hole etched through the cap oxide and the NILD4 oxide, 0.42 µm together in the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] to the cap film on top of the metal-2 line, and the resist stripped, which the page treats as part of this step. Which refractory film the cap is is not public: the overview of the metal cap sets out the TiW and the TiN readings, and the figure does not choose. The hole is drawn much shallower than it is: the PDK's via 2 is a 0.200 µm square (via2.1a),[^pdk-periph] about 2.1 times as deep as it is wide on the page's reckoning, and it is drawn about 1.4 times as deep as it is wide, deeper and wider than the via-1 hole as it is in the PDK. The walls are drawn vertical; their profile is not public. The oxides are drawn but not labelled in the lower panel, and the metal-2 line not in the upper. Not to scale.

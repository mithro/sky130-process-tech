Before, the implanted RRPM resist with its window over the resistor body; after, the resist gone and the gate film bare, now carrying two doping levels that are not drawn: n⁺ gate film and a p-type resistor body. The thin chemical oxide of the clean is not drawn. The field oxide is drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step a narrow capped gate stack stands on the thin oxide of the left-hand active area and a wider capped stack on the oxide-filled trench. After it a thin new oxide lines both sides of the gate stack and lies on top of the old thin oxide over both active areas; the tops of the stacks are unchanged.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-poly-063-iox45

Before, the etched gate and resistor stacks; after, a thin oxide on every exposed silicon surface: on both sidewalls of the gate film and on the active areas, where it thickens what the etch left of the gate oxide. The capped top of the gate film does not oxidise, which is the page's reading of the nitride cap. The resistor body's sidewalls oxidise too, less than the gate's, but that oxide is not drawn. The oxide's thickness, ambient, temperature and tool are not public, and that it is thin is the page's inference; it is drawn at the minimum film thickness. The silicon and the field oxide are drawn but not labelled (and the gate oxide in the lower panel), and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step a narrow capped gate stack stands on the thin oxide of the left-hand active area and a wider capped stack on the oxide-filled trench. After it a thin new oxide lines both sides of the gate stack and lies on top of the old thin oxide over both active areas; the tops of the stacks are unchanged.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-poly-063-iox45

Before, the etched gate and resistor stacks; after, a thin oxide on every exposed silicon surface: on both sidewalls of the gate film and on the active areas, where it thickens what the etch left of the gate oxide. The capped top of the gate film does not oxidise, which is the page's reading of the nitride cap. The resistor body's sidewalls oxidise too, less than the gate's, but that oxide is not drawn. The oxide's thickness, ambient, temperature and tool are not public, and that it is thin is the page's inference; it is drawn at the same minimum film thickness everywhere, although the page has the gate sidewalls growing the most oxide and the source/drain silicon the least. The thickening of the gate oxide under the foot of the gate is not drawn either. The colours of the gate film mark the type of its doping, not a depth profile. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) is drawn but not labelled. The gate oxide is not labelled in the lower panel, where its leader would run along the new oxide. The liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

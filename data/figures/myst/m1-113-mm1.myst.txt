Before the step three flat blanket films, thin purple, thick silver and thin purple, cover a flat pale oxide in which two small tungsten plugs sit over thin purple pads. After it three violet resist lines stand on the films: one over each plug and one in the middle of the slice.

Before, the blanket metal-1 stack on the flat oxide and plugs; after, resist lines wherever the layout draws metal 1: one over each contact, enclosing it (the right-hand one runs on past the edge of the slice), and one over the middle of the slice, which has no contact here. The layout is an illustrative choice; the PDK's rules give a 0.140 µm minimum width and space (m1.1, m1.2) and an enclosure of the contact of 0.030 µm (m1.4).[^pdk-periph] Which refractory films the bottom and the cap of the stack are is not public (the overview of the metal cap sets out the two readings, and the figure does not choose; the page's text opens with the TiW stack of the 2013 report, and the figure, like the overview, draws neither); either way the resist sits on a flat film. Nothing is drawn to scale. The metal contacts, the bottom film of the stack, the local interconnect, the glass, the cap oxide, the lower tungsten plugs, the silicide discs, the contact liners and the transistors' films (the spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide and the spacer oxide), the doped regions and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. The inter-level oxide under the stack is not labelled either. Not to scale.

Before, the metal-3 shapes etched at MM3E: a wiring line at the left and, at the right, the bottom plate of a capacitor, with its top plate standing on the thin dielectric; after, the inter-metal oxide the PDK's stack diagram labels "NILD5" (permittivity 4.1),[^pdk-04] filling the gap and burying the line and the plate. The page names HDP-CVD and PECVD TEOS as the two routes for such a gap and does not choose; the drawn profile, filled from the bottom and softened over the metal, only illustrates a gap-filling deposit. The deposited thickness and any liner are not public, and nothing is drawn to scale: the gap between 0.845 µm lines as close as 0.300 µm apart is about 2.8:1 on the page's arithmetic,[^pdk-04][^pdk-periph] and the gap is drawn about 1.9 times as deep as it is wide. The diagram draws no thin "_C" film beside NILD5.[^pdk-04] The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 2. The bottom and cap films of metal 3, the thin capacitor dielectric (left on the metal-3 shapes on this drawing, as at MM3E), the via plugs and their liners, the oxides below and the metal-2 lines are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

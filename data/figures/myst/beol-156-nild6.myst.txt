Before the step two separate metal shapes stand on a flat pale oxide over a buried level of metal: a line at the left and a wider one at the right carrying a raised thin purple plate. Before, the metal-4 shapes etched at MM4E: a wiring line at the left and, at the right, the second capacitor's bottom plate with its top plate on the thin dielectric, over the first capacitor; after, the inter-metal oxide the PDK's stack diagram labels "NILD6" (permittivity 4.0),[^pdk-04] filling the gap and burying the line and the plate. The page names HDP-CVD and PECVD TEOS as the two routes for such a gap; the drawn profile, filled from the bottom and softened over the metal, only illustrates a gap-filling deposit. The route, the deposited thickness and any liner are not public, and nothing is drawn to scale: the gap between 0.845 µm lines 0.300 µm apart is about 2.8:1 on the page's arithmetic,[^pdk-04][^pdk-periph] and it is drawn about 2.2 times as deep as it is wide. The diagram draws no thin "_C" film beside NILD6.[^pdk-04] The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The bottom and cap films of metal 4, the thin capacitor dielectric (left on the metal-4 shapes on this drawing, as at MM4E), the first capacitor and the metal-3 line, the via plugs and their liners and the oxides below are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

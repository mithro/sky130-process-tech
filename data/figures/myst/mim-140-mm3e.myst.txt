A close-up of the left edge of a capacitor and the right-hand end of a metal-3 wiring line; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. Before, the metal-3 resist over the line and over the capacitor; after, the chlorine etch has removed the dielectric outside the resist and then the whole metal-3 stack, stopping on the cap oxide, and the resist has been stripped (the page treats the passivation, strip and clean as part of this step). Beneath the resist the whole stack remains, and the capacitor now has a bottom plate of its own. Since nothing is etched there, on this drawing the thin dielectric also stays on the metal around the top plate and on the wiring line; the page does not discuss that, and the PDK's schematic cross-section draws the dielectric only under the plate.[^pdk-07] The edges are drawn vertical, and nothing is drawn to scale: the page uses the PDK's 0.845 µm for the metal[^pdk-04] and gives the minimum width and space as 0.300 µm (m3.1, m3.2).[^pdk-periph] The two oxides and the bottom film of the metal are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the aluminium–copper. Not to scale.

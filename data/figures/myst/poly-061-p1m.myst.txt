:width: 560px
:name: fig-poly-061-p1m

Before, the complete, unpatterned gate stack; after, two resist lines on it, one where this slice places a gate over the NMOS active area and one over the resistor body on the field oxide. The gate line's drawn minimum width is 0.150 µm (poly.1a), and a resistor body is at least 0.330 µm wide (poly.3);[^pdk-periph] neither width is drawn to scale. The gate-level resist is drawn thinner than the implant-mask resists, following the page's inference that it is thinner than the PDK's generic 1.14 µm;[^pdk-03] its thickness is not public. The colours of the gate film mark the type of its doping, not a depth profile. In the lower panel the p-type resistor body is not labelled, because its leader would have to rise through the resist line. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) is drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

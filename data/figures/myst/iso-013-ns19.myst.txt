The wafer before and after `NS19`, the last step of the isolation module. The nitride is dissolved in hot phosphoric acid, leaving the active areas covered only by the thin pad oxide from `BOX` and the field oxide standing slightly proud of them. The PDK's final field-oxide step of 0.07 µm above the silicon under poly is the net result of the nitride thickness, the polish and all the oxide recess that follows this step;[^pdk-03] the step drawn here is far larger than that. Not to scale.

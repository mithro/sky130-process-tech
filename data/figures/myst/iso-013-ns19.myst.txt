Before, the polished mosaic of nitride islands and filled trenches; after, the nitride gone and the oxide-filled trench standing proud of the pad oxide. The pad oxide is drawn as left in place, which the page gives as the usual case rather than a certainty. The PDK's final field-oxide step of 0.07 µm above the silicon under poly is the net result of the nitride thickness, the polish and all the oxide recess that follows this step;[^pdk-03] the step drawn here is far larger than that. This cross-section is drawn outside a deep N-well region, so the buried layer of `DNI` (step 008) does not appear. Not to scale.

A close-up of the via-3 plug on the capacitor's top plate; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the aluminium of the metal 3 under the capacitor. Before, the tungsten and its liner over the whole wafer; after, both polished off the field down to the cap oxide, leaving the plug: a 0.20 µm square in the PDK's rules,[^pdk-periph] drawn here flush with the oxide. How far the polish recesses the plug and erodes the oxide is not public, and neither is drawn; how much oxide remains over the plate is not public either. The plug on the metal-3 line, outside this view, is made in the same polish and is taller, since the oxide over metal 3 is 0.39 µm in the PDK's stack diagram[^pdk-04] and the plate stands above the metal. The plug is drawn a little less tall than it is wide, and its liner with one thickness, far thicker in proportion than it is. The liner, the NILD5 oxide, the aluminium–copper of metal 3 and, in the lower panel, the plate, the dielectric and the metal cap are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the cap oxide. Not to scale.

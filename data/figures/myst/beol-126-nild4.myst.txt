Before the step three separate metal lines, each a thick silver film between two thin purple ones, stand on a flat pale oxide. After it a thick pale oxide fills the gaps between the lines and buries them; its top is higher over the lines than over the gaps, with sloping sides.

:name: fig-beol-126-nild4

Before, the etched metal-2 lines; after, the inter-metal oxide the PDK's stack diagram labels "NILD4" (permittivity 4.2),[^pdk-04] filling the gaps between the lines and burying them. The page names HDP-CVD and PECVD TEOS as the two routes for such a gap, as at NILD3, and does not choose; the drawn profile, filled from the bottom and softened over the lines, only illustrates a gap-filling deposit. The deposited thickness and any liner under it are not public, and nothing is drawn to scale. The thin "NILD4_C" film the diagram draws beside it (0.030 µm;[^pdk-04] whether a liner or the cap to come is not public) is not drawn. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. The bottom and cap films of the metal-2 lines, the via plugs and their liners, the oxides of the via level and the metal-1 lines with their contact plugs are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

:name: fig-beol-129-vim2

Before, the cap oxide over the polished dielectric that buries the three metal-2 lines; after, the resist with a window over the left-hand metal-2 line, where a via 2 is to go. The PDK gives the via 2 as a 0.200 µm square (via2.1a) that metal 2 must enclose by 0.040 µm (via2.4);[^pdk-periph] one window in this slice, off the via-1 plugs below, is an illustrative choice. The window is not drawn to scale, and neither is the resist; the page reads the PDK's 1.14 µm resist[^pdk-03] as an antenna-rule assumption. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. The metal-2 and metal-1 lines with their films, the via-1 and contact plugs and the oxides between the levels are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the inter-metal oxide. Not to scale.

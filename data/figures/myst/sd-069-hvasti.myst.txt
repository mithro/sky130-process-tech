A close-up of the 5 V NMOS area at the left-hand end of the slice, inside the HVNTM window; the thin resist over the rest of the slice lies outside it. Before, the open window; after, the high-voltage n-type tip in the silicon. The arrows lean at the PDK's 40° "Angle for HV tip implant"; its 23° twist, which turns the beam out of the plane of the drawing, is not drawn, and the number of rotations is not public.[^pdk-03] There is no gate in the 5 V area of this slice, so the tip covers the whole area and the reach under a gate edge that the tilt gives is not shown; nor is the shadow that the resist wall casts. The tip is drawn deeper than the 1.8 V tip, as a graded, lightly doped drain; its depth is not public, and the PDK's 0.1 µm "N+ or P+ S/D (XJ)"[^pdk-03] is deeper still. That the species is arsenic follows the PDK's "N Tip (As)" row.[^pdk-03] The colour marks the type of the doping, not a profile. The thick gate oxide in the lower panel, the re-oxidation oxide over it and the field oxide at the right are drawn but not labelled, nor is the silicon, whose leader would run beside the trench wall or across the beam; the liner oxide is drawn faded, and the P-well made earlier is not drawn. Not to scale.

A close-up of the left edge of a capacitor and the right-hand edge of a metal-3 wiring line; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. Before, the top plate on the dielectric over the still blanket metal-3 stack; after, the resist of the metal-3 mask over the future wiring line and over the capacitor, where it covers the whole plate and reaches beyond its edge, so that the plate will lie inside its metal-3 bottom plate, as the PDK's cross-section draws it.[^pdk-07] The PDK's metal-3 lines are at least 0.300 µm wide and 0.300 µm apart (m3.1, m3.2); the enclosure of the plate by the metal beneath is a rule whose value is not published.[^pdk-periph] The layout and the resist thickness are illustrative, and nothing is drawn to scale: the metal is 0.845 µm on the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] the plate of the order of 0.1 µm and the dielectric a few tens of nanometres on the readings of the preceding pages. The two oxides and the bottom film of the metal stack are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the aluminium–copper. Not to scale.

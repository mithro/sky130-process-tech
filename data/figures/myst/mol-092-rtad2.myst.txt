The slice at the second rapid thermal anneal: the polished glass under its cap oxide, over the finished transistors. Nothing is deposited or removed, and nothing the drawing shows moves. The page reads two purposes, both inferred: a final, stable activation of the source/drains, and a densification of the glass and the cap that drives out the water they took up. The temperature, time and ambient are not public. The junctions are already at their 0.1 µm design depth in the PDK,[^pdk-03] not drawn to scale. The colours mark the type of the doping, not a depth profile. The transistors' films (the spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide and the spacer oxide), the doped regions and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

A close-up of the right-hand metal contact; the lower part of the slice is cut off. Before, the flush plug; after, the metal-1 stack over the whole wafer: a thin bottom film, the aluminium–copper alloy and a thin cap. Which refractory films the bottom and the cap are is not public for SKY130. A 2013 Cypress report for the fab's S8TNV-5R technology gives "Metal 1: 100A Ti / 3200A Al -0.5%Cu / 300A TiW",[^cyp-qtp-113005] and a 2014 report records a change to "150A Ti/250A TiN/3200A Al 0.5% Cu/90A Ti/500A TiN";[^cyp-qtp-123907] the overview of the metal cap sets out both, and this figure does not choose: the bottom and the cap are drawn in the one colour of the titanium films and labelled with both readings. The PDK's stack diagram labels metal 1 0.36 µm;[^pdk-04] nothing is drawn to scale. The tungsten plug, its liner, the LI nitride cap, the local interconnect, the local-contact plug, the glass and the cap oxide below are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the inter-level oxide. Not to scale.

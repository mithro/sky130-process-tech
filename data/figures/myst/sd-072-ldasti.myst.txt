At LDASTI a lightly doped arsenic tip goes through the LDNTM windows into the SONOS memory transistors. None lies in this slice, so here the resist stops the whole beam and nothing changes. The energy and dose are not public. The page infers that the PDK's 7° tip angle applies,[^pdk-03] but whether the implant is tilted or made at 0° is not public, so the arrows are drawn vertical. The tips' colour and the halo's hatching mark where the earlier implants are, not their profiles. The halo, the caps, the gate films, the gate oxides, the re-oxidation oxide and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Under the resist in this panel the high-voltage tip on the left is not labelled, because its leader would have to rise through the resist. Not to scale.

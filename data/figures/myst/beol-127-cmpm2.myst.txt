Before, the NILD4 oxide as deposited; after, polished flat until the topography is gone, at a height above the metal-2 tops. The polish is stopped by the removal amount, not by a stop layer, and how much it removes is not public. The oxide left over metal 2 is drawn a little thicker than the oxide over metal 1; on the page's reading, with the cap of the next step it makes up the PDK's via-2 height of 0.42 µm over metal 2.[^pdk-04] Nothing is drawn to scale, and the pattern-density steps the page describes are not drawn. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. The bottom and cap films of the metal-2 lines, the via plugs and their liners, the oxides of the via level and the metal-1 lines with their contact plugs are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

A close-up of the via-3 hole on the capacitor's top plate; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the aluminium of the metal 3 under the capacitor. Before, the hole etched to the plate; after, the liner over the whole wafer: across the oxide, down the walls of the hole and over its floor. The page describes it as ionised-metal-plasma titanium nitride; its thickness is not public (industry-typical: a few tens of nanometres on the field and much less on the via floor[^txt-05]). It is drawn with one thickness everywhere, which such a film does not have, and far thicker in proportion to the hole than it is. The liner, the plate and the metal cap are drawn in the one colour the figure conventions use for every Ti, TiN and TiW film; the plate is TiW as the page assumes, and which film caps metal 3 is not public (the overview of the metal cap sets out the readings). The hole on the metal-3 line, outside this view, is lined in the same way down to its floor on the cap. Over metal 3 the PDK's via 3 is 0.20 µm wide and 0.39 µm deep;[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04] over a plate it is shallower by the plate and the dielectric (0.1–0.2 µm on the page's reading), and this hole is drawn a little less deep than it is wide. The oxides and the aluminium–copper of metal 3 are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, are the plate, the dielectric and the metal cap. Not to scale.

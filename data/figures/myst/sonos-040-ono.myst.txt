:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step thin oxide covers both active areas except for a window over the right-hand one, where the silicon is bare. After it a thin oxide fills the window flush with the oxide around it, and two more thin films, a nitride and an oxide above it, run unbroken over the whole surface, active areas and filled trench alike.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-sonos-040-ono

Before, the bare silicon of the tunnel window; after, the three films of the stack: an oxide grown in the window only, then a nitride and a top oxide over the whole wafer, on the pad oxide (which the page infers) outside the window. No thickness of any of the three films is public for SKY130, so all three are drawn at the same minimum thickness; the fraction of a nanometre the step adds to the pad oxide is not drawn. The top oxide is drawn in the deposited-oxide colour only to tell it apart from the tunnel oxide: how it is formed is not public. The pad oxide and the fill oxide are labelled in the upper panel only. The punch-through stop and the depletion implant (the bands of PTSI and DEPI) and the liner oxide are drawn faded; the deep N-well is drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

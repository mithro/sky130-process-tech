Before the step a blanket of three metal films, a thick silver one between two thin purple ones, lies on a flat pale oxide over buried metal, and carries a raised thin purple plate at the right. After it two violet resist blocks stand on the blanket: a narrow one at the left and a wide one covering the plate and reaching past its edge.

:name: fig-beol-154-mm4

Before, the blanket metal-4 stack carrying the second capacitor's dielectric and top plate; after, the resist of the metal-4 mask: a line at the left, over a via-3 plug, and a wide block over the whole second capacitor, reaching past its top plate, where metal 4 is to remain as the capacitor's bottom plate. The PDK publishes no `cap2m` enclosure rule, so how far metal 4 must reach past the plate is not known; here it is an illustrative choice, as is the whole layout. The PDK's metal-4 lines are at least 0.300 µm wide and apart (m4.1, m4.2) and enclose a via 3 by 0.065 µm (m4.3);[^pdk-periph] neither the lines nor the resist is drawn to scale, and the page reads the PDK's 1.14 µm resist[^pdk-03] as an antenna-rule assumption. Which refractory films clad metal 4 is not public (the overview of the metal cap sets out the evidence). The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The first capacitor and the metal-3 line, the plugs and their liners, the oxides, the thin dielectric of the second capacitor and the bottom film of metal 4 are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the metal cap. Not to scale.

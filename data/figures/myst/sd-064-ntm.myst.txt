:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step a narrow capped gate stands on the right-hand active area and a wider capped stack on the oxide-filled trench, with a thin oxide over the active areas and the gate sidewalls. After it resist covers the left-hand active area, the trench and the wide stack, and leaves the right-hand active area and its gate bare.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-sd-064-ntm

Before, the gate and resistor stacks as the poly module leaves them; after, the NTM resist, with its window over the 1.8 V NMOS area on the right and resist over the 5 V NMOS area on the left, the field and the resistor. The PDK's Table F2b marks the layer "created" on the other device rows and "-" on the 1.8 V NMOS rows, and the page reads the created data as covering what is not implanted here;[^pdk-06] the window is drawn on that reading. The resist is drawn flat-topped and thick; the PDK's nominal photoresist thickness is 1.14 µm,[^pdk-03] and the thickness used for this mask is not public. The resist edge is drawn on the field oxide, well away from the gate; the 0.16 µm "NTM shadowing" allowance[^pdk-03] is not drawn to scale. The colours of the gate film mark the type of its doping, not a depth profile. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) and both gate oxides are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

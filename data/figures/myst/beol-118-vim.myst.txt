:alt: Two cross-sections of the upper films, one above the other, cut off at the bottom. Before the step three metal lines, each a thick silver film between two thin purple ones, lie buried in a flat pale oxide. After it a violet resist covers the oxide except for two narrow windows, one over the middle line and one over the right-hand line.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-beol-118-vim

Before, the cap oxide over the polished inter-metal oxide that buries the three metal-1 lines; after, the resist with a window over the middle line and one over the right-hand line, where a via is to go. The PDK gives the via as a 0.150 µm square (via.1a) that metal 1 must enclose by 0.055 µm (via.4a);[^pdk-periph] where the windows fall is an illustrative choice. The windows are not drawn to scale, and neither is the resist; the page reads the PDK's 1.14 µm resist[^pdk-03] as an antenna-rule assumption that need not describe this layer. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. The bottom and cap films of each metal-1 line, that inter-level oxide and the tungsten metal-contact plugs with their liners are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, are the metal-1 lines. Not to scale.

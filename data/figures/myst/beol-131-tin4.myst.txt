After it a thin purple film of even thickness covers the whole top: the oxide surface, the walls of the hole and its floor, leaving an opening in the middle.

:name: fig-beol-131-tin4

A close-up of via 2; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the aluminium of the metal-2 line under it. Before, the via-2 hole etched to the cap of the line; after, the liner over the whole wafer: across the oxide, down the walls of the hole and over its floor. The page describes it as ionised-metal-plasma titanium nitride; its thickness is not public (industry-typical: a few tens of nanometres over the oxide, much less on the floor of the via[^txt-05]). It is drawn with one thickness everywhere, which such a film does not have; on the floor of the hole in particular it is drawn thicker, in proportion to the hole, than it is. The liner and the cap of the line are drawn in the one colour the figure conventions use for every Ti, TiN and TiW film; which film the cap is is not public (the overview of the metal cap sets out the readings). The hole is drawn about 1.4 times as deep as it is wide; on the page it is 0.20 µm wide and 0.42 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04] about 2.1:1. The metal-2 line is drawn but not labelled in the lower panel, nor are the oxides in either panel. Not to scale.

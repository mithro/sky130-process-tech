A close-up of the 1.8 V NMOS gate edge, as for ASTI. Before, the n-type tip; after, the halo drawn under the tip and reaching a short way under the gate edge, on the channel side of the tip. The halo's shape is the industry-generic one the page describes; that it is boron is this reference's description (industry-typical),[^txt-04] and no public source gives its species, energy, dose, tilt or rotation. The page places the pocket under the gate edge with a large tilt, 20–45° being typical;[^txt-04] because the SKY130 tilt is not public, the arrows are drawn vertical. The halo is drawn as hatching over the silicon, which marks where the implant is and not its type or profile; the same resist as the tip's is assumed, as the page infers. The caps, the re-oxidation oxide and the gate oxide are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

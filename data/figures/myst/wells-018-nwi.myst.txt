Before, the N-well window; after, a beam of ions and, under the window only, the N-well, drawn from the silicon surface to a flat bottom. The PDK's process assumptions give the well a peak concentration of 6.0 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ and put its baseline vertical dimension at 1.1 µm, against a P-well vertical dimension of 0.75 µm;[^pdk-03] the N-well is drawn deeper than the P-well of step 027 in that ratio, but not to scale against anything else (the trench is drawn deeper relative to the wells than it is), and no doping profile is drawn, because the PDK gives a peak and a depth, not a shape. The well is drawn continuous under the field as under the active area, because the page infers that the implant passes through the field oxide as well as the pad oxide. The species is inferred and the energy, dose and tilt are not public; the arrows are drawn vertical. The fill oxide is drawn but not labelled; the NMOS channel implant and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

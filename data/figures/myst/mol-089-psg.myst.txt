Before the step a thin oxide covers two active areas, an oxide-filled trench between them with a wide stack on it, and a narrow capped gate on the right. Before, the slice after the source/drain anneal; after, the phosphosilicate glass over everything; its top copies the steps beneath it in broad terraces, lowest over the 5 V area on the left. The PDK's stack diagram labels this dielectric "PSG K=3.9".[^pdk-04] Its as-deposited thickness is not public: the page infers a film of the order of 0.6–0.9 µm, a typical industry figure,[^txt-05] and it is not drawn to scale. Its real profile, how it fills the gaps between lines and any undoped liner under it are not drawn. The colours mark the type of the doping, not a depth profile. The spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide, the tips and the halo, and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. In the lower panel only the new glass and the silicon are labelled. Not to scale.

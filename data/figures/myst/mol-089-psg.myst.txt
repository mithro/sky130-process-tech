Before, the slice after the source/drain anneal; after, the phosphosilicate glass over everything, standing higher where it covers the resistor and the gate. The PDK's stack diagram labels this dielectric "PSG K=3.9".[^pdk-04] Its as-deposited thickness is not public: the page infers a film of the order of 0.6–0.9 µm, a typical industry figure,[^txt-05] and it is not drawn to scale. The glass is drawn copying the steps beneath it in broad terraces; its real profile, how it fills the gaps between lines and any undoped liner under it are not drawn. The colours mark the type of the doping, not a depth profile. The spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide, the tips and the halo, and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. In the lower panel only the new glass and the silicon are labelled. Not to scale.

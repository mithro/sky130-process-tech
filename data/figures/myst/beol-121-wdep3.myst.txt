After it a thick grey cross-hatched tungsten film covers the liner everywhere and fills the hole completely, with a dip in its top surface over the hole.

A close-up of the middle via; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. Before, the lined via hole; after, the tungsten grown over the whole wafer, closing the hole from its walls inward and continuous above it. Its thickness is not public; the page gives a film of the order of 0.2–0.3 µm as the industry norm for a via of this size,[^txt-05] and the drawn film is not to scale, nor is the dip over the hole, and the liner is drawn far thicker in proportion to the hole than it is. The hole is drawn about 1.1 times as deep as it is wide; on the page it is 0.15 µm wide and 0.27 µm deep,[^pdk-periph][^pdk-04] an aspect ratio under 2:1. The right-hand via is filled in the same way, outside this view. The cap oxide, the inter-metal oxide, the metal-1 line and the inter-level oxide under it are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the liner. Not to scale.

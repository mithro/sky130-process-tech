Before, the blanket metal-5 stack, with its shallow dimples over the filled via-4 holes (the page's inference, on the fill reading of WTIAL5); after, the resist of the metal-5 mask: one block over each via, with the metal bare between them. The periphery rules give metal 5 a minimum width and space of 1.600 µm (m5.1, m5.2) and a via-4 enclosure of 0.310 µm (m5.3);[^pdk-periph] the layout is an illustrative choice, and neither the blocks nor the resist are drawn to scale. The resist is drawn thicker than at the lower metal levels, since the page describes it as thick enough to survive the etch of the 1.26 µm stack;[^pdk-04] the PDK's 1.14 µm resist[^pdk-03] is an antenna-rule assumption. Which films clad metal 5 is not public (the page reads the cap as TiW; the overview of the metal cap sets out the evidence). The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The underlayer of metal 5, the metal-4 line and the capacitors, the plugs and their liners and the oxides are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the metal-5 cap. Not to scale.

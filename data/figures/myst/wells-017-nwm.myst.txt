Before, the bare oxide surface; after, a thick resist over the NMOS side and a window over the future N-well. The PDK's mask table lists "N-Well*, NWM" as used in SKY130.[^pdk-05] The resist edge is drawn in the middle of the trench because the well edge has to fall inside the isolation between NMOS and PMOS; no layout is being shown. The resist thickness is this reference's inference, and that the three implants which follow share this one resist is its reading.[^txt-01] The NMOS channel implant and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

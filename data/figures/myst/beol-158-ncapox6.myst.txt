Before, the polished NILD6 oxide; after, a thin cap oxide over it, which with the oxide left by the polish makes up the dielectric over metal 4 on which the via-4 mask is printed. In the PDK's stack diagram that finished height is 0.505 µm;[^pdk-04] how thick the cap itself is is not public (the page gives of the order of 0.05–0.15 µm as industry-typical[^txt-05]), and that it brings the dielectric to its final thickness is the reading this reference applies to each cap oxide. The diagram draws no thin "_C" film at NILD6.[^pdk-04] Over the second capacitor's top plate the finished oxide is drawn thinner than over the metal-4 line, since the plate stands inside the via-4 dielectric; nothing is drawn to scale. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The metal-4 films, the thin capacitor dielectric, the first capacitor and the metal-3 line, the via plugs and their liners and the oxides below are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the NILD6 oxide. Not to scale.

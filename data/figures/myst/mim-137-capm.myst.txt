A close-up of part of the metal-3 level, at the left edge of a capacitor that runs on past the right-hand edge of the view; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the cap oxide under metal 3. Before, the blanket top-plate film on the dielectric; after, the resist of the capacitor mask left where the layout draws a top plate, with the film bare everywhere else. The PDK gives the capacitor mask a minimum width of 2 µm and a minimum space of 0.84 µm (CAPMCD, CAPMCDSP);[^pdk-03] where the plate edge falls is an illustrative choice, and neither the plate nor the resist is drawn to scale. The dielectric, some 20–30 nm on the estimate of the CAPILD page, and the plate film are drawn thicker in proportion than they are. The two oxides and the bottom film of the metal stack are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

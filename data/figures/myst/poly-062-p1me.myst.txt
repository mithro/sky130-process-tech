:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step two resist lines stand on three stacked films that cover the whole slice. After it the three films remain only under those lines, as a narrow gate stack on the thin oxide of the left-hand active area and a wider resistor stack on the oxide-filled trench; the resist is gone and the thin oxide is bare elsewhere.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-poly-062-p1me

Before, the P1M resist lines on the unpatterned stack; after, the oxide cap, nitride cap and gate film cut through wherever the resist was open, stopping on the gate oxide over the active areas and on the field oxide, and the resist stripped, as this reference counts the strip in this step. Whether the resist stays on through the poly etch or the caps serve as a hard mask after an early strip is not public, and the drawing shows only the result. The walls are drawn vertical, and the gate oxide is drawn intact; how much of it the over-etch removes is not public. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) is drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

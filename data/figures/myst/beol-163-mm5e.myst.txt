After it the resist is gone and the metal remains only under where it was: two thick metal shapes with vertical edges, each over a filled hole, with bare pale oxide between them.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-beol-163-mm5e

Before, the metal-5 resist; after, the chlorine etch has removed the whole metal-5 stack outside the resist, stopping on the cap oxide, and the resist has been stripped (the page treats the passivation, strip and clean as part of this step). What remains are two metal-5 shapes, each over a via-4 hole that its metal fills on the reading of WTIAL5. Unlike at MM3E and MM4E, no capacitor dielectric lies on this metal. The cap is read here as TiW, on the page's reading of the 2014 S8P qualification's exclusion of "top metal layers"[^cyp-qtp-123907] (the overview of the metal cap sets out the evidence), and is broken through first. The edges are drawn vertical, and nothing is drawn to scale: the page uses the PDK's 1.26 µm for the metal[^pdk-04] and gives the minimum width and space as 1.600 µm (m5.1, m5.2);[^pdk-periph] a minimum space is about 0.8:1 on the page's arithmetic, and this wider space is drawn about as deep as it is wide. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The underlayer of metal 5, the metal-4 line and the capacitors, the plugs and their liners and the oxides below the cap oxide are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the cap oxide. Not to scale.

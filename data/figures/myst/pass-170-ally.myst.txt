The finished slice at the final anneal, the same drawing as after the pad etch: the page reads ALLY as a low-temperature anneal with hydrogen in its ambient, and nothing the drawing can show changes. The ambient, temperature and time are not public; the page's 350–450 °C is the industry's typical range for an alloy anneal.[^txt-02] Where the hydrogen that reaches the gate oxides comes from, the furnace ambient through the openings and the passivation or the hydrogen-rich nitride itself, is not public, and nothing of it is drawn. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 5. The metal-5 films, the via-4 fill, the metal-4 line, the capacitor plates and the oxides under the passivation oxide are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

Before, the NILD5 oxide as deposited; after, polished flat until the topography is gone, at a height above the metal-3 line and the capacitor's top plate. The polish is stopped by the removal amount, not by a stop layer, and how much it removes is not public. Since the plate stands above the metal-3 top, the oxide left over it is drawn thinner than over the bare line, as the page infers from the construction; how much remains over a plate is not public (with the cap to come, about 0.2–0.3 µm on the page's estimate, of a finished 0.39 µm over metal 3[^pdk-04]). Nothing is drawn to scale, and the pattern-density steps the page describes are not drawn. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 2. The bottom and cap films of metal 3, the thin capacitor dielectric, the via plugs and their liners, the oxides below and the metal-2 lines are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

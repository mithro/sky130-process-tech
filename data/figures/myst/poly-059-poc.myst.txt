Before, the gate film under its nitride cap; after, the thin oxide cap on top, which completes the stack that the gate mask will pattern. The cap's thickness and deposition method are not public, and its thinness is the page's inference; the PDK's "poly cap after SPE" of 0.2 µm[^pdk-03] is read on the page as the whole cap stack after the spacer etch, so the two caps are not drawn in any ratio to the gate film or to each other. The colours of the gate film mark the type of its doping, not a depth profile. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) and both gate oxides are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

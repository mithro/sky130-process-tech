A close-up of the right-hand metal contact, over the local-contact plug beside the gate; the lower part of the slice is cut off. Before, the tungsten blanket; after, the polish has removed the tungsten and the liner from the field and landed on the inter-level oxide, leaving a tungsten plug flush with it. The PDK draws the plug as a 0.170 µm square (ct.1),[^pdk-periph] not drawn to scale. Whether a conventional or a high-selectivity tungsten slurry is used is not public, and the plug recess and oxide erosion the page describes are not drawn. The left-hand plug is polished in the same way, outside this view. The local interconnect, the local-contact plug and its liner, the glass and the cap oxide below are drawn but not labelled, as is the plug's liner. Not to scale.

At PWI2 a second P-well implant goes through the same block-mask opening, directly after PWI; the drawing is the state at this step, which is the same as before it. That PWI2 is a second boron dose at another energy is this reference's inference;[^txt-01] whether it is the shallower or the deeper member of the pair is not public, so the drawing does not divide the well between the two implants. The arrows are drawn vertical because the tilt is not public. The NMOS channel implant, the PMOS channel implant and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

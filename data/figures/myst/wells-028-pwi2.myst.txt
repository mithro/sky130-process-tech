Before, the P-well from PWI under the open part of the slice; after, a second beam on the same opening and the well drawn unchanged. That PWI2 is a second boron dose at another energy is this reference's inference;[^txt-01] whether it is the shallower or the deeper member of the pair is not public, so the drawing does not divide the well between the two implants. The arrows are drawn vertical because the tilt is not public. The two channel-implant bands and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

Before, the NILD6 oxide as deposited; after, polished flat until the topography is gone, at a height above the metal-4 line and the second capacitor's top plate. The polish is stopped by the removal amount, not by a stop layer, and how much it removes is not public. Since the plate stands above the metal-4 top, the oxide left over it is drawn thinner than over the bare line; how much remains over a plate is not public (with the cap to come, about 0.35–0.4 µm on the page's estimate, of a finished 0.505 µm over metal 4[^pdk-04]). Nothing is drawn to scale, and the pattern-density steps the page describes are not drawn. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The bottom and cap films of metal 4, the thin capacitor dielectric, the first capacitor and the metal-3 line, the via plugs and their liners and the oxides below are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

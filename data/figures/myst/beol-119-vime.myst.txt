:name: fig-beol-119-vime

A close-up of the middle via, over the middle metal-1 line; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. Before, the via resist; after, the hole etched through the cap oxide and the inter-metal oxide (together 0.27 µm thick in the PDK's stack diagram[^pdk-04]) to the cap film on top of the metal-1 line, and the resist stripped, which the page treats as part of this step. Which refractory film that cap is is not public: the overview of the metal cap sets out the TiW and the TiN readings, and the figure does not choose. The hole is drawn much shallower than it is: the PDK's via is a 0.150 µm square (via.1a),[^pdk-periph] about 1.8 times as deep as it is wide on the page's reckoning, and it is drawn about 1.1 times as deep as it is wide. The walls are drawn vertical, and the PDK's "Via1 slope" of 0.02[^pdk-03] is not drawn. The right-hand via is etched in the same way, outside this view. The bottom film of the line and the inter-level oxide under it are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, are the metal-1 line and the inter-metal oxide. Not to scale.

A close-up of the middle via; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. Before, the tungsten and its liner over the whole wafer; after, both polished off the field down to the cap oxide, leaving the via plug: a 0.15 µm square in the PDK's rules,[^pdk-periph] drawn here flush with the oxide. How far the polish recesses the plug and thins the oxide is not public, and neither is drawn. The oxide over the metal-1 line is 0.27 µm in the PDK's stack diagram;[^pdk-04] the plug is drawn about 1.1 times as tall as it is wide, and its liner with one thickness, thicker on the floor, in proportion, than it is. The right-hand via is polished in the same way, outside this view. The metal-1 line and the inter-level oxide under it are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, are the liner and the oxides. Not to scale.

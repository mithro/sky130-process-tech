At BFR the films that the furnace steps left on the back of the wafer are etched off, while the front, drawn here, is kept away from the chemistry. The back of the wafer is not part of any cross-section on this site, so the drawing is the same as before the step. Which films the back carries depends on whether the caps were deposited in a furnace, which is not public. The colours of the gate film mark the type of its doping, not a depth profile. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) and both gate oxides are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

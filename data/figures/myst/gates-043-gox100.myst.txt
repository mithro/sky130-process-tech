:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other, each showing two active areas of P-type silicon either side of an oxide-filled trench that stands proud of them. Before the step the silicon of both active areas is bare. After it a thick oxide film covers both active areas, and the filled trench is unchanged.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-gates-043-gox100

Before, bare silicon on both active areas; after, the thick gate oxide on both. The slice runs through logic: a 5 V NMOS area (left) and a 1.8 V NMOS area (right), both in the P-well. The silicon is drawn bare because this page has the logic silicon cleared at ONOME. The PDK gives the finished thick oxide as 110 Å;[^pdk-hv] the oxide grown here is thinner than that by an amount that is not public, and neither is drawn to scale. The memory cells and their ONO islands are not in this slice; what this oxidation does to the islands is not public. The channel implants of the wells module are not drawn, and the liner oxide is drawn faded. Not to scale.

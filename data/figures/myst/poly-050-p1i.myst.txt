:name: fig-poly-050-p1i

Before, the RPM island on the undoped gate film; after, the whole film outside the island n-type, the doping the PDK calls "N+ doped gate poly",[^pdk-07] and the film under the island, the future resistor body, left undoped, which is the page's reading of RPM. The colour fills the whole thickness of the film because it marks the type of the doping, not where it sits: the page places the dopant in the upper part of the film and has the later thermal steps spread it, and no depth is public. Species, energy, dose and tilt are not public; the arrows are drawn vertical. In the lower panel the undoped film under the island keeps the gate film's own colour and is not labelled, because its leader would have to rise through the resist. The field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) is drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the wells and channel implants made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

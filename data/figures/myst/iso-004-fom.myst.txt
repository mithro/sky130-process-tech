The wafer before and after `FOM`, the first photolithography step of the flow. The resist is left over the future active (transistor and tap) areas and opened over the future field (isolation) areas; the PDK's mask table lists "Field Oxide, FOM" as a mask used in SKY130.[^pdk-05] Where the resist edges fall in this drawing is illustrative: no layout is being shown.

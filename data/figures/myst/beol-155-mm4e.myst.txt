A close-up of the left edge of the second capacitor and the right-hand edge of a metal-4 wiring line; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 4. Before, the metal-4 resist over the line and over the capacitor; after, the etch has broken through the dielectric outside the resist (a fluorine-containing step, on the page's account, since chlorine makes little progress on it) and then removed the whole metal-4 stack in chlorine, stopping on the cap oxide, and the resist has been stripped (the page treats the passivation, strip and clean as part of this step). On this drawing the etch thus also clears the thin dielectric left on the blanket metal 4 between the two shapes; beneath the resist nothing is etched, so the dielectric stays on the metal around the top plate and on the line, which the page does not discuss. The capacitor now has a bottom plate of its own. The edges are drawn vertical, and nothing is drawn to scale: the page uses the PDK's 0.845 µm for the metal[^pdk-04] and gives the minimum width and space as 0.300 µm (m4.1, m4.2).[^pdk-periph] The two oxides and the bottom film of the metal are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the upper panel, is the aluminium–copper. Not to scale.

Before, the N-well from NWI under its window; after, a second beam on the same window and the well drawn unchanged. That NWI2 is a second phosphorus dose at another energy is this reference's inference from retrograde-well practice;[^txt-01] whether it is the shallower or the deeper member of the pair is not public, so the drawing does not divide the well between the two implants. The arrows are drawn vertical because the tilt is not public. The earlier channel-implant band and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

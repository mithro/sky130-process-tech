At NWI2 a second N-well implant goes through the same resist window, directly after NWI; the drawing is the state at this step, which is the same as before it. That NWI2 is a second phosphorus dose at another energy is this reference's inference from retrograde-well practice;[^txt-01] whether it is the shallower or the deeper member of the pair is not public, so the drawing does not divide the well between the two implants. The arrows are drawn vertical because the tilt is not public. The fill oxide is drawn but not labelled; the NMOS channel implant and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

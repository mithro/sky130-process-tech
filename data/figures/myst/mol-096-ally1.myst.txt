The slice at the alloy anneal: the contact holes open, their bottoms freshly cleaned, and no metal yet on the wafer. Nothing is deposited or removed, and nothing the drawing shows moves. The page reads the anneal as a hydrogen anneal that passivates interface damage and drives water out of the glass and the cap oxide before the liner seals them, both inferred; SkyWater lists "H2 and forming gas alloy" among its furnace processes[^skw-01] but does not say where it is used. The ambient, temperature and time are not public. The colours mark the type of the doping, not a depth profile. The transistors' films (the spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide and the spacer oxide), the tips and the halo, and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

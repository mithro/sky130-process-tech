The wafer before and after `DNM`, the second mask step of the flow. At this point the wafer has open, unfilled isolation trenches and a nitride/pad-oxide stack over the active areas; the thick resist is opened over the regions that are to receive the high-energy phosphorus implant at `DNI`. The PDK's mask table lists "Deep N-Well, DNM" as used in SKY130.[^pdk-05] Where the mask edge falls in this drawing is chosen only so that both sides of it can be seen. Not to scale.

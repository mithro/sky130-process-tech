Before, the block resist over the N-well; after, a beam on the open part of the slice and the P-well under it, drawn from the silicon surface to a flat bottom. The PDK's process assumptions give a P-well peak of 4 × 10¹⁷ cm⁻³ at 0.42 µm and a vertical dimension of 0.75 µm;[^pdk-03] the well is drawn shallower than the N-well in the ratio of the PDK's two depths, but not to scale against anything else, and no doping profile is drawn. Boron is inferred; the energy, dose and tilt are not public, and the arrows are drawn vertical. The two channel-implant bands and the liner oxide are drawn faded. Not to scale.

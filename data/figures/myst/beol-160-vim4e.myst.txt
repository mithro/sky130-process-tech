:alt: Two cross-sections of the upper films, one above the other, cut off at the bottom. Before the step a violet resist with two wide windows lies on a flat pale oxide that buries a metal line and a wider metal shape carrying a raised thin plate. After it the resist is gone and under each window a hole with vertical walls, wider than it is deep, goes down through the oxide: at the left to the top of the line, at the right, less deep, to the plate.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-beol-160-vim4e

Before, the via-4 resist; after, the holes etched through the cap oxide and the NILD6 oxide, 0.505 µm together over metal 4 in the PDK's stack diagram,[^pdk-04] and the resist stripped, which the page treats as part of this step. The two holes stop on two kinds of floor: at the left on the cap film of a metal-4 line, and at the right on the second capacitor's top plate, which stands above the metal-4 cap on the capacitor dielectric. Which refractory film caps metal 4 is not public: the overview of the metal cap sets out the TiW and the TiN readings, and the figure does not choose. On this drawing the etch over the metal-4 line also clears the thin capacitor dielectric left on the line at MM4E, down to the cap; the page gives the floor as the cap and does not discuss that film. The PDK's via 4 is a 0.800 µm square (via4.1),[^pdk-periph] about 0.63 times as deep as it is wide on the page's arithmetic; the left hole is drawn about 0.8 times as deep as it is wide, and the walls vertical, although whether via 4 is tapered is not public. No liner, tungsten fill or plug polish follows in the step list: the next step sputters the metal-5 stack into the open holes. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 3. The oxides, the aluminium and bottom film of metal 4, the thin capacitor dielectric, the first capacitor and the metal-3 line and the via plugs and their liners are drawn but not labelled. Not to scale.

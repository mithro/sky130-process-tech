:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step photoresist and a thin anti-reflective film cover the surface except for a window over the right-hand active area, whose bottom is thin oxide over two hatched bands. After it the resist and the film are gone, the thin oxide is gone from the window so that bare silicon shows there, and the oxide remains everywhere else.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-sonos-039-tunme

Before, the tunnel-mask resist over its window; after, the oxide gone from the window down to the silicon, and the resist and the anti-reflective coating gone from the whole wafer. The etch is drawn only inside the window, as this page describes a masked etch; whether the oxide is also removed elsewhere after the strip is an open question. The oxide is drawn as the pad oxide, which the page infers. The resist is drawn stripped, with the coating under it, because this page treats the strip as the tail of this step; how the coating comes off is not stated. The two implanted bands of PTSI and DEPI are unchanged. The deep N-well is drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded. Not to scale.

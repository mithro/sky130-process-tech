A close-up of the middle via; the lower part of the slice is cut off, and the drawing starts inside the inter-level oxide under metal 1. Before, the flush via plug; after, the metal-2 stack over the whole wafer: a thin bottom film, the aluminium–copper alloy and a thin cap. Which refractory films the bottom and the cap are is not public for SKY130. A 2013 Cypress report for the fab's S8TNV-5R technology gives "Metal 2: 100A Ti / 3200A Al -0.5%Cu / 300A TiW",[^cyp-qtp-113005] and a 2014 report gives the new metal 2 of its S8DI technology as "150A Ti/250A TiN/3200A Al 0.5% Cu/90A Ti/500A TiN";[^cyp-qtp-123907] the overview of the metal cap sets out both, and this figure does not choose: the bottom and the cap are drawn in the colour the figure conventions use for every Ti, TiN and TiW film, and labelled with both readings. The page follows the Ti/Al–Cu/TiW stack, as an inference from its 3 600 Å sum and the PDK's 0.36 µm for metal 2;[^pdk-04] the figure, like the overview, draws neither. The stack is drawn as thick as the metal-1 stack under it, since the PDK gives both 0.36 µm, but nothing is drawn to scale. The plug's liner, the metal-1 line and the oxides are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the plug. Not to scale.

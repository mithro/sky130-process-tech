A close-up of the right-hand metal contact, over the local-contact plug beside the gate; the lower part of the slice is cut off. Before, the lined hole; after, tungsten grown from every surface has closed it and covers the field. The deposited thickness is not public (the page gives a few hundred nanometres as the industry norm for a hole of this size[^txt-05]), and it is not drawn to scale. The hole, too, is drawn much shallower than it is: on the page's reading it is about 0.34 µm deep,[^pdk-04] about twice its width. The nucleation layer and any seam where the growth from the walls meets are not drawn. The left-hand hole is filled in the same way, outside this view. The local interconnect, the local-contact plug and its liner, the glass and the cap oxide below are drawn but not labelled, as is the new liner in the lower panel. Not to scale.

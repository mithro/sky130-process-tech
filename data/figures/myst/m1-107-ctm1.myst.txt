:alt: Two cross-sections of the wafer, one above the other. Before the step a flat pale oxide buries three thin purple pads, each capped by a thin hatched film and standing on a tungsten plug. After it a violet resist covers the oxide except for two narrow windows, one over the left pad and one over the right pad.
:width: 560px
:name: fig-m1-107-ctm1

Before, the polished inter-level oxide over the nitride-capped local interconnect; after, the resist with a window over each of the two outer pads, where a metal contact is to go. The PDK gives the contact as a 0.170 µm square (ct.1) with no enclosure required by the local interconnect (ct.4);[^pdk-periph] where the windows fall is an illustrative choice, drawn over the local-contact plugs below, and the middle pad has none in this slice. The windows are not drawn to scale, and neither is the resist, whose thickness for this layer is not public. The local interconnect (the thin purple pads, titanium nitride), the glass, the cap oxide, the tungsten plugs, the silicide discs, the contact liner and the transistors' films (the spacers, the caps, the gate oxides, the gate film, the re-oxidation oxide and the spacer oxide), the doped regions and the field oxide (the oxide-filled trench in the middle) are drawn but not labelled, and the liner oxide is drawn faded; the P-well and the NCHI channel implant made earlier are not drawn. Not to scale.

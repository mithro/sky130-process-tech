Before, the metal-5 shapes under the passivation oxide and nitride; after, the resist of the pad mask, with one opening. The left-hand metal-5 shape stands here for a pad (an illustrative choice: the slice is not drawn to any pad size). The opening lies inside the metal, which extends beyond it on both sides; in SkyWater's GPIO cell the opening is 60 µm × 70 µm and the metal-5 pad extends 2.7 µm beyond it on every side,[^pdk-io-gpiov2] and the smallest allowed opening is 2 µm (`PDMCD`),[^pdk-03] none of which is drawn to scale. The resist's thickness is not public; it is drawn covering the metal-5 steps. The lower part of the slice is cut off: the drawing starts inside the oxide under metal 5. The metal-5 films, the via-4 fill, the metal-4 line, the capacitor plates and the oxides under the passivation oxide are drawn but not labelled, nor, in the lower panel, is the passivation oxide. Not to scale.
